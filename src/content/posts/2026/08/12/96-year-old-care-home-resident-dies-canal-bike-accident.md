# 96-Year-Old Care Home Resident Dies in Canal Bike Accident

A 96-year-old resident of a care home in Brecht, Belgium, drowned on Tuesday afternoon after a caregiver lost control of a specially equipped wheelchair bicycle and both ended up in the Dessel-Schoten canal. The tragic incident occurred on the towpath between Brecht and Sint-Job-in-'t-Goor, according to [Het Laatste Nieuws](https://www.hln.be/brecht/bewoonster-96-rusthuis-verdrinkt-nadat-begeleider-controle-over-rolstoelfiets-verliest-en-in-kanaal-dessel-schoten-belandt~aa69c10d/).

## The Accident

Emergency services received a call on Tuesday afternoon. The woman was a passenger in a wheelchair mounted on a specially equipped tricycle — a type of bicycle with a platform at the front designed to carry a wheelchair. A staff member from Woonzorgcentrum (WZC) Sint-Maria was at the handlebars when control was lost, causing both to fall into the canal.

The caregiver managed to reach safety and was physically unharmed, though taken to hospital in shock. The 96-year-old woman remained in the water longer and was recovered by fire department divers, but all help came too late. She was pronounced dead at the scene, as [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/08/11/rusthuisbewoonster-96-overleden-na-val-in-kanaal-in-brecht/) reported.

Police initially received reports of two young people who had fallen into the water with their bicycle, but this was quickly corrected. The incident was confirmed by the local police zone Voorkempen, which covers Brecht, Malle, Schilde, and Zoersel.

## A Tragic Accident

According to initial police findings, this was a tragic accident with no indications of foul play. A witness confirmed to police that there was no malicious intent or involvement of third parties, as [La Libre Belgique](https://www.lalibre.be/dernieres-depeches/2026/08/11/une-nonagenaire-perd-la-vie-apres-une-chute-dans-le-canal-a-brecht-2YSUOWMSBVGXZJMKXIWMYVF6WU/) reported. The exact cause of the loss of control has not been publicly disclosed.

The caregiver is currently being supported by victim support services (slachtofferhulp).

Mayor Frans Van Looveren of Brecht issued a statement on behalf of the municipal government: "Our thoughts go out to the deceased, her family, and all those affected by this tragic accident. On behalf of the Brecht municipal government, we express our sincere condolences to the family, friends, fellow residents, and staff of WZC Sint-Maria."

## The Wheelchair Bike Program

The wheelchair bike used in the accident was part of a project at WZC Sint-Maria, a care home located at Welzijnscampus, Gasthuisstraat 11 in Brecht. The facility consists of three levels with 108 residential units, divided into four departments catering to residents with varying degrees of physical care needs and a dedicated unit for residents with dementia, according to the [Gemeente Brecht website](https://www.brecht.be/sociale-hulp-welzijn-gezondheid/ouderenzorg/woonzorgcentrum-sint-maria).

The rolstoelfiets was funded in 2022 by the charity initiative 'Groot Brecht met een hart' (Greater Brecht with a Heart), which raised money for Brecht residents with physical and mental disabilities, those living in poverty, and migrants or refugees. The care home planned to expand the project as a lending service for other care facilities, neighbors, and facilities for people with disabilities, as detailed on the [WZC Sint-Maria website](https://www.sintmariabrecht.be/gebeurtenissen/863897_rolstoelfiets).

## The Dessel-Schoten Canal

The accident occurred on the towpath (jaagpad) along the Dessel-Schoten canal, a waterway that connects the Bocholt-Herentals canal at Dessel with the Albertkanaal at Schoten. The canal, approximately 38 kilometers long with 10 locks, was built in phases between 1845 and 1874. Today, it primarily serves a recreational function, with a paved towpath on the right bank that is popular for car-free cycling and walking, according to [Visit Brecht](https://visit.brecht.be/zien/natuur/kanaal-dessel-schoten).

## Safety Questions and Community Impact

The incident raises serious questions about safety protocols for elderly residents during outdoor activities at care homes. The towpath along the canal runs directly alongside the water with no protective barriers in some sections, and the specialized wheelchair bike requires skill to maneuver, particularly with a passenger.

The incident has deeply affected the community of Brecht. The mayor's formal statement of condolences, the support being provided to the caregiver, and the widespread coverage in Belgian media underscore the emotional impact of the tragedy.

## What's Next

As the community mourns, attention will likely turn to reviewing safety measures for outdoor activities involving elderly care home residents. The exact circumstances that led to the loss of control remain under investigation, and questions about whether additional safety precautions — such as barriers along the towpath or enhanced training for caregivers operating specialized equipment — may be needed are likely to emerge in the coming weeks.

The tragic loss of a 96-year-old resident during what was meant to be an enjoyable outing serves as a sobering reminder of the vulnerabilities faced by elderly residents in care settings and the importance of rigorous safety protocols in all aspects of their care.
