# China's 100-Day Recruitment Drive Offers 80,000+ Jobs Across Key Industries

China's 100-Day Recruitment Campaign has generated more than 80,000 job opportunities in a single week, according to the [Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security](https://www.news.cn/politics/20260812/ed365eb17ddc48188dbcbeac1a064506/c.html). From August 10-16, the initiative launched four online recruitment special sessions covering electric power new energy, cross-border e-commerce, transportation, and internet industry sectors, with over 7,700 employers participating.

## Campaign Context

The 100-Day Recruitment Campaign is an annual national initiative organized by China's Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security. The 2026 edition, launched around July 2, is the seventh iteration of the program, which aims to provide millions of job opportunities during a roughly 100-day period from July to October. Operating under the theme "Guide the Future, Build Dreams of Youth," the campaign focuses on connecting job seekers—particularly college graduates—with employers across the country.

The initiative is part of a broader employment policy framework. In March 2026, the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security and the Ministry of Finance jointly issued a notice with 16 policy measures to promote employment for 2026 college graduates and youth, as [reported by China News](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/gn/2026/03-20/10590090.shtml). The measures include encouraging private enterprises to absorb more graduates, expanding public institution positions, supporting grassroots employment, and launching the fifth round of the Three Supports and One Assistance program.

## Sector Breakdown and Scale

The August 10-16 sessions showcased significant recruitment demand across four key industries:

- **Electric Power New Energy:** 243 employers, including Shen'ai Semiconductor, Yonglian Technology, and Wanbao Energy, offered over 15,000 positions for battery R&D engineers, technical support engineers, and materials engineers.
- **Cross-border E-commerce:** 3,000 employers offered 30,000 positions for foreign trade salespersons, cross-border e-commerce operations specialists, sales representatives, and business supervisors.
- **Transportation:** 162 employers, including Beijing Public Transport Group and COSCO Shipping, provided more than 3,000 positions for customs supervisors, hardware engineers, logistics safety officers, and automation system designers.
- **Internet Industry:** 4,346 employers, including tech giants Tencent and Baidu, offered 32,000 positions for AI strategy product managers, multimodal large model algorithm experts, and embedded development engineers.

The emphasis on AI-related and new energy positions reflects China's strategic push toward high-tech and digital economy sectors as drivers of future job growth.

## Live Stream and Regional Engagement

On August 13 at 7 PM, the Employment Online platform will host a Human Resources (Employment) Bureau Director Live Stream event featuring employment officials from Shanxi, Shandong, and Henan provinces. The event will include employers from manufacturing, transportation, warehousing and postal services, information transmission, software and IT services, and education sectors, offering positions ranging from algorithm engineers and intelligent engineers to laboratory technicians and teachers.

This digital-first approach represents a modernization of China's employment services. According to [MacroChina's coverage](http://www.macrochina.com.cn/news_speed/hgjj/20260702125145.shtml) of the campaign launch, the initiative includes online platforms such as the China Public Recruitment Network, China National Talent Network, and Employment Online, alongside live streaming events, virtual enterprise visits, and AI-powered recruitment tools.

## Local Implementation Across Regions

Beyond the national online sessions, local governments have implemented the campaign with innovative approaches. In Guangzhou, a July 22 recruitment event featured 50 quality companies offering over 1,000 positions, with AI-powered services including AI interview booths, AI resume diagnostics, and smart job matching, as [detailed by Guangzhou Daily](https://news.dayoo.com/guangzhou/202607/22/139995_54982648.htm). The event highlighted the campaign's human impact, with employment officials personally assisting job seekers with special needs.

Other regions have also embraced creative formats. Nanchang's Qingyunpu District held a cultural tourism-themed talent market at the Bada Shanren Meihu Scenic Area, integrating recruitment with cultural experiences, as [reported by Shandong News](https://f.sdnews.com.cn/syxx/202607/t20260715_4707669.htm). Meanwhile, [China Youth Net](https://m.youth.cn/qwtx/xxl/202608/t20260807_16803927.htm) documented a range of regional efforts, including Dongying's dual-track online-offline recruitment with live streaming, night market recruitment events in Xinyu and Luoyang, and VR-based virtual enterprise exploration in Inner Mongolia.

## Analysis and Implications

The scale and breadth of the August 10-16 sessions demonstrate China's ongoing commitment to addressing employment challenges through large-scale, organized recruitment initiatives. The focus on emerging industries—particularly AI, new energy, and cross-border e-commerce—signals where the government expects future job growth and reflects the alignment of job creation with industrial transformation.

The cross-border e-commerce sector's substantial demand (30,000 positions) highlights the continued expansion of China's digital trade, while the internet industry's 32,000 positions underscore the sustained importance of the digital economy. The inclusion of provincial employment officials in live stream events demonstrates the campaign's nationwide reach and the coordination between central and provincial governments.

## What to Watch For

As the campaign continues through October, observers will be watching whether the momentum translates into sustained employment gains, particularly for the large cohort of 2026 college graduates. The campaign's success in leveraging digital tools, regional coordination, and industry-specific targeting may serve as a model for future employment initiatives in China. The live stream event on August 13, featuring employment officials from three provinces, will be an early indicator of the campaign's continued effectiveness in connecting job seekers with opportunities across provincial boundaries.
