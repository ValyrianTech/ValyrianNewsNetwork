# China Unveils New Ecological Protection Plan for 2026-2030

China has announced a comprehensive new ecological protection plan for the 15th Five-Year Plan period (2026-2030), outlining ambitious targets for biodiversity conservation, ecosystem restoration, and green development. Six government ministries jointly issued the "Ecological Protection 15th Five-Year Plan" on August 6, according to [Xinhua News](https://www.news.cn/politics/20260812/60db1463024346aca4bda845923aaedc/c.html).

## A Key Component of the Beautiful China Agenda

The ecological protection plan is a central pillar of the broader "Beautiful China 15th Five-Year Plan," issued by the State Council in early July 2026. Together, these documents form what officials describe as a "1+1+N" implementation system for ecological civilization construction, with the ecological protection plan serving as one of the key special plans.

The plan was jointly issued by the Ministry of Ecology and Environment, the Ministry of Natural Resources, the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, the Ministry of Water Resources, the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs, and the National Forestry and Grassland Administration, with the Ministry of Finance also countersigning, as confirmed by the [Chinese government's official notice](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202608/content_7077908.htm).

## Key Targets and Measures

The plan sets a target for natural protected areas to cover **no less than 18% of China's land territory**, establishing a national park-based protected area system. It also emphasizes consolidating the "Three Zones and Four Belts" national ecological security barrier framework, implementing ecological protection red line management, and promoting major biodiversity conservation projects.

According to the [Ministry of Ecology and Environment's official announcement](http://www.mee.gov.cn/xxgk2018/xxgk/xxgk03/202608/t20260810_1163837.html), the plan builds on the broader Beautiful China framework, which sets 18 major indicators across four categories: ecological environment quality improvement, pollution reduction and carbon reduction synergy, ecosystem protection, and ecological environment risk prevention. Of these, six are binding (constraint) indicators—including PM2.5 concentration, water quality, carbon emission reduction per unit of GDP, non-fossil energy share, pollutant emission reduction, and forest coverage—while 12 are expected (anticipatory) indicators.

The plan proposes 20 major projects with strong support and leading roles, along with seven key tasks encompassing 28 major measures.

## A Strategic Shift in Environmental Governance

The "Beautiful China 15th Five-Year Plan" represents the **11th five-year plan** in China's ecological environment field and the **first named after "Beautiful China"**, marking a transition from pollution prevention campaigns to comprehensive ecological civilization construction.

In a signed article published in Liaowang magazine, Ministry of Ecology and Environment Party Secretary Sun Jinlong and Minister Huang Runqiu described the period as "a critical stage for Beautiful China construction to build on past achievements, expand and improve quality, and achieve a fundamental turnaround in the ecological environment," as reported by the [Ministry of Ecology and Environment](http://www.mee.gov.cn/ywdt/hjywnews/202608/t20260801_1163306.shtml).

## Expert Perspectives on the Plan's Design

Environmental experts have praised the plan's indicator framework. Ma Jun, director of the Public Environment Research Center, noted that dividing indicators into "binding" and "expected" categories "reflects the organic combination of an 'effective government' and an 'efficient market.'" He explained that binding indicators represent "the government's solemn commitments and development bottom lines that must be completed through strict administrative measures and performance assessment," while expected indicators "play more of a directional guiding role, relying on market mechanisms and social forces to achieve them," according to [coverage of the plan](https://news.10jqka.com.cn/20260704/c677947273.shtml).

Ma Jun also cautioned against ecological projects becoming "face projects," emphasizing the need for science-based approaches and performance evaluation based on actual ecological benefits rather than investment scale or surface-level greening.

## Building on Progress, Facing Persistent Challenges

The plan arrives amid significant environmental gains. According to the ministry's signed article, PM2.5 concentration fell to 28 micrograms per cubic meter in 2025 (down from 35 in 2020), surface water quality improved by 8 percentage points since 2020, and public satisfaction with the ecological environment has exceeded 90% for five consecutive years.

However, officials acknowledge that "structural, fundamental, and trend-based pressures on ecological environment protection have not yet been fundamentally alleviated," and the "inflection point from quantitative to qualitative improvement has not yet arrived." The plan's emphasis on unified supervision of ecological protection and restoration, along with effectiveness assessments, reflects these ongoing challenges.

## What to Watch For

As China implements the Ecological Protection 15th Five-Year Plan, key indicators to monitor include the expansion of national parks, progress on the "Three Zones and Four Belts" framework, and the effectiveness of ecological restoration projects. The plan also aligns with China's international commitments under the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework and its carbon peak and neutrality goals, positioning ecological protection as a cornerstone of the country's sustainable development strategy through 2030 and beyond.
