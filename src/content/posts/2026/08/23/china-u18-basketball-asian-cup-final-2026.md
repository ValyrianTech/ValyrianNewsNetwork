# China U18 Basketball Reaches Asian Cup Final After 12 Years

China's U18 men's basketball team has advanced to the final of the 2026 FIBA U18 Asia Cup for the first time in 12 years, defeating New Zealand 79-66 in the semifinal on August 22 in Ahmedabad, India. The victory marks a significant milestone for Chinese youth basketball and represents a resurgence of the national program at the junior level.

## A Long-Awaited Return

China last reached the U18 Asian Cup final in 2014 in Doha, Qatar, where the team defeated Iran 66-48 to claim its 11th championship. In the three editions since — 2018, 2022, and 2024 — China finished third each time, earning bronze medals but falling short of the championship game.

According to [Xinhua News](https://www.news.cn/sports/20260822/bc93fbc9417e4699b5833015a84b998b/c.html), the team's journey through this year's tournament has been dominant. China went undefeated in the group stage with three wins, then defeated the Philippines 117-103 in the quarterfinal on August 21 before beating New Zealand in the semifinal.

## Semifinal Dominance

China controlled the semifinal from the opening tip. The team jumped out to a 9-2 run and led by double digits after the first quarter, holding New Zealand to just 10 points in the opening period. Zhang Chunzhi scored 13 consecutive points during a stretch in the second quarter to push China's lead to 20-plus points.

Zhang Chunzhi led all Chinese scorers with 18 points on four three-pointers, adding 5 rebounds and 2 assists. Zhang Yizhaojie recorded a double-double with 12 points and 10 rebounds, while Wang Zichao contributed 11 points, 5 rebounds, and 9 assists. Yang Yixuan added 10 points, including key drives in the fourth quarter that helped China hold off New Zealand's comeback attempt.

As [Xinhua News](https://english.news.cn/20260821/d0b37021e0094884aa6cf445c7bce664/c.html) reported, China's three-point shooting has been a key weapon throughout the tournament. In the quarterfinal against the Philippines, China made 17 of 33 attempts from beyond the arc, with Wang Zichao leading the way with 25 points on six three-pointers.

## World Cup Qualification Secured

By reaching the semifinals, China secured a spot in the 2027 FIBA U19 Basketball World Cup, to be held in Pardubice, Czech Republic from June 26 to July 4, 2027. The top four teams from the U18 Asia Cup qualify for the global tournament.

The [2026 FIBA U18 Asia Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FIBA_U18_Asia_Cup) is the 28th edition of the tournament, held at the Veer Savarkar Indoor Stadium in Ahmedabad, India, from August 13-23. Sixteen teams from FIBA Asia and FIBA Oceania are competing in this year's event.

## The Final Challenge: Australia

China will face Australia in the final on August 23 at 22:00 Beijing time. Australia, the defending champion, defeated South Korea 111-74 in the other semifinal, dominating with six players scoring in double figures and outrebounding South Korea 61-17.

Australia won the [2024 edition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_FIBA_U18_Asia_Cup) of the tournament, defeating New Zealand 96-33 in the final, and has been dominant throughout this year's competition as well. The team has now won two U18 Asian titles since joining the Asian competition.

Chinese sports commentators have noted the controversial nature of Australia's participation in the Asian championship, given that Australia is a world-class basketball nation competing at a regional level. However, as [Tencent News](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260823A03SSM00) commentary points out, facing Australia provides valuable experience for the young Chinese players.

## Analysis: A Resurgent Youth Program

China has historically been one of the strongest youth basketball nations in Asia, winning the U18 Asian Championship 11 times — the most of any nation. The current squad features several players who also participated in the U17 World Cup, including Zhang Yizhaojie and Zhao Boxin.

The team's depth has been praised throughout the tournament, with multiple players able to step up in different games. This balanced scoring attack, combined with strong three-point shooting (43% tournament-best) and a formidable interior presence, gives China a legitimate chance against Australia despite the defending champions' dominance.

Key tactical advantages for China in the final include forcing turnovers — Australia committed 23 in their semifinal — protecting the paint, and maintaining defensive intensity. As [Beijing Daily](https://www.sohu.com/a/1066308075_163278) reported, the team's ability to withstand pressure and respond in key moments has been a hallmark of this tournament run.

## What's Next

The final against Australia represents more than just a chance at a 12th Asian title — it is an opportunity for China's next generation of basketball talent to test itself against one of the world's best youth programs. The [2022 FIBA U18 Asian Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_FIBA_U18_Asian_Championship) saw South Korea take the title, with China finishing third, while Australia claimed the crown in 2024.

Regardless of the outcome, this tournament has already demonstrated that Chinese youth basketball is on an upward trajectory. With a U19 World Cup berth secured and a generation of promising young players gaining invaluable international experience, the future looks bright for China's basketball program.
