# Apartment Fire in Brecht: Residents Evacuate Safely

A serious fire broke out in an apartment building in Brecht, Belgium, on Sunday morning, forcing all residents to evacuate the premises. Emergency services responded to the scene on Beukenlei in Sint-Job-in-'t-Goor, a sub-municipality of Brecht in Antwerp province, after a smoke plume was spotted by bystanders. According to [Het Laatste Nieuws](https://www.hln.be/brecht/flatbewoners-haasten-zich-uit-brandend-flatgebouw-in-brecht-vlammen-beschadigen-appartement-op-tweede-verdieping~a450bfb9/), the fire originated in an apartment on the second floor and spread to another unit on the same floor.

The smoke plume was visible from a great distance, prompting the fire department to rush to the scene for what was described as a fully developed fire. Fire crews from Brandweer Zone Rand, the fire assistance zone responsible for the area, responded with multiple units.

## All Residents Accounted For

Despite the severity of the blaze, all residents present in the affected building managed to evacuate safely. Ben Van Gils, spokesperson for Politiezone Voorkempen, confirmed that no injuries were sustained. "Everyone was able to leave the building in time and no one sustained injuries," Van Gils told [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/08/02/uitslaande-brand-vernielt-appartementsgebouw-in-sint-job-in-t-g/).

## Extensive Damage to Building

The fire caused significant damage to the building, with authorities reporting that the structure suffered extensive destruction. "Both the ground floor and the 1st and 2nd floors are completely lost," police zone Voorkempen stated. The flames also caused substantial damage to the roof of the building, which is located at the corner of Eikenlei and Beukenlei.

Additional reports from [NewsLocker](https://www.newslocker.com/nl-be/regio/kaprijke/zware-uitslaande-brand-treft-huis-in-brecht-vlammen-overgeslagen-op-dak-aanpalende-woning/) indicate that the fire also spread via the roof to an adjacent property, though the full extent of damage to neighboring structures has not been confirmed.

## Investigation Underway

The fire department has fully extinguished the blaze, and emergency services remained on site to complete their operations. The cause of the fire remains under investigation. "The public prosecutor's office was informed and the investigation into the cause has been initiated," Van Gils said.

## Context and Background

The incident occurred during a period of intense heat in Belgium, with temperatures forecast to reach around 35°C on Monday. The Brecht area has experienced previous fire incidents, including a roof fire in a farmhouse in March 2021 and a roof fire that destroyed a home in Sint-Job-in-'t-Goor in December 2022. The [De Krantenkoppen](https://www.dekrantenkoppen.be/detail/3716218/hevige-brand-beschadigt-appartement-in-brecht-alle-aanwezigen-raken-tijdig-uit-gebouw.html) news aggregator also reported on the incident, highlighting the severity of the blaze.

## What to Watch For

As the investigation into the cause of the fire continues, authorities will be examining the building's structural integrity and assessing the full extent of the damage. Residents affected by the fire will need to find alternative accommodation while the building undergoes assessment and potential repairs. The outcome of the investigation may also provide important lessons for fire safety in similar residential buildings across the region.
