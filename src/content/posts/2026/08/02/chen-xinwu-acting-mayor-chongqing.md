# Chen Xinwu Appointed Acting Mayor of Chongqing

Chen Xinwu has been appointed as Vice Mayor and Acting Mayor of Chongqing, one of China's four direct-administered municipalities, according to [Xinhua News](https://www.news.cn/politics/20260731/3fe0b15a495a429a8f425d75bd03882e/c.html). The appointment, made by the 25th session of the Standing Committee of the 6th Chongqing Municipal People's Congress on July 31, fills the vacancy left by former Mayor Hu Henghua, who was placed under investigation for serious disciplinary violations in March.

## Background: A Leadership Vacancy

Hu Henghua, who had served as Chongqing's mayor since December 2021, was [investigated](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_32800411) by the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection (CCDI) on March 20, 2026, for suspected serious violations of party discipline. He formally [resigned](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_33252655) as mayor on May 27, 2026, approved by the 23rd session of the Standing Committee.

Hu's downfall marked the sixth senior official from Chongqing to fall since the 20th Party Congress, part of a broader anti-corruption campaign that has targeted multiple officials in the municipality. Reports from [Lianhe Zaobao](https://www.zaobao.com.sg/news/china/story20260324-8784845) suggest the case may extend beyond Chongqing, potentially involving Lin Xiucheng, founder of San'an Group and former Fujian richest man known as the "China LED King."

## Chen Xinwu's Career Trajectory

Chen Xinwu, born July 1968 in Huanggang, Hubei Province, brings extensive administrative experience to the role. A graduate of Nankai University with an MBA from Ohio University, he began his career in his home province of Hubei, serving in various capacities including Mayor of Shiyan City, Director of the Hubei Provincial Development and Reform Commission, and Secretary-General of the Hubei Provincial Party Committee.

According to [The Paper](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_33691535), Chen transferred to Chongqing in July 2023 as a Party Standing Committee member and Secretary-General. He was appointed Executive Deputy Mayor in May 2024 and promoted to Deputy Secretary of the Chongqing Municipal Party Committee in October 2025. His appointment as Acting Mayor elevates him to the full provincial-ministerial rank.

His current roles include Deputy Secretary of the Chongqing Municipal Party Committee, Vice Mayor, Acting Mayor, and Party Group Secretary of the Municipal Government, as confirmed by the [Chongqing Municipal Government](https://www.cq.gov.cn/zwgk/szfld/sz/202607/t20260731_15878282.html) official profile.

## Broader Leadership Restructuring

The 25th session of the Standing Committee also approved other significant personnel changes, indicating a comprehensive restructuring of Chongqing's government leadership. [Tan Quan](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_33692563) was appointed Vice Mayor and Public Security Bureau Director, having previously served as Director of the Ministry of Public Security's Cybersecurity Bureau. [Wu Shulin](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_33693107), a former National Development and Reform Commission official, was also appointed Vice Mayor, concurrently serving as Party Secretary of the Western Science City Chongqing High-Tech Zone.

## Analysis: Stability Amid Turmoil

Chen's appointment follows a natural career progression within Chongqing, suggesting the central government values stability and continuity in the municipality's leadership during a turbulent period. His extensive background in economic development, including as head of the Hubei Provincial Development and Reform Commission, may be relevant to Chongqing's economic priorities.

His transfer from Hubei to Chongqing in 2023 reflects the central government's practice of rotating senior officials across provinces to prevent local factionalism. As acting mayor, Chen will need to be formally confirmed by the 6th Chongqing Municipal People's Congress 5th session, which has been scheduled.

The appointment comes amid ongoing scrutiny of Chongqing's political landscape, which has been described by Chinese media as a "high-risk position" due to the frequency of corruption investigations in the municipality. Notable predecessors include Bo Xilai and Sun Zhengcai, both former Chongqing Party Secretaries sentenced to life imprisonment.

## What's Next

Observers will be watching whether Chen's appointment signals a new direction for Chongqing's economic and governance policies, and how the ongoing anti-corruption campaign will continue to affect the stability of the municipal government. The formal confirmation process at the upcoming People's Congress session will be a key milestone.
