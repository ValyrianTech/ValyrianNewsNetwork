# China Volleyball Falls to Thailand, Misses Olympic Direct Qualifier

The Chinese women's volleyball team fell 2-3 to Thailand in the final of the 2026 Asian Women's Volleyball Championship in Tianjin on August 30, securing silver but missing out on direct qualification for the 2028 Los Angeles Olympics. The defeat, witnessed by 9,390 spectators at the Tianjin Olympic Center Gymnasium, marked the second consecutive time Thailand has beaten China in an Asian Championship final.

## A Five-Set Thriller

Thailand took the opening set 25-20 before China responded with dominant performances to win the next two sets 25-11 and 25-23, taking a 2-1 lead. But Thailand forced a decider with a 25-18 fourth-set victory and held their nerve in the fifth, closing the match 15-10 after two aggressive jump serves forced consecutive Chinese reception errors, according to [Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20260830/513cc49c8dca4488bca1825d030a8ffa/c.html).

The match lasted 2 hours and 5 minutes. Thailand outscored China 68-59 in attack and posted a massive dig advantage of 107-85. Sasipaporn Janthawisut led all scorers with 28 points for Thailand, while Wu Mengjie and Zhuang Yushan each scored 21 points for China.

## Historic First for Thailand

The victory gave Thailand its fourth Asian Championship title — previously won in 2009, 2013, and 2023 — and secured the country's first-ever Olympic berth in women's volleyball. As [FIVB](https://www.fivb.com/thailand-retain-avc-continental-title-to-secure-first-ever-olympic-berth/) reported, the Asian Championship winner earned the continent's direct qualification place for the LA28 Olympic Games. Thailand became the second team to qualify through continental competition, after Canada beat host Dominican Republic 3-1 in the NORCECA final a day earlier.

"For decades, Thai women's volleyball has been a source of national pride, consistent Asian medalists, beloved at home, respected internationally. The Olympics were the missing piece," [The Pattaya News](https://thepattayanews.com/2026/08/31/thailands-womens-volleyball-team-beats-china-and-goes-to-2028-olympics/) noted. "Tokyo 2020 and Paris 2024 came and went without them. The 2026 AVC Women's Volleyball Continental Championship winner received Asia's direct berth for LA 2028. Thailand grabbed it."

Thai Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul praised the squad for "fighting through height disadvantages and a hostile crowd with discipline, unity, and heart," as reported by The Pattaya News.

## China's Olympic Streak at Risk

China, a three-time Olympic champion (1984, 2004, 2016), has competed at every Olympic Games since 1984 — 11 consecutive appearances. Sunday's defeat denied the host nation an early ticket to its 12th consecutive Olympics.

According to the [FIVB qualification system](https://www.fivb.com/volleyball-and-beach-volleyball-qualification-systems-for-la28-olympic-games-confirmed/), China still has two alternative pathways to Los Angeles 2028. The team can qualify through the 2027 FIVB Volleyball World Championship, where the top three teams not yet qualified earn quota places, or through the FIVB World Ranking pathway, which awards three places to the top-ranked teams not yet qualified at the close of the VNL 2028 preliminary phase.

Given China's current world ranking of 7th, the ranking pathway appears feasible if the team maintains its position. The 2027 World Championship will be jointly hosted by the USA and Canada.

## A Rebuilding Challenge

The loss highlights ongoing challenges under head coach Zhao Yong, who took over during a rebuilding phase. China finished 4th in the 2026 Volleyball Nations League finals, showing promise, but the Asian Championship exposed vulnerabilities against Asian opponents.

The team was also missing key players, including star outside hitter Li Yingying. Wu Mengjie, who scored 21 points in the final, had only recently returned from an injury suffered in May that forced her to miss the VNL.

As [Thai Examiner](https://www.thaiexaminer.com/thai-news-foreigners/2026/08/30/thailand-wins-asian-womens-volleyball-title-for-the-third-time-in-a-thrilling-win-over-china-on-sunday/) reported, China entered the final ranked 7th worldwide while Thailand stood 17th. Thailand's world ranking jumped from 17th to 14th after the win.

## What's Next

Both China, Thailand, and Japan secured spots at the 2027 FIVB Volleyball Women's World Cup through their top-3 finishes at the Asian Championship. For China, the focus now shifts to the 2027 World Championship and maintaining a strong world ranking to secure Olympic qualification through alternative routes.

Thailand, meanwhile, will compete at the 2027 World Championship and make its Olympic debut at LA28 as reigning Asian champions. The team's success validates years of investment, including FIVB Volleyball Empowerment support totaling $168,000 in coaching support, $138,000 in equipment support, and $12,000 for knowledge transfer projects between 2022 and 2026.

For China, the road to Los Angeles is longer than expected, but the path remains open. The team must now regroup and focus on the challenges ahead — the 2027 World Championship and the world ranking pathway — to secure its place at the 2028 Games.
