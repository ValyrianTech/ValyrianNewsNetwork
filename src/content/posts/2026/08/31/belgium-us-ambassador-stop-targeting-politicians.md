# Belgium tells US ambassador to stop targeting politicians

Belgian Foreign Minister Maxime Prévot has formally summoned US Ambassador Bill White for the second time this year, demanding that the diplomat stop making public statements that personally target Belgian politicians. The confrontation follows White's Instagram post mocking Health Minister Frank Vandenbroucke with an AI-generated image and the caption "Who's the biggest loser in Belgium?"

According to [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/08/31/bill-white-aangekomen-bij-buitenlandminister-prevot/), the meeting lasted approximately 1.5 hours on Monday morning. In a formal statement issued afterward, Prévot said the ambassador's conduct "cannot be tolerated," emphasizing that "the role of an ambassador is bound by clear diplomatic rules and customs, including mutual respect and non-interference in domestic affairs."

## The Instagram post that sparked the crisis

The controversy began when White shared an AI-generated image of Vandenbroucke on Instagram, with text reading "Schaadt iedere vorm van levensvreugde" ("Harms all forms of enjoyment in life") and the caption "Who's the biggest loser in Belgium?" The post referenced the ongoing Belgian political debate over new health warnings on alcohol advertising, specifically the proposed slogan change from "Alcoholmisbruik schaadt de gezondheid" to "Alcohol schaadt de gezondheid."

As [HLN](https://www.hln.be/binnenland/prevot-roept-bill-white-op-het-matje-maar-die-laat-zich-niet-het-zwijgen-opleggen-zeer-frank-gesprek-ik-blijf-mijn-mening-posten~ad8dbd25/) reported, Prévot called the post "unacceptable" and summoned White immediately. The Belgian brewers' association had launched a campaign against the new alcohol warning slogan on August 26, with 100 brewers and approximately 1,000 sector stakeholders signing an open letter opposing the change, as [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/08/26/belgische-brouwers-kanten-zich-tegen-slogan-alcohol-schaadt-de/) documented.

## White's defiant response

White arrived at Prévot's office attempting to downplay the reason for the meeting, instead highlighting planned US investments in Belgium. "We will discuss 2 very important things today," he said upon arrival. "On the one hand, the new embassy of 1 billion dollars here in Brussels, as well as the 10 billion in support for new nuclear sites and an LNG deal of 45 billion."

After the meeting, White remained unrepentant. "We had a very frank conversation. I respect what he said. But I will keep posting what I think and asking what people here in Belgium think," he told reporters. On X, he had earlier denied calling Vandenbroucke a loser, describing the summons as "a complete and total waste of time."

The ambassador's confrontational style has become emblematic of the MAGA movement's approach to diplomacy, according to Sven Biscop, an expert in international relations at UGent and the Egmont Institute. "That he has to come to Prévot again is unusual," Biscop told [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/05/06/nieuwe-rel-rond-kwestie-joodse-besnijders-prevot-vlijmscherp-vo/). "It also shows the shamelessness of the MAGA movement of which Bill White is an exponent."

## A pattern of diplomatic friction

This is the second time White has been summoned this year. In February 2026, Prévot called him in after White accused Belgium of "ridiculous and antisemitic persecution" of three Jewish circumcisers in Antwerp. The [VRT NWS profile](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/02/16/wie-is-bill-white-ambassadeur-belgie/) of White describes a former Democrat donor who switched to the Republican camp after Trump's 2016 victory, and who Trump has described as a "benefactor and tireless patriot."

In May 2026, tensions escalated further when White and Israeli Foreign Minister Gideon Sa'ar criticized Belgium's prosecution of two Jewish circumcisers. Prévot responded sharply, saying it was "inappropriate to publicly criticize a country." Defense Minister Theo Francken described US-Belgium relations as "a transatlantic marital crisis."

## Economic interests vs. diplomatic protocol

Despite the tensions, White highlighted massive economic deals during his visit, suggesting that economic interests remain paramount on both sides. The ambassador also announced plans for a new $1 billion US embassy in Brussels, $10 billion in support for new nuclear sites, and a $45 billion LNG deal.

Biscop noted that Belgium is unlikely to expel White given the "enormous economic and security interests at stake," but suggested alternative measures: "There are other ways to make our displeasure clear. For example, he can no longer receive invitations from the government and thus be 'kaltgestellt' (frozen out)."

The broader context includes similar tensions across Europe, where Trump administration ambassadors have been more confrontational with allies. As [RTL Netherlands](https://www.rtl.nl/nieuws/buitenland/artikel/5645117/amerikaanse-ambassadeur-tart-de-belgen-opnieuw-wie-de-grootste) noted, US Ambassador Pete Hoekstra in Canada has repeatedly said Canada should become the 51st US state, reflecting a pattern of confrontational diplomacy.

## What's next

White has stated he will continue posting his opinions, suggesting the diplomatic tension is unlikely to resolve quickly. Prévot warned that "any further provocation would be counterproductive and could lead to unnecessary escalation in our relations," adding that "Belgian sovereignty must be fully respected."

The alcohol health warning debate continues in Belgium, with the brewers' campaign ongoing and the measure still needing to go through EU procedures before taking effect. Meanwhile, the diplomatic standoff between Brussels and Washington shows no signs of abating, as both sides appear unwilling to back down.
