# Woman (28) Found Dead in Mol Home: Probe Launched

The body of a 28-year-old woman was discovered on Sunday in a home on Doornboomstraat in Mol, a municipality in Belgium's Antwerp province. Authorities have launched an investigation into what they describe as a [suspicious death](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/08/31/mol-lichaam-gevonden-vrouw-28-verdacht-overlijden/), with an autopsy pending to determine the exact cause of death.

A resident of the apartment building found the woman and alerted emergency services. The building, which contains separate units, was sealed off as investigators began their work. According to [HLN](https://www.hln.be/mol/lichaam-van-vrouw-28-aangetroffen-in-woning-in-mol-parket-onderzoekt-mogelijk-verdacht-overlijden~a0fb5747/), the woman was found in the bottom-right unit, and the door was subsequently sealed by authorities.

## Investigation Underway

The Antwerp prosecutor's office (Antwerps parket) has opened an investigation into the circumstances surrounding the death. Kristof Aerts, spokesperson for the prosecutor's office, confirmed that the circumstances point toward a suspicious death.

"The circumstances in which the woman was found point in the direction of a suspicious death," Aerts said, as reported by VRT NWS. "A planned autopsy on the body must further determine the precise cause of death."

Overnight, a formal scene examination (known in Belgium as an "afstapping") was conducted at the building. The investigating judge, forensic laboratory, forensic doctor, and police all attended the scene to gather evidence and make official determinations.

## Key Details Emerging

According to HLN, the woman was reportedly not a resident of the building where her body was found. This detail has raised further questions about the circumstances of her death and her connection to the property.

The regional broadcaster [RTV](https://www.rtv.be/actualiteit-en-nieuws/vrouw-van-28-dood-teruggevonden-woning-mol-parket-start-onderzoek) reported that Aerts emphasized the early stage of the investigation: "It seems like a suspicious death, but an autopsy still needs to be done. All possible leads are still open at the moment and the investigation is ongoing."

Authorities have not yet disclosed whether there are any suspects or persons of interest. The [Belga news agency](https://www.msn.com/nl-be/misdaad/algemeen/onderzoek-naar-dood-van-28-jarige-vrouw-in-mol/ar-AA2bg7DX) reported that the cause of death must still be established through an autopsy and other forensic examinations.

## About Mol

Mol is a municipality in the eastern part of the Belgian province of Antwerp, located in the Kempen region. It has approximately 38,000 inhabitants. The Doornboomstraat, where the discovery was made, runs through a residential area of the town, and part of the street was renamed "Heldenstraat" in 2021. The building where the body was found is in the residential section of the street.

## Legal Context

In Belgium, when a death occurs under suspicious circumstances, the public prosecutor's office (parket) initiates an investigation. A formal scene examination ("afstapping") involves the investigating judge (onderzoeksrechter), forensic laboratory, forensic doctor (wetsdokter), and police. An autopsy is typically ordered to determine the cause of death.

The Antwerp prosecutor's office has jurisdiction over the municipality of Mol. The careful language used by officials — describing the death as "possibly suspicious" and emphasizing that "all leads remain open" — reflects the early stage of the investigation and the need to avoid premature conclusions.

The decision to seal the door of the unit where the body was found suggests that authorities are treating the scene carefully, preserving potential evidence. The overnight scene examination with forensic specialists indicates a thorough approach to gathering evidence at this early stage.
