# China's 'First Ship' Harbin Retired After 32 Years of Service

The People's Liberation Army Navy (PLAN) officially decommissioned the destroyer Harbin (hull number 112), known as "China's First Ship" (中华第一舰), on August 28 at a military port in Qingdao, according to [Xinhua News Agency](https://www.news.cn/politics/20260828/8972ea7594eb4801a8106486c73ec04f/c.html). The vessel served as the lead ship of the Type 052 (Luhu class) missile destroyer class, China's second-generation domestically-built main warship.

## A Milestone in Chinese Naval Modernization

The Harbin was launched in August 1991 and entered service in May 1994, representing the highest level of Chinese surface warship technology of its era. Equipped with naval guns, surface-to-air missiles, anti-ship missiles, and the capacity to carry two helicopters, the ship possessed complete anti-air, anti-surface, and anti-submarine warfare capabilities. It was the first PLAN warship equipped with a fully integrated automated combat command system, incorporating Western technology including GE LM2500 gas turbines and French radar and sonar systems.

When the ship made its first public appearance during a naval review in Qingdao in October 1995, it earned the nickname "China's First Ship," symbolizing the nation's naval modernization ambitions.

## Three Decades of Distinguished Service

Over more than 30 years of service, the Harbin completed over 100 major combat readiness and training missions and safely sailed more than 400,000 nautical miles. The ship's service record includes several historic firsts for the PLAN.

In 1997, the Harbin visited four American countries, achieving the first-ever PLAN ship crossing of the Pacific Ocean and the first visit to the American continent. In 2013, during its 14th escort mission, crew members formed a marching contingent for Seychelles' National Day parade — the first time PLAN infantry participated in a foreign country's national day parade, as [Phoenix News](https://news.ifeng.com/c/8vx8mb8rYQW) reported.

Throughout its career, the ship visited 20 countries and 22 ports including Russia, the United States, and Australia, conducted 17 joint military exercises with foreign navies, and hosted senior military leaders from over 60 countries. In 2003, the Central Military Commission awarded the ship the honorary title "Maritime Pioneer Ship" (海上先锋舰).

The decommissioning ceremony was marked by the lowering of the August 1st military flag from the deck mast as officers and soldiers gave their final salute to the ship, according to [China Military Online](http://www.81.cn/yw_208727/16481810.html).

## New Role as Defense Education Base

Following its retirement, the meritorious warship will serve as a national defense education base, docked in Changsha, Hunan Province — the hometown of Xiao Jinguang, the first commander of the PLAN — where it will be open to citizens and tourists for visits and learning, [Lianhe Zaobao](https://www.zaobao.com.sg/news/china/story20260828-9588262) reported.

## Broader Naval Transition

The retirement of the Harbin is part of China's broader naval modernization. With the Type 052 class leaving service, the PLAN destroyer fleet is moving toward "100% vertical launch system (VLS) coverage," as the 052 class was the last destroyer type without VLS. Analysts have noted that the 112 and 113 hull numbers may be inherited by new Type 055 (Renhai class) guided-missile destroyers, which are 10,000+ ton vessels with advanced capabilities, according to [QQ News analysis](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250902A02N8V00).

The sister ship, the Qingdao (hull number 113), the second Type 052 destroyer, is expected to retire in 2027. Analysts also note that the retirement of the 052 class, which had dual helicopter hangars for anti-submarine warfare, may create a temporary gap in PLAN anti-submarine capabilities that newer ships may need to fill, as discussed in [NetEase analysis](https://www.163.com/dy/article/KRK8D8TJ0553BL7O.html).

## A Symbol of China's Naval Rise

The Type 052 destroyer was a critical step in China's naval development. As [Wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/052型导弹驱逐舰) notes, the class was the first PLAN warship equipped with a comprehensive combat data system and represented the highest technical level of Chinese surface warship technology in the mid-1990s. The Harbin's retirement marks the end of an era for the PLAN, closing a chapter on the ships that first demonstrated China's ability to build modern, multi-role destroyers capable of operating far beyond its shores.

As the PLAN continues its rapid modernization with advanced destroyers, frigates, and aircraft carriers, the legacy of the "First Ship" lives on — not only as a museum ship for future generations but as the foundation upon which China's modern blue-water navy was built.
