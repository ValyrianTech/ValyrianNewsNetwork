# Belgium Summons US Ambassador Again Over Attack on Minister

Belgian Foreign Minister Maxime Prévot has announced he will summon US Ambassador Bill White in the coming days after the diplomat posted an AI-generated image mocking Health Minister Frank Vandenbroucke on Instagram. Prévot described the post as "unacceptable," marking the second time in six months that White has been called before the Belgian government.

The controversy erupted after White reacted to the ongoing debate over Belgium's new mandatory alcohol warning slogan, "Alcohol schaadt de gezondheid" (Alcohol harms health), which must appear on all alcohol advertising. The ambassador posted an AI-generated image of Vandenbroucke with the text "Schaadt iedere vorm van levensvreugde" (Harms every form of joy in life) and the caption "Who's the biggest loser in Belgium?" on his Instagram stories, according to [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/liveblog/minister-prevot-roept-vsambassadeur-bill-white-op-het-matje-na-~1780024580839/).

## Diplomatic Escalation

"It is unacceptable that an ambassador accredited in Belgium turns against a member of our government in this way," Prévot said in a statement. "As Foreign Minister, I will call in the American ambassador in the coming days." The Belgian foreign minister is currently visiting the Sahel region, [RTL Info reported](https://www.rtl.be/actu/belgique/politique/cest-intolerable-maxime-prevot-convoque-lambassadeur-des-etats-unis-apres-quil/2026-08-28/article/798589).

Vooruit party chairman Conner Rousseau also responded sharply to White's post. "Bill can be a funny guy. But he knows nothing about healthcare. He only repeats what daddy Trump says. They only care about money, not our health," Rousseau wrote on Instagram, as [The Brussels Times](https://www.brusselstimes.com/belgium/2291759/minister-prevot-summons-us-ambassador-white-after-attack-on-belgian-health-minister) reported.

The incident stems from the Belgian government's decision in March to impose stricter rules on alcohol advertising, including the mandatory health warning. Belgian brewers launched a campaign against the measure on 26 August, arguing that it stigmatizes an entire sector, as [VRT NWS reported](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/08/26/belgische-brouwers-kanten-zich-tegen-slogan-alcohol-schaadt-de/).

## A Pattern of Confrontation

This is not the first time White has clashed with Belgian officials. In February 2026, Prévot summoned the ambassador after White publicly accused Belgium of antisemitism over the prosecution of three Jewish mohels in Antwerp, calling the investigation "ridiculous and antisemitic" and demanding Health Minister Vandenbroucke intervene in the judicial case. Prévot called the accusations "false, offensive, and unacceptable," as [Politico reported](https://www.politico.eu/article/belgium-summons-us-ambassador-bill-white-antisemitism-accusations/).

The February crisis deepened when White demanded that Rousseau retract a video comparing President Donald Trump to Hitler, and declared that the Vooruit leader would be banned from entering the United States. The situation was eventually defused on 24 February when Prévot and White met, with the ambassador agreeing to lift the travel restrictions on Rousseau. The Belgian Foreign Ministry said it was time to "turn the page," according to [Politico](https://www.politico.eu/article/us-belgium-donald-trump-envoy-diplomatic-row/).

Following his February summons, White told reporters there was "no need for an apology," saying "I enjoyed the discussion very much; I love Belgium," as [The Brussels Times reported](https://www.brusselstimes.com/belgium/1978584/theres-no-need-for-an-apology-says-us-ambassador-after-being-summoned-by-belgium/).

## A Rare Diplomatic Measure

Summoning a foreign ambassador is an exceptionally rare diplomatic step. Before the February incident, Belgium had last summoned the US ambassador in 2013 over the Edward Snowden revelations, when the NSA was found to have spied on European allies, as [VRT NWS noted](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/02/16/reacties-rousseau-prevot-ambassadeur-amerika-bill-white/). That Belgium has now done so twice in six months — both times involving the same ambassador and the same minister — signals a growing frustration with White's confrontational style.

White, a former president of the Intrepid Museum in New York and a prominent Trump ally, has been known for his combative approach to diplomacy since arriving in Belgium in 2025. He has publicly intervened in domestic Belgian matters, threatened sanctions, and engaged in social media attacks on Belgian politicians, as [VRT NWS detailed](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/02/16/wie-is-bill-white-ambassadeur-belgie/). His background as a businessman and Trump donor who has supported both Democratic and Republican candidates over the years was noted by [The Brussels Times](https://www.brusselstimes.com/1357129/bill-white-trump-donor-becomes-us-ambassador-to-belgium).

## What's Next

The repeated incidents suggest that the February détente was temporary at best. The pattern of escalation raises questions about the future of US-Belgium relations under the Trump administration, particularly as White continues to employ a MAGA-style approach that mirrors the president's combative rhetoric.

Prévot has not specified when exactly the meeting with White will take place, saying only that it will occur "in the coming days." Whether the ambassador will adopt a more conciliatory tone this time — or whether the relationship will continue to deteriorate — remains to be seen. What is clear is that Belgium's patience with White's diplomatic style appears to be wearing thin, and the coming meeting will be a test of whether the two sides can find common ground.
