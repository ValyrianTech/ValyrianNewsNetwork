# Belgian Man Arrested for Murder After Fatal Collision in Leopoldsburg

A 41-year-old man from Lommel has been arrested on suspicion of murder after his 40-year-old partner died following a collision on Saturday evening in Leopoldsburg, a municipality in Belgium's Limburg province. The man was brought before an investigating judge on Monday and placed under an arrest warrant, according to [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/08/10/vrouw-40-overleden-na-aanrijding-door-partner-41-in-leopolds/).

## The Incident

The incident occurred on Saturday evening, August 8, in the early evening on the Kerkhovenweg in Leopoldsburg. According to [De Lommelse Gazet](https://www.delommelsegazet.be/41-jarige-lommelaar-verdacht-van-moord/), the woman stepped out of the van after an argument, and the man then reversed his vehicle into his partner, striking her fatally.

The woman, a mother of two children, died at the scene from her injuries. The man called emergency services himself and was arrested shortly after the incident for questioning.

## Investigation and Legal Proceedings

The case was initially treated as a traffic accident but has since been upgraded to murder. The public prosecutor's office confirmed that the preliminary qualification is murder, though further investigation is needed to determine whether the act was intentional.

"The woman is said to be the man's partner," said Marijke Teunis, spokesperson for the public prosecutor's office. "As part of the investigation, it is being examined whether the woman was deliberately hit. That is currently not clear. We also don't know what preceded the events."

The prosecutor's office dispatched a traffic expert, the federal police's judicial laboratory, and a forensic doctor to the scene for the necessary investigations.

On Monday, August 10, the man was brought before the investigating judge on suspicion of murder. The judge ordered him to be placed under an arrest warrant and transferred to prison. He is scheduled to appear before the council chamber in Hasselt on Friday, August 14.

## Defense Response

The man's defense lawyer, Vanessa Penninger, said her client denies the murder charge and is cooperating with the investigation.

"He is very emotional, but he is cooperating well with the investigation," Penninger said. "Much still needs to be investigated because the circumstances are still unclear."

According to preliminary findings, the man tested positive for alcohol in a breath test at the time of the incident. A saliva test for drugs came back negative.

## Context and Implications

The case raises questions about domestic violence and the role of alcohol in intimate partner incidents. In Belgium, when a suspect is brought before an investigating judge, the judge can place the suspect under an arrest warrant. The suspect must then appear before the council chamber within five days, which will decide whether to extend the detention.

## What's Next

The investigation will need to determine whether this was intentional murder or an unfortunate accident. Key questions include what preceded the argument, the exact sequence of events on the Kerkhovenweg, and how the man's alcohol consumption factors into the determination of intent.

The man's first appearance before the council chamber in Hasselt on Friday will be a critical step in the legal process. The outcome of the investigation will determine whether the murder charge stands or whether the case is reclassified.
