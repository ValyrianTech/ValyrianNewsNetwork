# CPC Advances Normalization of Work Style Construction

The Communist Party of China (CPC) is advancing the normalization and long-term effectiveness of work style construction, part of a broader effort to institutionalize discipline and improve governance. The initiative was highlighted in a new installment of the "铸魂强党" (Strengthening the Party by Forging the Soul) series published by [Xinhua News Agency](https://www.news.cn/politics/leaders/20260811/6659a24c20df4c609b1f76033dbf77d9/c.html) on August 11, which systematically presents Xi Jinping's key statements on Party building.

## Institutional Foundation

The push for normalized work style construction is rooted in Xi Jinping's Thought on Party Building, formally established at the [National Party Building Work Symposium](https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202606/content_7072157.htm) held in Beijing on June 15. The symposium codified the thought's scientific framework into "Fourteen Persistences" (十四个坚持), one of which is "persisting in promoting the normalization and long-term effectiveness of work style construction." The framework, as detailed by [12371.cn](https://www.12371.cn/2026/06/16/STUD1781576214491514.shtml), represents a comprehensive theoretical consolidation of the Party's approach to governance and discipline.

Central to this effort is the Central Eight-Point Regulations, introduced in December 2012 as the Party's "act of establishing credibility through concrete action in the new era." The 600-character document addressed issues ranging from improving investigation and research to streamlining meetings and reducing extravagance. Since its introduction, the regulations have been reinforced through successive revisions and institutional expansions.

## Sustained Enforcement

The campaign against the "Four Winds" — formalism, bureaucratism, hedonism, and extravagance — remains a central focus. According to a [Xinhua commentary](https://www.qstheory.cn/20260626/4fa924eaab5b4f81b8c4ac77cc12dc08/c.html) published in June, the Party has pursued these issues "with the spirit of driving nails," emphasizing that work style construction requires persistent, sustained effort rather than short-term campaigns.

Enforcement data underscores the scale of the effort. In 2025, the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection (CCDI) directly investigated and supervised 8,116 cases of misconduct and corruption affecting the people, with 967,000 related cases investigated nationwide. The CCDI's [commentary](http://dangjian.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0707/c117092-40754921.html) published in July emphasized that "work style construction is always on the road," warning against complacency in addressing persistent problems.

## Institutional Reforms

The Party has strengthened its institutional framework to support sustained reform. Key measures include the Party Accountability Regulations, which explicitly list lax work style as grounds for accountability, and the revised Party Discipline Regulations, which now include provisions targeting excessive meetings and documentation. These reforms aim to transform work style improvement from a temporary campaign into a permanent institutional mechanism.

## Broader Campaign

The current initiative follows a series of Party-wide education campaigns. In 2025, following the Two Sessions, the Party Central Committee deployed a Party-wide education campaign on deeply implementing the spirit of the Central Eight-Point Regulations. In early 2026, after the Spring Festival, a campaign on establishing and practicing a correct view of political achievements was launched.

The "铸魂强党" series, of which this article is the latest installment, represents the Party's systematic effort to communicate its theoretical framework. Previous installments have covered topics including organizational systems, cadre training, and Party discipline, as reported by [China News Service](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/ll/2026/08-11/10675512.shtml).

## Significance and Outlook

The emphasis on normalization reflects a strategic shift in how the CPC approaches governance improvement. Rather than relying on episodic campaigns, the Party is building durable institutional mechanisms designed to embed discipline into daily operations. This approach signals a long-term commitment to anti-corruption and governance reform.

As the Party continues to develop and refine its theoretical framework, the practical implementation of these institutional reforms will be closely watched. The ongoing education campaigns and the expansion of the "铸魂强党" series suggest that work style construction will remain a central pillar of the Party's governance agenda for the foreseeable future.
