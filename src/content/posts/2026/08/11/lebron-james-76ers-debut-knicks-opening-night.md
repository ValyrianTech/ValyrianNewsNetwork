# LeBron James to Debut with 76ers vs Knicks on Opening Night

LeBron James will make his highly anticipated debut with the Philadelphia 76ers on NBA opening night, Tuesday, Oct. 20, against the defending champion New York Knicks at Madison Square Garden. The [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/nba-schedule-lebron-james-76ers-knicks-christmas-00d478f224ef9611ba6dfc879c204ace) reported that the game, scheduled for 7 p.m. ET, will be part of a tripleheader broadcast on NBC and Peacock, with the Knicks raising their championship banner before tipoff.

The matchup marks the beginning of what James called his "last decision" — a two-year, $8 million deal signed on July 24 that sent shockwaves through the league. The NBA's all-time scoring leader, who turns 42 in December, will be entering his 24th NBA season with a chance to accomplish something no player has ever done: win championships with four different franchises.

## A New Chapter in Philadelphia

James's arrival in Philadelphia came after the 76ers orchestrated one of the most transformative offseasons in franchise history. According to [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/lebron-james-philadelphia-76ers-decision-a3e619dd99b6b21ce7f070107f77b3f4), the team fired president Daryl Morey, hired longtime Cleveland executive Mike Gansey, and pulled off a blockbuster trade with Boston that landed 2024 NBA Finals MVP Jaylen Brown in exchange for Paul George and multiple draft picks.

The 76ers now boast a starting lineup featuring James, Brown, league MVP Joel Embiid, All-Star Tyrese Maxey, and promising second-year standout VJ Edgecombe. It's a collection of talent that has transformed Philadelphia from a team that hasn't advanced past the second round since 2001 into one of the Eastern Conference's most formidable threats.

"This is my last decision," James wrote on X when announcing his choice. "I'm not going for money. I'm not going for family. What am I really playing for at this point? I still want to sacrifice. I still want to work. I still want to grind. I still want to compete, to win and to have a chance at the feeling of winning another championship."

## The Knicks' Coronation

The Knicks enter the season as defending champions for the first time since 1973, ending a 53-year drought. As [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/nba-finals-knicks-champions-0391290b598972abdf5dd230c2f49d82) detailed, New York defeated the San Antonio Spurs 4-1 in the NBA Finals, with Jalen Brunson scoring 45 points in the clinching Game 5 victory. Brunson was named Finals MVP, and the Knicks' title run included a record-setting 29-point comeback in Game 4 at Madison Square Garden.

Opening night will feature the Knicks receiving their championship rings before hosting James and the 76ers — a fitting start to what promises to be one of the most anticipated regular-season games in recent memory. The [NY Post](https://nypost.com/2026/08/11/betting/knicks-opening-night-odds-revealed-with-lebron-james-76ers-visiting-msg/) reported that the Knicks opened as 4.5-point favorites at home, with the over/under set at 228.5.

## A Loaded Eastern Conference

The 76ers-Knicks rivalry is just one storyline in an Eastern Conference that has become remarkably deep. Detroit won 60 games last season, Miami acquired two-time MVP Giannis Antetokounmpo from Milwaukee, and Toronto is hopeful of landing Kawhi Leonard pending an NBA investigation. The Celtics, meanwhile, retooled after trading Brown, signing Mitchell Robinson from the Knicks.

According to [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/nba/breaking-news/article/nba-schedule-lebron-james-making-76ers-debut-in-opening-night-battle-vs-champion-knicks-115335452.html), James's arrival has made the 76ers must-see television, and the league has responded by featuring Philadelphia prominently on its biggest days. Beyond opening night, James will return to Los Angeles to face his former Lakers team on Christmas Day, and he'll play his first home game in Philadelphia on Oct. 22 against the Cleveland Cavaliers.

The full NBA schedule is expected to be released Thursday, Aug. 13. Opening night will also feature the Boston Celtics visiting the Detroit Pistons at 3 p.m. ET and the Oklahoma City Thunder traveling to San Antonio to face the Spurs at 9:30 p.m. ET.

## What's at Stake

For James, the stakes couldn't be higher. A championship with the 76ers would make him the first player in NBA history to win titles with four different franchises — a feat that would further cement his legacy as one of the greatest players the sport has ever seen. For the 76ers, it's a chance to end a championship drought that dates back to 1983, when Julius Erving led the franchise to its last title.

As [USA TODAY Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/nba-opening-night-matchups-revealed-120113612.html) noted, the 76ers' home debut against Cleveland will be followed by a Christmas Day matchup with the Lakers, setting up a season of marquee matchups for James's final chapter. The Eastern Conference has eight different champions in the last eight seasons, and with the Knicks defending their crown and the 76ers loading up, the race for supremacy has never been more compelling.

Opening night at Madison Square Garden will provide the first glimpse of whether James's gamble in Philadelphia pays off — or whether the Knicks' celebration will be spoiled by the league's most accomplished player. Either way, the basketball world will be watching.
