# Flanders Mourns Michiel Vandeweert, Progeria Advocate

Flanders is in mourning following the passing of Michiel Vandeweert (28), a beloved content creator, DJ, and one of the oldest living people with progeria worldwide. Vandeweert died on August 10 after undergoing open-heart surgery, according to [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/08/10/michiel-vandeweert-28-overleden/). His death has triggered an outpouring of tributes from celebrities, sports figures, and the public across Belgium.

## A Life That Defied Expectations

Born in Diepenbeek, Limburg, Vandeweert was diagnosed with progeria (Hutchinson-Gilford syndrome), an extremely rare genetic condition that causes accelerated aging at roughly eight times the normal rate. Doctors initially predicted he would not live past age 12. Yet Vandeweert defied those expectations, reaching 28 and becoming one of the oldest people in the world living with the condition.

Despite his diagnosis, Vandeweert lived life to the fullest. He was active as a DJ, gamer, and content creator, winning a Jamie Award for his gaming content. His [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@michielvandeweert) amassed over 10,000 subscribers, and he gained more than 50,000 followers on Instagram. At age 15, he wrote the book "Ik ben Michiel" (I Am Michiel), and he later delivered a TEDx talk titled "Life's Short, Make the Most of It."

## An Outpouring of Tributes

Comedian William Boeva, a close friend, shared an emotional tribute on Instagram. "Mie, this was not the agreement," he wrote. "We were going to show the world together that we can't be pushed away. Rest In Power, dear friend." Speaking to [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/08/11/bekend-vlaanderen-rouwt-om-michiel-vandeweert/), Boeva described Vandeweert as "a fantastic guy who always went for it and always saw the positive."

Vandeweert's sister Amber (20), who also has progeria, shared her grief on social media. "Rest easy, brother. I miss you so incredibly much," she wrote. Amber is now the only progeria patient in Belgium.

Influencer Ender Scholtens called Vandeweert "the most inspiring person I ever had the honor of meeting," noting they had plans to watch Spider-Man together after his hospital stay. KRC Genk, the football club Vandeweert supported passionately, also paid tribute, writing on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/krcgenk/posts/pfbid02p28unX1Ju2YHRf5zTLU7kHspFiVGZraAVkPsvAdC5DzmjMuoqLF21Bn8hqvyYMvwl): "Michiel was a true enjoyer of life and a loyal Genkie. His courage and positivity were a source of inspiration for many."

Club legend and Belgian national team goalkeeper Thibaut Courtois added simply: "Rest easy, Genkie." Erik Gerits, Head of Community at KRC Genk, told Radio 1 that Vandeweert "was the sunshine in our stadium" and was "carried on the hands of the supporters."

## A Final Wish Fulfilled

Vandeweert's last wish was to have his funeral held at the Cegeka Arena, KRC Genk's stadium, with a livestream so the world could say goodbye. That wish will be fulfilled on Monday, as [TVL](https://www.tvl.be/nieuws/michiel-zijn-laatste-wens-gaat-in-vervulling-hij-krijgt-maandag-afscheid-in-cegeka-arena-182386) reports. A public visitation will take place Sunday at funeral home Reweghs-Pipeleers in Diepenbeek. The family has requested donations to vzw Kleine Prins instead of flowers.

The municipality of Diepenbeek opened a mourning register on Tuesday, the first time the town has done so. Mayor Rik Kriekels (N-VA) said: "It's the first time we open a mourning register, but there was no doubt that we had to do it. Michiel is an example for many, by the way he stood in life."

## A Legacy of Positivity

Vandeweert and his sister Amber were recently the subject of the documentary series "How to Be Alive: Amber and Michiel" by production house Boundbreakers, which premiered on July 25. In the documentary, Vandeweert spoke openly about his life, his love for music and festivals, and his wishes for his own farewell.

His family released a heartfelt statement via [RTL Nieuws](https://www.rtl.nl/nieuws/buitenland/artikel/5637658/progeriapatient-michiel-vandeweert-overleden): "Suddenly it became deafeningly quiet and our fairy tale came to an abrupt end. Few people have touched so many lives in such a short time as Michiel."

## What's Next

As Flanders prepares to say its final goodbye at the Cegeka Arena, Vandeweert's legacy of courage, humor, and relentless positivity will endure. His story has raised awareness about progeria and inspired countless people to embrace life despite its challenges. His sister Amber continues to carry their shared mission forward as the only progeria patient in Belgium.
