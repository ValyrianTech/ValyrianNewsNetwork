# Woman Found Dead in Zoersel Home, Investigation Launched

A 33-year-old woman was found dead in her home in Zoersel, a municipality in the Belgian province of Antwerp, on Saturday morning. The Antwerp public prosecutor's office has opened an investigation into what it is treating as a suspicious death pending further examination, according to [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/08/15/vrouw-overleden-zoersel/).

## Discovery and Initial Response

The woman's body was discovered in her residence on Pijlstraat in Zoersel by her 44-year-old partner, who immediately alerted emergency services. Police and the forensic laboratory were dispatched to the scene, and the home has since been sealed as part of the ongoing investigation.

The partner was taken into custody for questioning as part of standard procedure but was released on Saturday evening, according to VRT NWS. The release does not indicate that the man has been cleared, but rather that investigators did not have sufficient grounds to hold him pending further evidence.

## Investigation Underway

Because the cause of death is not yet clear, the Antwerp public prosecutor's office has formally classified the case as a suspicious death. An autopsy was scheduled to be performed on Sunday, August 16, to determine the exact cause of death and provide investigators with critical answers.

"The prosecutor's office is treating the case for now as a suspicious death pending further investigation," VRT NWS reported. "Because the cause of death is not yet clear, the prosecutor's office has started an investigation into a suspicious death. An autopsy will be performed today on the body, which should provide more clarity."

Following the questioning and the results of the autopsy, authorities will decide whether the partner will be brought before the investigating judge, a step that would indicate formal judicial proceedings in the case.

## About Zoersel

Zoersel is a municipality in the province of Antwerp, in the Flemish region of Belgium, with approximately 23,000 inhabitants. The town falls under the judicial district of Antwerp and is served by the Voorkempen police zone, which also covers the neighboring municipalities of Brecht, Malle, and Schilde. The police zone spans an area of 218 square kilometers and serves nearly 90,000 residents.

## Suspicious Death Protocol in Belgium

In Belgium, when a death occurs under unclear circumstances, the public prosecutor's office routinely opens an investigation into a suspicious death. An autopsy is typically ordered to establish the cause of death, and partners or relatives who discover the body are routinely taken in for questioning as part of standard investigative procedure.

It is not uncommon for individuals questioned in such cases to be released if there is no immediate evidence against them, pending the results of the autopsy and further investigation. Forensic experts note that Belgium performs autopsies on only about 1 percent of deceased persons, compared to approximately 4 percent in France and 8 percent in Germany, making each autopsy in suspicious death cases particularly significant.

## What to Watch For

The autopsy results will be pivotal in determining the direction of this investigation. If the cause of death is determined to be natural or accidental, the case may be closed without further judicial action. However, if the autopsy reveals evidence of foul play, the investigation could escalate significantly, potentially leading to formal charges.

Authorities have not released the identity of the deceased woman, and no further details about the circumstances surrounding her death have been made public at this stage. The case was initially reported by Het Laatste Nieuws and confirmed by the Antwerp prosecutor's office.

As the investigation continues, residents of the quiet Antwerp municipality await answers about what happened inside the home on Pijlstraat. The coming days will be crucial as forensic results are analyzed and authorities determine the next steps in their inquiry.
