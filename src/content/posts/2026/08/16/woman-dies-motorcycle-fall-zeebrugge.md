# Woman (45) Dies After Motorcycle Fall in Zeebrugge

A 45-year-old woman from Zuienkerke has died following a motorcycle accident on the Koffieweg in Zeebrugge, West Flanders, on Saturday evening. According to the federal police, the woman presumably became unwell while riding, causing her to fall from her motorcycle. No other vehicles were involved in the incident.

## The Incident

Emergency services were alerted just before 9 PM on Saturday, 15 August, after the fall was noticed on the Koffieweg, located in the rear harbor area of Zeebrugge. According to [Het Laatste Nieuws](https://www.hln.be/brugge/vrouw-45-overleden-na-valpartij-met-motor-in-zeebrugge-ze-werd-vermoedelijk-onwel~ad760dc4/), the 45-year-old woman was riding her motorcycle when she lost control and fell, making a heavy slide across the road.

The federal police reported that the woman presumably became unwell first, after which she fell from her motorcycle. The Public Prosecutor's Office of West Flanders confirmed that no other vehicles were involved in the accident.

Emergency services arrived at the scene and attempted to resuscitate the victim, but their efforts were unsuccessful, and she died at the scene. The shipping police (scheepvaartpolitie) made the necessary findings at the location, while local police closed the road completely for all traffic pending the investigation.

Because no other vehicles were involved in the accident, no traffic accident expert was appointed, according to [Het Nieuws van West-Vlaanderen](https://www.hetnieuwsvanwestvlaanderen.be/45-jarige-vrouw-uit-zuienkerke-komt-om-het-leven-na-val-met-haar-motor-in-zeebrugge/).

## Location: The Koffieweg in Zeebrugge

The Koffieweg is an important access route in the rear harbor of Zeebrugge, Belgium's second-largest port after Antwerp. The road connects various harbor facilities and sees a significant mix of industrial traffic, including trucks and port vehicles, alongside other road users.

The area has experienced traffic incidents in the past. In April 2024, a 43-year-old motorcyclist was seriously injured on the same road after colliding with a truck that was turning left into a parking area. That accident also required the road to be closed.

In 2022, a new connection bridge (Verbindingsbrug), the longest swing bridge in Europe, was opened in the area, connecting the Verschaveweg and the Koffieweg.

## The Victim's Home Municipality

The victim was a resident of Zuienkerke, a small municipality in West Flanders with approximately 2,600 inhabitants. Zuienkerke is one of the few municipalities in the province whose population has declined in recent years. It falls under the police zone Blankenberge/Zuienkerke.

The name of the victim has not been disclosed by authorities.

## Investigation and Medical Cause

As reported by [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/08/16/vrouw-op-motorfiets-komt-om-na-valpartij-in-dudzele-vermoedeli/), the police suspect that the woman developed health problems while riding, which led to the fall. The exact medical cause of her becoming unwell has not yet been disclosed, and the investigation into the circumstances remains ongoing.

The [Krant van West-Vlaanderen](https://kw.be/nieuws/ongevallen/verkeersongeval/vrouw-45-overleden-na-valpartij-met-motor-in-zeebrugge-ze-werd-vermoedelijk-onwel/) confirmed that the accident occurred approximately in the middle of the Koffieweg, and that the road was fully closed to all traffic while authorities conducted their investigation.

## Motorcycle Safety Concerns

Motorcycle accidents remain a significant concern in Belgium, with motorcyclists being among the most vulnerable road users. When a rider becomes unwell while riding, the consequences can be particularly severe due to the lack of protection and the speeds at which motorcycles travel.

The incident has drawn condolences from the local community, with readers expressing sympathy for the victim's family on various news platforms. [Focus-WTV](https://focus-wtv.be/nieuws/vrouw-45-overleden-na-val-met-motor-in-zeebrugge) reported that the accident occurred in the Koffieweg in Zeebrugge on Saturday evening.

## What's Next

The investigation into the exact medical cause of the woman's unwellness is ongoing. Authorities have not released further details about the circumstances surrounding the accident. The local community in Zuienkerke and the wider Zeebrugge area has been left in mourning following the tragic incident.

As the investigation continues, questions remain about what specific health issue may have caused the rider to lose consciousness or control while on the road. The findings could potentially inform broader discussions about motorcycle safety and the risks faced by riders who experience sudden medical emergencies while on the road.
