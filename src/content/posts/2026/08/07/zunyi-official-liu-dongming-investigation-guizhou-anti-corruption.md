# Zunyi Official Under Investigation in Anti-Corruption Push

Liu Dongming, Vice Director of the Standing Committee of the Zunyi Municipal People's Congress and Secretary of the Honghuagang District Committee in Guizhou Province, is under review and investigation for suspected serious discipline and law violations, according to [Xinhua News](https://www.news.cn/politics/20260807/933d9c9109de44f890f0b92d0d4b4d62/c.html). The announcement, made by the Guizhou Provincial Commission for Discipline Inspection on August 7, marks the latest development in China's sustained anti-corruption campaign at the local government level.

## Context: A Province Under Scrutiny

Guizhou Province has witnessed an intensified anti-corruption drive throughout 2026, with investigations spanning multiple government sectors and levels. The province saw four female department-level officials fall within just 34 days between late March and late April, involving the transport, judicial, discipline inspection, and women's federation systems, as [Lianhe Zaobao](https://www.zaobao.com.sg/news/china/story20260430-8977070) reported. More recently, Liu Benli, a member of the Standing Committee of the Guiyang Municipal Party Committee and Secretary-General, was placed under investigation on July 31.

Liu Dongming's case extends this pattern to Zunyi, a prefecture-level city in northern Guizhou historically significant as the site of the 1935 Zunyi Conference, a pivotal turning point in the Chinese Communist Party's history.

## A Pattern of Investigations in Zunyi

The investigation of Liu Dongming follows a notable pattern of anti-corruption actions targeting Zunyi's leadership. Most significantly, Liu held the same dual role as Jiang Lingchun, who was investigated in December 2021 while serving as both Honghuagang District Committee Secretary and Vice Director of the Zunyi Municipal People's Congress Standing Committee. According to [Beijing Daily](https://xinwen.bjd.com.cn/content/s6333af90e4b015f9c7a16dc2.html), Jiang was expelled from the Party and public office in June 2022 after being found to have violated political, organizational, work, and life discipline, and was subsequently prosecuted on bribery charges.

Wang Jinjiang, another former Honghuagang District Committee Secretary, was sentenced to 11 years in prison in 2023 with a fine of 1 million yuan for bribery, according to research compiled from official sources.

More recently, Huang Wei, who served as Mayor of Zunyi City for six years, was placed under investigation on February 3, 2026, for serious discipline violations. As [Nandu Video](https://m.mp.oeeee.com/oe/BAAFRD0000202602031519170.html) reported, the 54-year-old Huang had been serving as Zunyi's mayor since January 2020 when the announcement was made. His background, detailed by [Guiyang News](http://www.gynews.com/2026/02/03/321.html), shows a career spanning provincial party organization, prefecture-level leadership, and ultimately the mayoralty of Zunyi.

## Liu Dongming's Role and Career

Liu Dongming was serving in a dual capacity at the time of his investigation: Vice Director of the Standing Committee of the Zunyi Municipal People's Congress, a vice-department level position, and Secretary of the Honghuagang District Committee, a county-level position. He was confirmed in this dual role as early as May 2023, when he was reported to have praised a local poet at a district committee meeting, as noted by [Macau City Daily](http://www.fanfucn.cn/liangan/20230517/1811.html). His exact appointment dates and full career history have not yet been publicly disclosed.

## Analysis: Systemic Focus on Zunyi's Leadership

The pattern of investigations in Zunyi, particularly targeting the Honghuagang District Secretary position, suggests a systemic anti-corruption focus on the city's leadership network. The fact that Liu Dongming occupied the same positions as the previously investigated Jiang Lingchun raises questions about potential continuity in corruption investigations within the same political network.

The Guizhou Provincial Commission for Discipline Inspection's announcement, as [reported by NetEase](https://c.m.163.com/news/a/L3NGPBST0534A4SC.html), stated that Liu is suspected of serious discipline and law violations and is currently undergoing disciplinary review and supervisory investigation. The specific allegations against him have not yet been disclosed.

## What's Next

As the investigation proceeds, observers will be watching for several key developments: the specific nature of the alleged violations, whether the case is connected to earlier investigations of Huang Wei or Jiang Lingchun, and the eventual outcome of the disciplinary review. The case underscores the continued reach of China's anti-corruption campaign into local government structures, particularly in Guizhou Province, where 2026 has seen an unusually high number of senior official investigations across multiple sectors and levels of government.

For now, Liu Dongming's case adds another chapter to the ongoing narrative of anti-corruption enforcement in China's southwestern provinces, as the campaign shows no signs of abating at the local government level.
