# Alcatraz Metal Festival Sells Out for First Time in History

The Alcatraz metal festival in Kortrijk, Belgium has sold out completely for the first time in its 18-year history, with 75,000 visitors expected across its four-day run from August 6-9. The milestone marks a significant achievement for one of Europe's premier metal music festivals, which has grown from a small indoor event in Deinze into a major international attraction.

According to [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/08/06/metalfestival-alcatraz-in-kortrijk-begonnen/), the festival is operating at maximum capacity for the first time ever, with 15,000 visitors expected on Thursday and 20,000 each on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday. The expanded camping grounds at the De Lange Munte domain will accommodate 9,000 campers.

![Alcatraz Open Air 2026 festival poster](https://eurotravelo.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/poster.jpg)

## A Historic Sell-Out

"Last year all combo tickets were sold out for the first time, but this year all day tickets are also sold out," festival spokesperson Bernard De Riemacker told VRT NWS. "We thank our 1,200 staff members first and foremost, it is their dedication that is being rewarded."

The complete sell-out builds on steady growth that has seen the festival expand dramatically over the past several years. In 2023, the festival welcomed a then-record 45,000 visitors for its 15th edition, as [KW.be](https://kw.be/nieuws/cultuur/muziek/alcatraz-klokt-af-op-45-000-bezoekers-en-kijkt-al-uit-naar-2024/) reported. By 2025, combo tickets had sold out for the first time, a milestone covered by [Het Nieuws van West-Vlaanderen](https://www.hetnieuwsvanwestvlaanderen.be/voor-het-eerst-uitverkocht-vierdaags-alcatraz-in-kortrijk-startte-gisteren/).

## More Than Music: An Immersive Experience

Alcatraz has long distinguished itself from other metal festivals through its immersive prison theme, inspired by the infamous Alcatraz Federal Penitentiary in San Francisco Bay. The festival grounds feature guard towers, prison-themed decor, and actors dressed as guards entertaining festival-goers.

This year, the festival introduced a new deathride (zip line) from its 20-meter guard tower replica, allowing visitors to literally "escape" from the prison theme. "Besides the music, we are mainly an experience festival," De Riemacker explained to [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/07/30/voorbereidingen-alcatraz-metalfestival-kortrijk-deathride/). "That's how we came up with the idea of the deathride, which perfectly fits the Alcatraz feeling and the prison story we attach to it."

The guard tower itself, introduced in 2025 as a 19.5-meter replica built from authentic plans, has become a signature feature of the festival, as [VRT NWS reported](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2025/07/23/metalfestival-alcatraz-bouwt-replica-gevangenistoren-op-terrein/) at the time. It also houses Canal Metal, a radio studio hosted by Studio Brussel personality Sofie Engelen.

## Digital Innovation and Expanded Opening

For the first time, the festival has moved to a fully digital payment system using "chains" instead of paper vouchers. Visitors can purchase chains in advance at €3.40 or on-site at €3.60, with refunds available for unused balances.

The festival has also expanded its Thursday opening, with all four stages now operational from the first day. "A logical step," De Riemacker said. "Our teams are ready anyway, so we might as well open everything at once." More than 25 Belgian bands are among the 123 acts performing across the Prison, Swamp, Helldorado, and La Morgue stages.

Residents of Kortrijk and Zwevegem continue to receive 3,500 free tickets for the Thursday opening night, maintaining the festival's strong community ties.

## Channel Zero's Historic Farewell

The opening night featured a particularly poignant moment as Belgian metal legends Channel Zero played their last open-air concert. The band, which announced its farewell in October 2024, is on a farewell tour culminating in a final show at the AB in Brussels in December 2026.

"They close that evening with what will be their last big open-air concert," De Riemacker said. "The band is on a farewell tour and it will be a historic farewell concert at Alcatraz." As [VRT NWS reported](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2024/10/23/belgische-metalband-channel-zero-stopt-ermee-laatste-concert-in/) on the announcement, vocalist Franky De Smet-Van Damme said: "It has been a hell of a ride. Channel Zero has given me so much as a person and as an artist. But you also have to dare to close things at their peak."

## Looking Ahead

With a complete sell-out and 123 bands across four days, the 2026 edition of Alcatraz represents the festival's most ambitious year yet. The lineup features international heavyweights including Powerwolf, Lamb of God, Arch Enemy, and Primus, alongside a strong contingent of Belgian acts.

The festival's continued growth from its origins as a small indoor event in 2008 to one of Europe's premier metal festivals reflects both the enduring appeal of the genre and the festival's unique ability to blend music with immersive theatrical experiences. The opening day saw long queues at the gates, with wait times quickly exceeding one hour as the festival operated at full capacity for the first time — a testament to the unprecedented demand for this year's edition.

For festival-goers and organizers alike, the question now turns to what comes next for an event that has reached full capacity. With the deathride, digital payment systems, and expanded programming already in place, Alcatraz continues to push the boundaries of what a metal festival can be.
