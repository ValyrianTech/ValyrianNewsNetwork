# Mika Godts Completes Dream Move to PSG in Record-Breaking Transfer

Belgian winger Mika Godts has completed his dream move from Ajax to Paris Saint-Germain, signing a contract with the two-time Champions League winners until 2031. The transfer fee can rise to a maximum of 55 million euros, making the 21-year-old the ninth most expensive Belgian transfer in football history and the fourth biggest sale in Ajax's history.

## A Transfer Years in the Making

Godts' journey to the French capital began long before this summer's whirlwind negotiations. According to [Sporza](https://sporza.be/nl/2026/08/16/bijna-bij-bayern-een-cruciaal-facetime-gesprek-en-geen-ziektebriefje-het-verhaal-van-de-droomdeal-die-mika-godts-naar-parijs-bracht~1786875095525/), PSG had scouting reports on Godts dating back to his youth career in Belgium. "The club still had reports on Mika from his youth," revealed Niels De Jonck, Godts' agent, who has guided the player for the past six years. "They've known him since then. After his move to Ajax, they kept a close eye on him."

PSG's long-term interest paid off. After a breakout 2025/2026 season in which Godts scored 17 goals and provided 13 assists in 32 Eredivisie matches, the Parisian giants accelerated their pursuit. The Belgian was named Johan Cruijff Talent of the Year in the Eredivisie and received the Marco van Basten Trophy as Ajax's Talent of the Year.

## The Bayern Munich Near-Miss

The transfer story could have taken a very different turn. As Sporza reported, Godts came within a hair's breadth of joining Bayern Munich. "It was very close that Mika would have ended up at Bayern Munich," De Jonck explained. "Before the concrete negotiations with PSG, they pushed hard to bring Mika in. But they suddenly chose a different type of player." Bayern ultimately selected Ismael Saibari instead, opening the door for PSG.

## The FaceTime That Sealed the Deal

With Bayern out of the picture, PSG moved decisively in early July. Coach Luis Enrique personally reached out to Godts via FaceTime, a conversation that proved crucial. "Immediately a very pleasant and warm conversation," De Jonck recalled. "With a lot of humor, but of course also room for the serious and sporting part. Enrique clearly explained how he sees Mika. He likes players who aren't tied to one position, but roam across the field, find spaces, and provide unpredictability."

The personal touch from the coach convinced Godts that PSG was the right destination. Club-level negotiations followed with remarkable speed. "We discussed the sporting plan and of course the financial side, but in barely an hour we were actually done," said De Jonck. "I've had much more difficult negotiations in my career. That's the advantage of working with a hyper-professional club like PSG."

## Ajax's Initial Resistance

Ajax did not part with their star player willingly. Sporting director Jordi Cruijff initially stated the club wanted to keep Godts for at least another season. PSG's first bid of 40 million euros plus 5 million in bonuses was rejected outright. According to [Voetbalprimeur](https://www.voetbalprimeur.be/nieuws/2122327/paris-saint-germain-wil-ajax-transfer-van-mika-godts-deze-week-afronden.html), super-agent Jorge Mendes was brought in on a no-cure-no-pay basis to help broker a deal, with Ajax holding out for a fee closer to 60-65 million euros.

Ultimately, a compromise was reached. As [NOS](https://nos.nl/artikel/2626889-godts-maakt-transfer-naar-paris-saint-germain-ajax-ontvangt-55-miljoen-euro) confirmed, Ajax accepted a fee of approximately 55 million euros, which could rise to 58 million with bonuses. The official announcement came on 15 August, with Ajax [confirming the transfer](https://www.ajax.nl/club/pers/persberichten/mika-godts-vertrekt-naar-paris-saint-germain/) in a press release.

## A Remarkable Financial Success Story

Ajax signed Godts from KRC Genk for just 1 million euros in January 2023. Three years later, they have sold him for 55 million euros — a 5,400 percent return on investment. Only Antony (95M to Manchester United), Frenkie de Jong (86M to Barcelona), Matthijs de Ligt (85M to Juventus), and Lisandro Martinez (57M to Manchester United) fetched higher fees for the Amsterdam club.

In total, Godts made 115 official appearances for Ajax, scoring 26 goals and providing 28 assists. He made his debut for the Belgian national team on 28 March 2026 in an away match against the United States.

## Professionalism Through the Transfer Saga

Throughout the transfer speculation, Godts maintained remarkable professionalism. As Sporza noted, many players in his position might have submitted a "sick note" to force a move or avoid injury. Godts did the opposite — he continued to deliver for Ajax, scoring and assisting in Conference League qualifiers and the Eredivisie opener against PEC Zwolle.

"Mika is a fantastic professional," said De Jonck. "He works incredibly hard and never causes problems. From the beginning, Mika assured that he wanted to give everything for Ajax until the very last minute." Ajax showed their appreciation by unveiling a large banner in Paris reading "The future belongs to young Godts" as a farewell gesture, as [Ajax reported](https://www.ajax.nl/artikelen/ajax-neemt-afscheid-van-godts-met-een-groot-spandoek-in-parijs).

## World Cup Controversy Resurfaces

The transfer has reignited debate about Godts' exclusion from Belgium's 2026 World Cup squad. Despite playing in warm-up matches against the USA and Mexico, then-coach Rudi Garcia left him out of the final selection. The decision now looks increasingly questionable given PSG's willingness to invest 55 million euros in the player.

Former Belgian women's national team player Imke Courtois expressed mixed feelings. "Proud of Mika Godts, but also a certain shame," she told [Voetbalprimeur](https://www.voetbalprimeur.be/nieuws/2124103/ajax-vertrek-godts-zorgt-voor-schaamte-in-belgie.html). "You realize it's that boy who did play in the friendly matches against America and Mexico, but who was then determined not to be allowed to go to the World Cup. That is in a certain way embarrassing. Luis Enrique apparently sees a lot in him."

Rudi Garcia has since been replaced by Mark van Bommel as Belgium coach, leaving the door open for Godts' international return.

## What Awaits at PSG

At PSG, Godts will face fierce competition for the left wing position, competing with Khvicha Kvaratskhelia and Bradley Barcola, who may depart for Liverpool. However, as [RTL Nieuws](https://www.rtl.nl/nieuws/sport/artikel/5639361/ajax-bevestigt-vertrek-sterspeler-mika-godts-naar-psg) noted, Luis Enrique's rotation-heavy approach should provide opportunities. Last season, Ballon d'Or winner Ousmane Dembele played only 34 percent of Ligue 1 matches, and Desire Doue featured in less than half.

"Does Mika need to be a starter at PSG at 21? Not for me," said De Jonck. "He can learn here from the best coach and players in the world."

Godts himself is ready for the challenge. "I am enormously proud to be able to play at one of the best clubs in the world," he said upon his presentation. "I can't wait to put on this shirt and experience the Parc des Princes and all the fans. I will give everything on the field to repay the trust I receive from president Nasser Al-Khelaifi, Luis Campos, and coach Luis Enrique."

## Looking Ahead

The transfer represents a significant moment for Belgian football, placing Godts alongside the country's most expensive exports. For Ajax, the 55 million euros will be reinvested in reinforcements, with the club reportedly targeting a right winger, left winger, right-footed central defender, and defensive midfielder.

For Godts, the dream has become reality. As his agent put it: "Seeing Mika leave for the double Champions League winner at 21? Not much tops that." The question now is whether the young Belgian can translate his Ajax brilliance to the grandest stage in European football.
