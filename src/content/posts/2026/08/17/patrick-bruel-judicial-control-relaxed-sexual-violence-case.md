# Bruel's Judicial Control Relaxed in Sexual Violence Case

French singer and actor Patrick Bruel, who faces formal investigation for sexual violence charges, has been granted permission to leave French territory after a judge partially lifted his judicial control restrictions. The decision, confirmed by the Nanterre prosecutor's office, has sparked immediate backlash from plaintiffs' lawyers and been appealed by prosecutors.

## Context and Background

The 67-year-old artist was formally placed under investigation (mis en examen) in mid-June 2026 for rape, attempted rape, sexual assault, and sexual harassment across four separate cases, following a [Mediapart investigation](https://www.mediapart.fr/journal/france/180326/patrick-bruel-accuse-de-violences-sexuelles-par-huit-femmes) that first revealed testimonies from eight women in March 2026. The investigation, led by journalist Marine Turchi, has since documented accounts from more than 30 women alleging sexual violence between 1992 and 2019.

A second Mediapart investigation in May added [15 new testimonies](https://www.mediapart.fr/journal/france/070526/affaire-patrick-bruel-quinze-nouvelles-femmes-mettent-en-cause-le-chanteur), and the case gained extraordinary momentum when TV host Flavie Flament publicly filed a rape complaint against Bruel, alleging he drugged and assaulted her in 1991 when she was 16 years old. [Le Dauphine Libere reported](https://www.ledauphine.com/faits-divers-justice/2026/05/15/l-animatrice-flavie-flament-a-porte-plainte-pour-viol-contre-patrick-bruel) that Flament stated: "I have another appointment with my past. And a man who pillaged my adolescence."

## Key Developments

On July 21, 2026, the investigating judge granted a request from Bruel's lawyers to partially lift the judicial control measure that prohibited him from leaving French national territory. According to [RTBF](https://www.rtbf.be/article/patrick-bruel-accuse-de-violences-sexuelles-controle-judiciaire-assoupli-pour-le-chanteur-11770915), Deputy Prosecutor Virginie Deneux confirmed: "On July 21, 2026, the investigating judge, seized by Patrick Bruel's lawyers of a request for partial lifting of the judicial control concerning the prohibition to leave national territory, granted it."

The Nanterre prosecutor's office immediately appealed the decision, considering it unjustified and excessive. The appeal hearing took place on August 14 before the chambre de l'instruction of the Versailles Court of Appeal, with the decision placed in deliberation until September 16, 2026.

[Mediapart revealed](https://www.mediapart.fr/journal/france/140826/le-controle-judiciaire-de-patrick-bruel-ete-allege) the relaxation on Friday evening, noting that readers had reported seeing Bruel abroad this summer, in airport corridors or in hotels. The singer has been formally investigated in four cases and holds assisted witness status in four others, according to [RTS](https://www.rts.ch/info/monde/2026/article/affaire-patrick-bruel-le-chanteur-autorise-a-voyager-a-l-etranger-le-parquet-fait-appel-29329544.html).

At least 31 complaints have been filed against Bruel in total, with at least eight new complaints filed since his formal investigation for acts committed between September 1992 and 2014. The two most recent complaints were filed on July 16 and 23 with the Paris judicial police, as [RTBF reported](https://www.rtbf.be/article/patrick-bruel-est-vise-par-deux-nouvelles-plaintes-pour-violences-sexuelles-11763741).

## Reactions and Analysis

The relaxation of judicial control has provoked strong reactions from those representing the plaintiffs. Me Corinne Herrmann, lawyer for Flavie Flament and five other plaintiffs, told RTL: "The message being sent to them is inadmissible, it is incomprehensible." She added: "All these women who file complaints for crimes, who had the courage to speak, who had the courage to free their voice, don't understand..." [Voici reported](https://www.voici.fr/celebrites/affaire-patrick-bruel-lavocate-de-flavie-flament-revoltee-par-lallegement-de-son-controle-judiciaire-c-est-inadmissible-827728) on her criticism of what she called "the lightness of justice."

Actress and activist Andrea Bescond posted an ironic Instagram message: "Justice wishes Patrick Bruel a nice vacation, despite the testimonies of some thirty courageous women. Patriarchal contempt exceeds all realism." Flament shared the post on her Instagram story.

Herrmann also announced plans to file two new complaints and to refer the case to the National Pole for Serial Crimes (PCSNE), questioning: "How is it that with all these victims who have come forward, we haven't taken the seriousness of this case?"

## What's Next

The Versailles Court of Appeal will deliver its decision on the prosecutor's appeal on September 16, 2026. Meanwhile, Bruel remains authorized to travel abroad while continuing to contest all accusations against him. His lawyers - Fanny Colin, Celine Lasek, and Christophe Ingrain - have not responded to requests for comment.

The case has already had significant professional consequences for the singer, who cancelled his summer festival tour and withdrew from Les Enfoires charity concerts. As the legal proceedings continue to unfold, the September 16 ruling will be a critical moment for all parties involved, determining whether the relaxation of judicial control stands or is reversed.
