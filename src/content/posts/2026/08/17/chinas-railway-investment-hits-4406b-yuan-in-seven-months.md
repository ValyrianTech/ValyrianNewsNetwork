# China's Railway Investment Hits 440.6B Yuan in Seven Months

China's railway sector completed fixed asset investment of 440.6 billion yuan (approximately $64.9 billion) in the first seven months of 2026, up 1.8 percent year-on-year, according to [Xinhua News](https://www.news.cn/fortune/20260817/a4f1bc19d0df4829bb3740f0a3bdf03e/c.html). The figure reflects continued strong infrastructure spending as the country accelerates construction of its modern railway network.

## Steady Investment Growth

The investment trajectory through 2026 shows consistent but gradually moderating growth. After reaching 137.9 billion yuan in the first quarter (up 5.1 percent year-on-year) and 363.2 billion yuan in the first half (up 2.1 percent), the January-July figure of 440.6 billion yuan represents a 1.8 percent increase over the same period last year.

China State Railway Group Co., Ltd. (China Railway), the national railway operator, announced the figures via its official "China Railway" WeChat account on August 17. The company stated that railway construction has been "advancing with high quality and efficiency," with the modern railway infrastructure system being accelerated and investment-driven effects injecting "strong momentum into regional economic and social development."

The investment data aligns with the broader national strategy of expanding domestic demand and stabilizing growth. As [China Daily](https://global.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202608/10/WS6a792ceca310986e2b469dad.html) reported, Wang Qiming, deputy director of the National Railway Administration, noted in July that railway fixed asset investment has been "playing an important role in expanding domestic demand and stabilizing growth."

## Major Projects Advancing

July saw significant progress on multiple key railway projects across the country. The Guangzhou-Zhanjiang High-Speed Railway's Foshan Station opened for operation, reducing travel time between Guangzhou and Foshan city centers to just 18 minutes. The Xi'an-Ankang High-Speed Railway launched full-line pull-through testing, while the Harbin-Tieli-Yichun High-Speed Railway completed joint commissioning and testing.

Other notable milestones include the completion of track laying on the Shanxi section of the Xiong'an-Xinzhou High-Speed Railway, the start of ballastless track construction on the Nantong-Ningbo High-Speed Railway's Fenhu Tunnel, and the completion of all box girder erection on the Ankang-Chongqing section of the Xi'an-Chongqing High-Speed Railway. The Chongqing-Xiamen High-Speed Railway's Caiyuanba Yangtze River Railway Tunnel was also holed through.

In the first half of 2026, a total of 355.2 kilometers of new railway lines were put into operation, according to [dahe.cn](https://en.dahe.cn/2026/07-17/2110983.html). The company also reported that Chinese railways handled 2.015 billion metric tons of freight in the first half, up 1.8 percent year-on-year.

## Building for the 15th Five-Year Plan

The current investment cycle marks a strong start to China's railway development during the 15th Five-Year Plan period (2026-2030). China has built the world's largest high-speed railway network, with total operating railway mileage reaching 165,000 kilometers by the end of 2025, including more than 50,000 kilometers of high-speed rail.

Under the current five-year plan, China Railway aims to expand total operating mileage to 180,000 kilometers by end-2030, with high-speed rail increasing to 60,000 kilometers. These targets represent a significant expansion of the network and underscore the continued strategic importance of railway infrastructure to China's economic development.

The investment growth rate has been moderating through 2026, reflecting a maturing railway network with continued but more measured investment compared to earlier years. In 2025, railway fixed asset investment reached a historical high of 901.5 billion yuan, up 6 percent year-on-year, as [China Daily](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202511/18/WS691bd700a310d6866eb2a0fc.html) reported in November 2025.

## Intelligent Construction and Innovation

Chinese railway builders are increasingly deploying intelligent and smart tools in modern railway construction. Zhao Yanlong, a superintendent at China Railway No. 4 Engineering Group (CREC4), told China Daily that these innovations "serve as a witness to the upgrade of the national infrastructure." He described builders as "creating steel 'dragons' on Chinese soil, making cities closely connected and the countryside rejuvenated, helping solidify the country's social and economic progress."

CREC4 recently rotated a 145.5-meter beam weighing 10,000 metric tons on the Shaoyang-Yongzhou High-Speed Railway in Hunan province, part of the 2,300-kilometer Hohhot-Nanning HSR corridor. The operation demonstrated the advanced engineering capabilities being deployed across China's railway construction projects.

## Outlook

China Railway has stated that it will continue to coordinate the planning and construction of new lines with the quality improvement and capacity expansion of conventional railways. The company plans to accelerate preliminary work on key projects, optimize construction organization, and ensure completion of annual construction tasks.

Looking ahead, railway investment is expected to remain a key pillar of China's infrastructure strategy, supporting regional connectivity, economic development, and the modernization of the country's comprehensive transportation system. The continued expansion of the network will be closely watched as China works toward its ambitious 2030 targets.
