# China U17 Women Rout Real Madrid 5-0 in Shanghai Cup Opener

China's U17 women's football team delivered a statement performance on Saturday, dismantling Real Madrid's U17 women's side 5-0 in the opening round of Group A at the 2026 Shanghai Rising Stars Champions Cup. The dominant victory at Yuanshen Sports Center showcased the depth of young Chinese women's football talent just two months ahead of the U17 Women's World Cup.

## A Fast Start and Relentless Attack

China seized control from the opening whistle. In the 8th minute, captain Zhao Siqing pounced on a rebound to open the scoring after Yang Yuhan's cross set up the initial chance. The early breakthrough set the tone for a one-sided encounter, as [Xinhua News](https://www.news.cn/20260817/c41418d9a7934a8a874c73a0956fbef2/c.html) reported.

Midfielder Yang Yuhan turned provider again in the 23rd minute, delivering a precise through ball that sent forward Fang Zhenzhu through on goal. Fang calmly dribbled past the goalkeeper and slotted home to double China's lead before halftime.

The second half brought no respite for the Spanish side. Fang Zhenzhu added her second in the 58th minute, capitalizing on a defensive error to score from a tight angle. Zhao Siqing then produced the highlight of the match in the 78th minute, unleashing a spectacular long-range strike from the edge of the box that flew into the top corner. Fang completed her hat-trick four minutes later with a stunning solo run from midfield, driving through the Real Madrid defense before finishing past the advancing goalkeeper.

According to [PP Sports](https://www.ppsports.com/article/news/2594778.html), the match was effectively decided within the first 23 minutes, with China's clinical finishing and organized pressing leaving Real Madrid unable to mount any meaningful response.

## Coach Cautious Despite Scoreline

Despite the emphatic margin, head coach Ma Xiaoxu struck a measured tone in her post-match assessment. The former Chinese women's national team star acknowledged her players' fighting spirit but noted areas for improvement.

"Overall, the players' fighting attitude is commendable," Ma said, as quoted by Xinhua. "However, after taking a big lead, the team's focus and mentality loosened. There is still room for improvement in overall style and attention to detail."

Ma, who is preparing the squad for the U17 Women's World Cup in October, was candid about the team's development stage. "The team's overall development is about 70 percent complete," she said. "Young teams generally lack big-match experience and on-field adaptability. Under high-pressure match scenarios, the players' ability to think independently and adjust on the field is still a clear weakness."

Fang Zhenzhu, who scored three of China's five goals, admitted the result exceeded expectations. "I did not expect such a big score," she said. "The team was fully mentally prepared before the match and treated this as a tough battle. We gained a lot of valuable experience."

## Raul Watches Daughter's Team Struggle

Adding international intrigue to the occasion, Real Madrid legend Raul Gonzalez Blanco was in attendance at Yuanshen Sports Center to watch his daughter, Maria Gonzalez, who wore the number 7 jersey for the Madrid youth side. According to [Shanghai Observer via NetEase](https://www.163.com/dy/article/L4GMEAUU055040N3.html), Raul and his wife traveled to Shanghai specifically to support their daughter, who is considered one of the standout talents in Real Madrid's women's youth setup.

Ma Xiaoxu revealed that her coaching staff had been aware of Maria's presence but deliberately withheld the information from the players. "We knew about this information, but we did not tell the players before the match," she said. "We wanted them to focus on the overall game rather than targeting a specific player. Of course, the number 7 is a talented player and she stands out in the Real Madrid women's youth team."

Reports described Raul's expression shifting from relaxed smiles at the start of the match to furrowed brows at halftime, and finally to a blank expression at the final whistle as his daughter's team was comprehensively outplayed.

## Tournament Significance

This year marks the first time the Shanghai Rising Stars Champions Cup has included a women's division since the tournament's inception five years ago. The women's competition features eight teams split into two groups, with China U17 drawn alongside Real Madrid, Bayern Munich, and Manchester City in Group A. The tournament serves as crucial preparation for China's national youth teams ahead of the U17 World Cup in October, as [Dongqiudi](https://m.dongqiudi.com/article/6065719.html) noted.

The result follows the China U17 men's team's title-winning performance in the men's division earlier this month, underscoring a broader surge in Chinese youth football development.

## What's Next

China U17 will face Bayern Munich U17 in their second Group A match on August 17 at 16:00 Beijing time. Bayern, another European powerhouse youth side, will present a different challenge as China looks to build momentum toward the U17 Women's World Cup in October. Ma Xiaoxu emphasized the value of this tournament for her squad's development. "This tournament brings together many high-level European youth teams," she said. "The overall competitive level is very high, making it a valuable training opportunity for our team."
