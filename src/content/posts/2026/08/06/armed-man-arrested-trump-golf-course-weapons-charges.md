# Armed Man Arrested at Trump Golf Course Faces Charges

A 38-year-old California man was arrested at President Donald Trump's golf course in Rancho Palos Verdes on illegal weapons charges just two days before the president was scheduled to visit for a Republican National Committee fundraising dinner, according to [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/trump-golf-course-los-angeles-arrest-weapons-a3303c5b86f325b848a3ed828e32e76f).

Jeanine John Taele, of Downey, was taken into custody on Sunday, August 2, after federal agents conducting a security assessment observed him walking throughout the course wearing an earpiece and photographing and filming their security-planning activities. The incident marks the latest in a decade of security threats and close calls surrounding the former president.

## The Arrest

Federal agents first spotted Taele on Friday, July 31, as they prepared for Trump's scheduled visit. He returned to the course on Sunday and approached agents, claiming he was employed by the State Department and was there for a security detail, according to the [U.S. Department of Justice](https://www.justice.gov/usao-cdca/pr/downey-man-who-was-arrested-loaded-pistol-trump-golf-club-charged-federal-complaint).

Taele admitted to having a loaded firearm in his vehicle. A search of his pickup truck in the club parking lot turned up a loaded 9mm pistol with hollow-point ammunition, binoculars, and a badge reading "security protection agent." A 16-round magazine containing hollow-point ammunition was also found in his pants pocket.

A subsequent search of Taele's Downey residence on Monday uncovered an illegally modified AR-platform rifle with a barrel cut down to 10 inches from 14 inches, a 1911 .45-caliber pistol, body armor, high-capacity magazines, bulk ammunition, two radio signal devices, and multiple notebooks containing what authorities described as "concerning statements."

## Federal and State Charges

Taele faces a federal charge of possession of an unregistered short-barreled rifle, a felony carrying a maximum sentence of 10 years in federal prison. He also faces state charges including second-degree robbery for an unrelated incident in El Segundo, receiving a large-capacity magazine, and possession of a short-barreled rifle or shotgun.

He pleaded not guilty to all charges on Monday. A Los Angeles County judge set bail at $250,000, ordered him not to possess weapons, barred him from Trump National Golf Club, and prohibited him from leaving California while the case is pending. His federal court hearing on Wednesday was cancelled, and he remains in state custody.

"Mr. Taele's troubling behavior at the President's public golf course merely days before the President was expected to arrive raised serious red flags for law enforcement," said Patrick Grandy, Assistant Director in Charge of the FBI's Los Angeles Field Office. "There is no room for error, particularly in light of previous attempts on President Trump's life."

## A Decade of Threats

The arrest adds to a troubling pattern of security incidents targeting Trump that spans his entire political career, as documented by [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/us/trumps-decade-assassination-threats-butler-shooting-latest-golf-club-breach). From a British national attempting to grab a police officer's gun at a 2016 Las Vegas campaign rally, to a man who drove a stolen forklift toward Trump's motorcade route in North Dakota in 2017, to a ricin-laced letter mailed to the White House in 2020, the threats have been persistent.

The most serious incidents include the July 2024 Butler, Pennsylvania rally shooting, where a gunman wounded Trump in the right ear and killed one attendee, and the September 2024 incident at Trump's West Palm Beach golf course, where a Secret Service agent spotted a rifle-wielding suspect hiding in brush. More recently, in April 2026, an armed man rushed a security checkpoint at the White House Correspondents' Dinner, striking a Secret Service officer in the chest before being apprehended.

## Suspect's Background

Taele is a former U.S. Marine corporal who served from 2008 to 2016, with deployments to Iraq in 2008 and Afghanistan in 2010. His final duty assignment was with the 1st Reconnaissance Battalion at Camp Pendleton. State records show he was a licensed security guard, though several of his permits to carry a firearm as a security guard were cancelled or suspended, according to [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/05/man-arrested-trump-california-golf-course).

He was also wanted by the El Segundo Police Department in connection with a robbery investigation, which was discovered when he was booked.

## Investigation Ongoing

Authorities say the investigation remains active. "There's a lot of what ifs at this point," said Captain Calvin Mah of the Los Angeles Sheriff's Department Major Crimes Bureau. "We have to do some scrubbing of backgrounds and areas we might have access to to see if there are additional weapons."

The Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department said there is currently "no credible threat" to the surrounding community. Federal and local law enforcement agencies, including the FBI, Secret Service, ATF, and the Joint Terrorism Task Force, are working together on the case.

Trump, who visited the golf club on Tuesday for the RNC dinner, addressed the arrest in remarks to Fox News, saying, "Only consequential presidents are targeted, and I am definitely consequential," which he said brought him "a little solace."

As the investigation unfolds, questions remain about Taele's motives, the contents of the notebooks, and whether additional weapons or evidence may be uncovered. The case underscores the ongoing security challenges facing the president and the agencies tasked with protecting him.
