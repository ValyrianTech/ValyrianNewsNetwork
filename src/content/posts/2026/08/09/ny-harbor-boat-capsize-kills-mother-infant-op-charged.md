# NY Harbor Boat Capsize Kills Mother, Infant; Op. Charged

The operator of a boat that capsized in New York Harbor near Liberty Island on Saturday night, killing a 27-year-old woman and her 5-month-old daughter, has been charged with 13 counts of reckless endangerment, according to [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/new-york-harbor-baby-woman-capsized-boat-226cd727f7211f8a292b0a4ea1001fe4).

The 22-foot Bayliner speedboat overturned around 10:25 p.m. Saturday near Liberty Island, home to the Statue of Liberty, the U.S. Coast Guard said. Twelve people were rescued from the water before police arrived, and police divers later located the woman and infant in the water. Both were transported to a hospital where they were pronounced dead.

## Victims Identified

The victims have been identified as Sara Sanchez, 27, and her 5-month-old daughter Antonella Garcia, both from College Point, Queens, according to [NY Post](https://nypost.com/2026/08/09/us-news/tragic-new-york-harbor-boat-crash-victims-idd-as-mom-and-her-5-month-old-infant-with-babys-stricken-dad-also-on-board/). Sanchez's boyfriend, who was the baby's father, was also on board at the time of the tragedy and survived.

The boat was dangerously overloaded with 14 people on board, according to NYPD sources cited by the [NY Post](https://nypost.com/2026/08/09/us-news/captain-hit-with-slew-of-charges-after-boat-capsized-near-liberty-island-killing-2-including-infant/). The vessel, described as a bowrider with front seating, has a typical capacity of 10-12 people according to online sites, though one source said it should not have been carrying more than five passengers given the location.

## Operator Arrested

Manuel Hernandez, 46, of Manhattan, was arrested around 2:30 a.m. ET on Sunday, about four hours after the capsizing, according to [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/US/2-dead-12-injured-after-boat-capsizes-new/story?id=135496017). His last known address was a Midtown Manhattan condo building.

The NYPD confirmed Hernandez was operating the boat at the time of the incident, according to [North Jersey Media Group](https://www.northjersey.com/story/news/crime/2026/08/09/two-bodies-found-in-ny-bay-after-boat-capsizes-near-liberty-island/91233495007/). Additional charges are expected to be filed in connection with the deaths.

## Coast Guard Investigating Possible Illegal Charter

The U.S. Coast Guard announced it is investigating whether the boat was operating as an illegal charter operation — defined as any passenger-for-hire vessel operation that lacks Coast Guard credentials, safety equipment, and inspection certificates, as reported by [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/nation/2026/08/09/boat-capsizes-new-york-harbor/91232980007/).

The Coast Guard, in coordination with the Coast Guard Investigative Service (CGIS) and the NYPD, is conducting an active marine casualty investigation into the circumstances and cause of the fatal capsizing.

"We are deeply saddened by the tragic loss of life last night, and our thoughts are with the victims' family and loved ones during this incredibly difficult time," said Captain Doreen McCarthy, the Coast Guard Sector New York Commander, as quoted by [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/09/man-charged-new-york-harbor-boat-capsized-deaths). "We are extremely grateful for the heroic, swift actions of our partner agencies and Good Samaritan vessel who assisted in rescuing survivors from the water."

## Rescue Operation

Multiple agencies responded to the scene, including the NYPD Harbor Unit, SCUBA Team and Aviation, as well as FDNY and U.S. Coast Guard vessels, according to [ABC7 New York](https://abc7ny.com/post/crews-responding-vessel-overturns-off-liberty-island-sources-say/19649559/). The NYPD's Harbor Unit, SCUBA Team and Aviation responded to the scene after receiving a 911 call reporting an overturned vessel in the vicinity of Liberty Island.

The 12 surviving passengers were rescued from the water and transported to area hospitals with minor injuries. They were reported in stable condition, according to [News 12 Long Island](https://longisland.news12.com/2026/08/09/woman-5-month-old-baby-dead-after-boat-capsizes-near-statue-of-liberty/5d5XVmninJtE4kjjt0dnYk).

## Broader Concerns About Harbor Safety

One unnamed law enforcement source quoted by the NY Post described recreational boating in New York Harbor as "the Wild West," citing uncharted debris, rip rap, bridge abutments, anchorage chains, commercial shipping channels, and marine plane traffic as hazards.

The incident is among several recent boating tragedies in U.S. waters. AP News noted a search was suspended for three missing people after a boat carrying a family spreading ashes capsized on San Francisco Bay, and a July 4 boat trip death investigation is ongoing in the Deep South.

## What's Next

The overturned vessel was lifted out of the water by a U.S. Army Corps of Engineers crane vessel at the NYPD Harbor Unit base in Brooklyn on Sunday morning. The Coast Guard and NYPD continue their investigation into what caused the boat to capsize.

Hernandez faces 13 counts of reckless endangerment, with additional charges potentially forthcoming related to the deaths of the mother and child. The case has not yet been entered into online court records, and no phone number for Hernandez could be found, according to AP News.

As the investigation unfolds, authorities are expected to determine whether the vessel was operating illegally as a charter service and whether additional safety violations contributed to the tragedy.
