# Gansu Province Announces 18 Cadre Appointments in Public Notice

The Organization Department of the Gansu Provincial Committee of the Chinese Communist Party has issued a public notice announcing the proposed appointment or further use of 18 cadres across universities, city governments, state-owned enterprises, and disciplinary oversight positions. The notice, published on August 3, 2026, on the [Gansu Organization Work website](http://www.gszg.gov.cn/20260802/f6dde59bca0b407e851a0a49913e232c/c.html), was also carried by [People's Daily](http://renshi.people.com.cn/n1/2026/0803/c139617-40772754.html) and [Xinhua News Agency's Gansu channel](http://www.gs.xinhuanet.com/20260802/ffcfd251eae445a3a0e683efeefeca08/c.html).

## The Public Notice System

The cadre pre-appointment public notice system is a standard procedure in China's governance framework, mandated by the Regulations on the Selection and Appointment of Party and Government Leading Cadres. Article 41 of these regulations requires that proposed appointments for leadership positions be publicly announced to solicit feedback from the public before formal appointment procedures begin.

The system is designed to expand democratic participation in cadre selection, increase transparency in personnel decisions, and allow public scrutiny of candidates' qualifications and conduct. The notice explicitly states that any issues affecting appointment found in terms of morality, ability, diligence, performance, and integrity can be reported through multiple channels.

## Key Appointments

The notice covers a broad spectrum of positions across Gansu Province, reflecting the comprehensive nature of cadre management in China's party-state system.

### University Leadership

Several appointments target provincial universities and vocational colleges. **Wang Wendong**, currently a Tianshui Municipal Party Committee Standing Committee Member and Head of the Propaganda Department, is proposed for a provincial regular undergraduate university administrative head position. **Zheng Gaojian**, President of Gansu University of Political Science and Law, is proposed for the university's Party Secretary role, while **Shi Yucheng**, Vice President of the same institution, is proposed for a university administrative head position.

### City and Prefecture Government Positions

Two notable appointments target city government leadership. **Wang Yang**, Deputy Party Secretary of Wuwei City, is proposed as a candidate for city government head, and **Li Shengqian**, Jiuquan City Party Committee Standing Member and Suzhou District Party Secretary, is also proposed as a candidate for city government head. Additionally, **Wang Guanglong**, Vice Governor of Linxia Prefecture, is proposed for a city/prefecture Party Committee Standing Committee Member role.

### State-Owned Enterprise Leadership

The notice includes significant changes at provincial enterprises. **Shi Hailong**, President of Gansu Bank Co., Ltd., is proposed for provincial enterprise Party Secretary and Board Chairman, while **Dong Dongyuan**, Vice President of Agricultural Bank of China's Gansu Branch, is also proposed for a provincial enterprise Party Secretary and Board Chairman position.

### Discipline Inspection and Oversight

**Liu Dianlong**, Director of the Provincial Commission for Discipline Inspection General Office, is proposed for a city/prefecture Party Committee Standing Member, Discipline Inspection Commission Secretary, and Supervisory Commission Director candidate role. **Cui Jinyun**, currently Qingyang City's Discipline Inspection Commission Secretary, is proposed for a city/prefecture Party Committee Deputy Secretary position.

## Public Comment Period

The notice establishes a five-working-day public comment period from August 3 to August 7, 2026. During this time, members of the public can report concerns through the official 12380 reporting system, which operates via telephone (0931-12380), SMS, online submission at www.gs12380.gov.cn, or mail to the provincial organization department's address in Lanzhou.

The 12380 system, operated by the Organization Department's Cadre Supervision Division, accepts reports on violations of cadre selection regulations, issues with leading cadres' political, ideological, work style, or integrity, and other concerns about proposed appointments.

## Analysis and Implications

This notice reflects the ongoing process of personnel rotation and renewal within Gansu Province's governance system. Gansu regularly issues these notices - typically monthly or every few months. Previous notices in [March 2026](https://gansu.gansudaily.com.cn/system/2026/03/29/031344955.shtml) listed 27 cadres, while a [May 2026 notice](https://szb.gansudaily.com.cn/gsrb/pc/con/202605/25/c280237.html) listed 18 cadres.

Several patterns emerge from the current notice. The listed cadres range from those born in 1970 to 1982, showing a mix of experienced and younger leaders. Four of the 18 cadres (22.2%) are women, consistent with China's emphasis on promoting women in leadership. All listed cadres hold at least a bachelor's degree, with many holding master's or doctoral degrees.

The breadth of positions covered - from universities to state-owned enterprises to local government - demonstrates the cross-sector mobility that characterizes China's cadre management system. Cadres frequently move between government, universities, state-owned enterprises, and party organizations throughout their careers.

## What to Watch

The public comment period will determine whether any of the listed cadres face objections before formal appointment procedures proceed. After August 7, if no issues affecting appointment are identified, the provincial committee will handle appointment procedures according to relevant regulations. The appointments will likely reshape the political landscape of the affected cities and institutions in the coming months.
