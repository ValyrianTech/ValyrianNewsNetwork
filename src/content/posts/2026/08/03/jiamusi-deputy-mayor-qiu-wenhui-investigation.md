# Jiamusi Deputy Mayor Qiu Wenhui Under Investigation for Disciplinary Violations

The Heilongjiang Provincial Commission for Discipline Inspection announced on August 3 that Qiu Wenhui, Deputy Mayor of Jiamusi City and member of the Municipal Government Party Leadership Group, is under review and investigation for suspected serious disciplinary and legal violations. The announcement, published on the [Central Commission for Discipline Inspection website](https://news.cctv.com/2026/08/03/ARTIfOQLbfxJ4t1nHWuDoOUf260803.shtml), confirms that Qiu is currently undergoing disciplinary review and supervision investigation by the provincial commission.

## Background: A Career in Local Government

Qiu Wenhui, born in August 1975, is a Han Chinese male and a member of the Communist Party of China. He holds a university degree and has built his career in Heilongjiang Province's local government system. Before his appointment to Jiamusi, Qiu served as County Magistrate and later Party Secretary of Qing'an County in Suihua City, a predominantly agricultural county in the province.

At the end of July 2025, Qiu was appointed Deputy Mayor of Jiamusi City. He subsequently also took on the role of Party Secretary of the Party Working Committee of the Heilongjiang Jiamusi National Agricultural High-tech Industry Demonstration Zone, a position focused on agricultural development and rural affairs. According to [Dahe Daily via Tencent News](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260803A0685G00), his responsibilities included agriculture, rural affairs, and the agricultural high-tech zone.

## The Investigation Announcement

The official announcement from the [Heilongjiang Provincial Commission for Discipline Inspection](https://www.hljjjjc.gov.cn/Hljjjjc/html/draftNews_detail.html?publish_id=934cfc9e76d74ac09242fac78f8beb7e) states that Qiu Wenhui is suspected of "serious disciplinary and legal violations" (涉嫌严重违纪违法), the standard formulation used when a Chinese official's investigation reaches a formal stage. The phrase covers both Party discipline violations and potential legal violations, and the dual process of disciplinary review and supervision investigation reflects China's integrated anti-corruption framework.

The announcement was widely reported by Chinese state media on August 3. [Xinhua News](https://www1.xinhuanet.com/20260803/3f10ed2b05f2490bb0b1acabedcdd4bc/c.html) reported from Harbin that the provincial commission issued the notice on the same day. [China News](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/gn/2026/08-03/10671097.shtml) and [Phoenix News Heilongjiang](https://hlj.ifeng.com/c/8vHcewPyii8) also carried the announcement, citing the provincial commission as the source.

## Timeline and Context

The investigation comes just over one year after Qiu Wenhui's appointment as Deputy Mayor of Jiamusi. His relatively short tenure in the city role before facing investigation suggests that the alleged violations may date back to his earlier career in Qing'an County, though no specific allegations have been publicly disclosed at this stage.

In the months leading up to the announcement, Qiu remained active in his official duties. In June 2026, he inspected flood prevention preparations at the Lianjiangkou Farm Irrigation Station and river embankments in Huachuan County, and visited the Jiamusi National Agricultural High-tech Zone to review test base construction and safety production. He also participated in municipal government meetings in April 2026.

## Part of a Broader Anti-Corruption Campaign

The investigation of Qiu Wenhui is part of China's sustained nationwide anti-corruption campaign, which has continued under the leadership of the Communist Party. The Heilongjiang Provincial Commission for Discipline Inspection has been particularly active, investigating officials across various sectors including agriculture, health, and even within the disciplinary inspection system itself.

The province has also promoted the "Clean Heilongjiang" (勤廉龙江) campaign initiative, reflecting a broader push for integrity and accountability in government. The investigation of a sitting deputy mayor in Jiamusi signals continued enforcement at the municipal level and underscores the campaign's reach into local government operations.

## Implications and What to Watch

While specific details of the alleged violations have not been made public, the investigation could have implications for Jiamusi's agricultural sector. Qiu's role overseeing the National Agricultural High-tech Industry Demonstration Zone may bring scrutiny to agricultural development projects and land management practices in the region.

As the investigation progresses, observers will be watching for whether formal charges are brought, whether Qiu is expelled from the Party, and whether other officials connected to the case may also face investigation. The case also raises questions about the impact on the Jiamusi National Agricultural High-tech Industry Demonstration Zone, a key development initiative for the city.

The investigation remains in its early stages, and further details are expected to emerge as the disciplinary review and supervision investigation continues. [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103302543.html) noted that the announcement follows the standard procedure for such cases, with the provincial commission leading the investigation.
