# Couple Found Dead in Bruges Home, Cause of Death Unclear

A woman (30) and a man (34) were found dead in their home in the Berkenstraat in Sint-Pieters, a sub-municipality of Bruges, on Monday evening. Emergency services were alerted around 6 PM following a concerning call from the residence, according to [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/08/04/brugge-koppel-dood/). All help arrived too late for the victims.

## A Family Tragedy

The couple, described as parents of four children, were found lifeless in their home in a social housing area of Sint-Pieters. According to [Het Laatste Nieuws](https://www.hln.be/brugge/vrouw-30-en-man-34-ouders-van-vier-kinderen-levenloos-aangetroffen-in-woning-in-brugge-een-van-de-kinderen-vond-hen~a6a45c04/), one of the children discovered the parents' bodies and raised the alarm. The four children were taken into care by emergency services, though their current whereabouts remain unknown.

Earlier in the day, the woman's employer had already grown concerned when she failed to show up for work as a household help at a client. The employer visited the family home in the afternoon, where the children opened the door and said their mother was sleeping. The employer left, and it was only hours later that one of the children raised the alarm after their parents did not respond.

## Investigation Underway

The public prosecutor's office has appointed an investigating judge to determine the cause of death. The forensic lab and a forensic doctor were dispatched to the scene on Monday evening for further examination. According to the parket, as reported by [De Krantenkoppen](https://www.dekrantenkoppen.be/detail/3720272/jonge-ouders-34-en-30-levenloos-aangetroffen-in-hun-woning-in-brugge-parket-onderzoekt-overlijdens.html), "An autopsy will take place on both bodies, as well as toxicological testing."

No immediate signs of violence were found at the scene, according to [P-magazine](https://p-magazine.com/nl/articles/koppel-dood-aangetroffen-in-woning-in-brugge-politie-onderzoekt-omstandigheden). The couple was described as being of foreign origin, though their specific country of origin has not been disclosed.

## Community Shock and Support

The news has generated significant public sympathy, with dozens of comments on Belgian news sites expressing condolences and concern for the four children. Many readers have urged that the children be kept together and receive proper psychological support following the traumatic discovery.

## What's Next

The autopsy and toxicological tests are expected to provide more clarity on the cause of death in the coming days. Authorities have not ruled out any possibilities, and the investigation remains ongoing. The children's current placement and long-term care arrangements have not been publicly disclosed.

As the investigation continues, the community of Sint-Pieters mourns the loss of two young parents and rallies around their four orphaned children, who now face an uncertain future following this devastating family tragedy.
