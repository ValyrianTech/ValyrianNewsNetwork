# Falcons' Bijan Robinson Signs Historic $75M Extension, Becoming NFL's Highest-Paid Running Back

The Atlanta Falcons have agreed to a three-year contract extension with star running back Bijan Robinson that locks him up through the 2030 season and makes him the NFL's highest-paid running back, according to [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/falcons-bijan-robinson-contract-de4424501114327b923522ed7e5661c5).

The extension has a base value of $66.75 million with a maximum value of $75 million including incentives. Robinson's average annual value of $22.25 million surpasses Philadelphia Eagles running back Saquon Barkley's $20.6 million, and the deal includes $51 million guaranteed ($37 million at signing) — the most guaranteed money ever given to a running back on a non-rookie contract in NFL history.

## A Market-Setting Deal

Robinson, the No. 8 overall pick in the 2023 NFL Draft out of the University of Texas, has established himself as one of the league's most dynamic offensive weapons since entering the NFL. The deal was first reported by NFL Network's Ian Rapoport and ESPN's Adam Schefter on Tuesday, with Robinson's agent Nicole Lynn of Klutch Sports handling the negotiations.

Robinson reposted the NFL's Instagram post announcing the extension with a message of gratitude: "God this is for you! Blessings. Love this team and this city!"

Falcons head coach Kevin Stefanski expressed enthusiasm about the deal. "Obviously very excited for him, excited for us," Stefanski said. "It's all guys that we believe in, all guys that fit who we are."

## Robinson's Dominant Career

Since entering the NFL in 2023, Robinson has played in all 51 games without missing a single contest. His 5,648 career scrimmage yards are the most by any player at age 23 or younger in NFL history, and he has amassed 1,003 career touches and 34 touchdowns in three seasons.

In 2025, Robinson led the NFL with 2,298 yards from scrimmage and scored 11 touchdowns, finishing fourth in the league with 1,478 rushing yards while adding 820 receiving yards. His 6.3 yards per touch tied with O.J. Simpson's 1975 season and Marshall Faulk's 2001 season as the fifth-best average in league history among players with at least 2,000 scrimmage yards in a single season. He is only the second player ever — joining Steven Jackson in 2006 — to record at least 1,400 rushing yards and 800 receiving yards in the same season, according to [NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/news/falcons-bijan-robinson-contract-extension).

The two-time Pro Bowl selection and First- and Second-Team All-Pro was also ranked as the NFL's top running back by a panel of eight AP Pro Football Writers in a preseason survey.

## Falcons' Offensive Investment Strategy

The Robinson extension continues a broader pattern of the Falcons locking up their young offensive core under first-year president of football operations Matt Ryan and general manager Ian Cunningham. The team has now committed significant resources to key offensive players this offseason, including wide receiver Drake London (four-year, $141.05 million extension in June), tight end Kyle Pitts (three-year, $54 million extension in late June), and left guard Matthew Bergeron (four-year, $96 million extension on August 1), as [CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/nfl/news/falcons-sign-bijan-robinson-historic-three-year-extension/) reported.

This investment strategy comes as the Falcons have struggled to find consistency at quarterback. The team hasn't had a winning season or playoff appearance since 2017, going 8-9 in each of the past two campaigns. Michael Penix Jr., last year's opening-game starter, is coming off a season-ending knee injury and is now competing with Tua Tagovailoa for the starting job.

## The Hold-In and Return to Practice

Robinson's extension came after he spent the first week of training camp as a spectator in what was described as an apparent contract "hold-in," attending practices but not participating. He joined practice on Tuesday after the deal was finalized, with Stefanski noting the team will "slowly get him back" into the fold.

"It means obviously as a coach, you want to get your guys ready for the football season and it allows us to get Bijan ready," Stefanski said. "And he's eager, beyond eager, to get out here."

## Impact on the Running Back Market

The Robinson deal resets the running back market in the NFL and puts immediate pressure on the Detroit Lions to reach an extension with Jahmyr Gibbs, Robinson's 2023 draft classmate who is also holding in at training camp. Lions head coach Dan Campbell acknowledged the impact of Robinson's deal on Gibbs' negotiations.

"It's certainly not going to hurt," Campbell said, via the [Detroit News](https://www.detroitnews.com/story/sports/nfl/lions/2026/08/04/dan-campbell-hopes-bijan-robinson-deal-hastens-jahmyr-gibbs-extension/91162390007/). "But we'll see. Look, I'm anxious to get Gibbs back out there."

Gibbs, who has posted 49 career touchdowns — the most in the first three seasons by any player in NFL history — has said only "time will tell" regarding his own contract situation.

## What's Next

With Robinson secured through 2030, the Falcons have locked in one of the NFL's most explosive offensive playmakers for the long term. The team will now focus on resolving its quarterback situation and building on the foundation of its young offensive core as it aims for its first winning season since 2017. Robinson, for his part, remains focused on improvement.

"Last year was a cool year," Robinson said, "but this year I want to be even better in all aspects."

As the Falcons continue their training camp in Flowery Branch, Georgia, the newly-extended star back will be ramped up gradually ahead of the 2026 regular season — with the security of knowing he's in Atlanta for the long haul.
