# Antwerp Pride 2026: Everything You Need to Know About the Parade and Safety

Antwerp Pride 2026 kicks off Wednesday, 5 August, and runs through Sunday, 9 August, bringing a week of celebration, protest, and community to Belgium's second-largest city. With the theme 'Fearless' taking on renewed urgency following the deadly attack on Berlin Pride last month, organizers have released the full program and comprehensive safety measures for what promises to be the city's most significant Pride edition yet.

## A Theme Born of Anxiety and Defiance

The 'Fearless' theme was chosen after community consultations in October 2025 revealed growing anxiety about the rise of far-right extremism in Europe and the influence of the manosphere on social media, according to [Gaylive.be](https://www.gaylive.be/articles/antwerp-pride-angst-mag-ons-niet-verlammen_18963.html). "'Fearless' doesn't mean we feel no fear," said Antwerp Pride chairman Geert van Praet. "It means we refuse to let that fear guide us. That we remain visible, even when it becomes difficult."

The theme has taken on added significance after a van drove into crowds at CSD Berlin Pride on 25 July, killing one person and injuring 29. The 21-year-old suspect, from the "Islamist milieu," was shot dead by police the following day, as [VRT NWS reported](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/07/25/auto-rijdt-in-op-menigte-pride-duitsland/).

## The Pride Parade: Saturday's Centerpiece

The Pride Parade remains the beating heart of Antwerp Pride. On Saturday, 8 August, participants will gather at Brouwersvliet from 14:00, with the parade following the Scheldekaaien southward before turning through the Kloosterstraat and ending at the Ledeganckkaai for the Love United Festival, according to the [official Antwerp Pride website](https://antwerppride.com/calendar/antwerp-pride-parade). The parade will be broadcast live in collaboration with Proximus.

Last year's parade drew a record 160,000 attendees, and organizers expect similarly large crowds this year. The festival recommends arriving on foot or by bicycle, with P+R parking advised for those driving from outside the city.

## Safety Measures: A Priority After Berlin

Safety is a top priority this year, especially following the Berlin attack and a foiled terror plot against Leuven Pride in October 2024, when an 18-year-old was arrested for planning an attack on the event, as [Gaylive.be reported](https://gaylive.be/articles/twintiger-zocht-wapens-voor-aanslag-op-pride-in-leuven_19035.html).

Premier Bart De Wever and Antwerp Mayor Els van Doesburg have both emphasized that Antwerp Pride is a safe event and that attendees should not be intimidated. The [official safety page](https://antwerppride.com/veiligheid) details comprehensive measures including:

- Police presence in both uniform and plainclothes, including a diversity team specializing in hate crimes
- First aid posts and info points throughout the route and festival grounds
- Free water points along the parade route
- A new 'Change Station' at Antwerpen-Centraal station on Saturday from 11:00-22:00, offering a safe space to prepare before the festivities
- A behavior code for all attendees
- Emergency number 112 for urgent situations

## Program Highlights

The week's program is packed with events across the city. The opening takes place Wednesday at OLT Rivierenhof in Deurne with performances by TRINXX, Leonard Santos, Sonny Queen, a Lady Gaga tribute by Donna Maria, and 'Les Copine.' Thursday features a business network event at DPG Media and 'Muziek aan het Water' with Clouseau and Laura Tesoro.

Friday brings 'Queer Families in Town' with headliners Mega Mindy and Bart Kaëll, followed by 'Pride in Town' at Sint-Andriesplaats featuring city poet Esohe Weyden, author Fleur Pierets, and the Antwerp Queer Choir, as [HLN detailed](https://www.hln.be/antwerpen/bart-kaell-2-fabiola-en-mega-mindy-antwerp-pride-maakt-volledig-programma-bekend~a2fd6bd9/).

Saturday's Love United Festival at Cockerillkaai (from 15:00, free entry) features DJs Dimitri Wouters, Karakals, 5napback, and Crazy Sir G, with drag shows by Leroy and Sahra, per the [Love United Festival page](https://antwerppride.com/calendar/love-united). Sunday's Closing Festival brings 2 Fabiola, The Juliets, Showball by Sederginne, and a Rihanna tribute by Rose Cohen.

## Community Response and Broader Context

In the wake of the Berlin attack, çavaria, the Flemish LGBTQI+ umbrella organization, has demanded a decisive approach to hate, extremism, and violence. "A Pride is a celebration and a protest," said spokesperson Thomas Jans. "But for many people, it's also one of the few moments where they can fully be themselves." The organization is calling for strengthened police response, amendment of Article 150 of the Constitution, strict enforcement of EU social media regulations, and mandatory education on gender and sexual diversity, as [Gaylive.be reported](https://gaylive.be/articles/cavaria-eist-kordate-aanpak-van-haat-na-aanslag-in-berlijn-en-dreiging-in-leuven_19036.html).

The safety concerns extend beyond Belgium's borders. Amsterdam's WorldPride, which takes place this weekend, has also announced extra security measures following the Berlin attack, with over 40 'safer spaces' established across the city, according to [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/07/31/worldpride-amsterdam/).

## What to Watch For

Antwerp Pride needs more than 800 volunteers to make the week run smoothly. Organizers are urging anyone who wants to contribute to sign up through the official website. With the parade broadcast live, a record-breaking crowd expected, and safety measures at their most comprehensive, this year's Pride promises to be both a celebration and a powerful statement of resilience.

As the vision text for the 'Fearless' theme puts it: "Antwerp Pride is about being visible, but above all about daring to be." The city is ready to show that fear will not win.
