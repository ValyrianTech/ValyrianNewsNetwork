# Adam Driver to Play Mister Sinister in Marvel's X-Men Reboot

ANAHEIM, Calif. — Marvel Studios unveiled the long-awaited cast for its upcoming X-Men reboot at D23: The Ultimate Disney Fan Event on Friday, confirming that Adam Driver will portray the villainous Mister Sinister and that breakout star Inde Navarrette will play Rogue. The announcement, made by Marvel Studios President Kevin Feige before a crowd of 12,000 at the Honda Center arena, marks a major milestone for the first standalone X-Men film since Disney acquired Fox's film studio in 2019, according to [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/d23-xmen-driver-navarette-star-wars-frozen-fb8c8adcc00cf19fe3fa88696cd47157).

## A Star-Studded Mutant Lineup

The full cast assembled on stage at the Anaheim event, with Driver appearing via video screen while his fellow cast members took the stage in person. Alongside Driver and Navarrette, the newly confirmed roster includes Christopher Abbott as Professor Charles Xavier, Maya Boyd as Storm, and previously announced cast members Samara Weaving as Emma Frost, Kit Connor as Cyclops, and Sadie Sink as Jean Grey — whose role was first revealed through her appearance in this summer's blockbuster "Spider-Man: Brand New Day."

Driver's casting had been the subject of intense speculation, with many fans predicting he would take on the role of Magneto. In a playful moment at D23, Driver teased the crowd before revealing his actual role. "Kevin and I had been talking for years about my joining the Marvel Cinematic Universe, and I think we finally found the perfect film," Driver told the audience via video. "So I'm very excited to be playing the role of Maaagneto!" — before pausing and correcting himself: "even better, Nathaniel Millbury," an alias for Mister Sinister.

Navarrette, who became one of Hollywood's most sought-after actors following her striking performance in the indie horror hit "Obsession" earlier this year, had also been heavily rumored for a role in the film. The casting was confirmed by [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/x-men-cast-inde-navarrette-rogue-christopher-abbott-prof-x-adam-driver-mr-sinister-1236817698/), which noted that earlier speculation about Cailee Spaeny playing Rogue had been thoroughly debunked.

## The Road to the MCU's X-Men

The X-Men joining the Marvel Cinematic Universe became possible with Disney's $71.3 billion acquisition of 21st Century Fox's film and television assets in 2019. Since then, the mutants have been slowly integrated into the MCU through cameos and crossovers, including Patrick Stewart's appearance as Professor X in "Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness" and Kelsey Grammer's cameo as Beast in "The Marvels."

The upcoming reboot, directed by Jake Schreier ("Thunderbolts*"), is scheduled for release on May 5, 2028, as [confirmed by Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/x-men-release-date-marvel-reboot-1236816959/). The script is being written by "Beef" creator Lee Sung Jin and "The Bear" co-showrunner Joanna Calo, bringing a prestige television pedigree to the project.

According to [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/08/x-men-christopher-abbott-adam-driver-1237012957/), the casting process has been one of the most active in Hollywood this summer, with director Schreier and Marvel executives meeting and auditioning actors throughout the season, including a final round of tests after the Fourth of July holiday.

## A New Era for the X-Men

The film will be the first standalone X-Men movie since Fox's "Dark Phoenix" (2019) and "The New Mutants" (2020), which marked the end of the Fox era. The original Fox franchise, which began in 2000 with Patrick Stewart as Professor X and Hugh Jackman as Wolverine, became one of the most successful superhero franchises in cinema history before being rebooted with prequel films in the 2010s.

Feige noted that the new cast members met each other for the very first time backstage at the D23 event, underscoring the fresh start for the franchise. "They've just met backstage for the very first time," Feige said.

The X-Men's arrival in the MCU comes at a pivotal moment for Marvel Studios. "Avengers: Doomsday," set for release on December 18, will feature original X-Men cast members including Patrick Stewart, Ian McKellen, and Kelsey Grammer, while the animated series "X-Men '97" on Disney+ has been a critical and commercial success.

## What's Next

With the core cast now confirmed and a release date set for May 2028, Marvel Studios appears to be moving forward at full speed on the X-Men reboot. The film represents one of the most significant projects in the MCU's next phase, bringing one of Marvel's most beloved franchises fully into the interconnected universe.

Fans can expect more casting announcements and production updates in the months ahead as the project moves toward production. The X-Men's journey from Fox's long-running franchise to the MCU's interconnected universe marks a new chapter for these iconic characters — and with a cast featuring both established stars and rising talents, the reboot promises to bring a fresh take to the mutant team.

For now, the announcement at D23 has given fans plenty to anticipate as Marvel Studios builds toward what could be one of its most ambitious films yet.
