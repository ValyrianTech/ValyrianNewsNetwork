# 5 Shot at Virginia State University, Police Seek Suspects

Five people were wounded in a shooting involving multiple suspects at Virginia State University early Saturday, prompting a campus-wide lockdown and a multi-agency police investigation just days before the fall semester is set to begin. Officers responded to the 3300 block of Boisseau Street just before 1:30 a.m., where they discovered five people suffering from gunshot wounds outside campus dormitories, according to [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/virginia-state-university-shooting-campus-lockdown-783d5190d7fe0818fc27a98123311dc3).

## The Incident

The shooting occurred near the university's Quad Annexes residence halls in Ettrick, Virginia, about 24 miles south of Richmond. All five victims were transported to nearby hospitals. One victim, initially listed as having life-threatening injuries, was later upgraded to critical condition, while the other four sustained non-life-threatening injuries, county police said.

The university confirmed at least one victim was a student who was treated and released from the hospital. Investigators believe other victims may be from surrounding communities, Lt. James R. Lamb of the Chesterfield County Police Department's Criminal Investigations Major Crimes Unit told [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/shooting-virginia-state-university/). Lamb also confirmed the incident was not a drive-by shooting.

## Campus Lockdown and Investigation

The campus was placed on lockdown shortly after the shooting. VSU Police Chief David Bragg announced around 8:20 a.m. that the lockdown had been lifted and normal access to campus could resume. "Although the lockdown has been lifted, members of the VSU community are asked to remain mindful of the ongoing law enforcement activity and avoid the immediate area where investigators are processing the scene," Bragg said, as reported by [WDBJ7](https://www.wdbj7.com/2026/08/15/multiple-people-shot-virginia-state-university-campus-police-say/).

The Chesterfield County Police Department is leading the investigation, with assistance from VSU Police, the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives (ATF), and the Hanover County Sheriff's Office. As of the latest updates, no arrests had been made and no suspect information had been released.

"A significant law enforcement presence remains on campus as investigators process the scene," Lamb said, according to [WTVR CBS 6](https://www.wtvr.com/news/local-news/chesterfield-county/virginia-state-university-shooting-prompts-lockdown-aug-15-2026).

## Community Impact

The shooting comes at a particularly sensitive moment for the university community. Virginia State University, a historically Black institution with approximately 5,700 students, had just welcomed nearly 1,500 new students with a special pinning ceremony on Friday featuring NFL MVP Cam Newton as guest speaker, [WTVR reported](https://www.wtvr.com/news/local-news/chesterfield-county/vsu-pinning-ceremony-cam-newton-aug-14-2026). Residence halls opened for students a week ago, and classes are set to begin Monday.

Arthur Fridrich, the university's former director of distance education who lives near campus, expressed the community's heartbreak. "To start a new year with this event is very tragic. It casts a shadow in the minds of all students, and for some it will be difficult to rid their minds of this term," Fridrich told [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/15/multiple-people-injured-virginia-state-university-shooting). "For those that were shot, my heart bleeds. I hope that they all mend and somehow do not let this event define their lives."

## Broader Context of Campus Gun Violence

The shooting adds to a troubling pattern of gun violence at U.S. higher education institutions. The Gun Violence Archive, a national tracker, recorded 100 incidents where individuals were wounded or killed in shootings at higher education institutions in 2025, and 75 shootings in 2024, [Al Jazeera noted](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/15/five-wounded-in-shooting-at-virginia-state). As of August 15, there had been nearly 300 mass shootings reported across the U.S. so far this year.

Virginia has experienced notable campus shootings in recent memory, including the 2007 Virginia Tech shooting that killed 32 people and wounded 17, and an incident earlier this year when a gunman opened fire at an ROTC group at Old Dominion University, killing an instructor and wounding two others.

## What's Next

Police have not released information about suspects or a possible motive, and no arrests have been made. The university community is now preparing to process the shooting as students return to campus for the start of the academic year on Monday. Authorities are urging anyone with information to contact Chesterfield County Police at 804-748-1251, Crime Solvers at 804-748-0660, or VSU Police at 804-524-5411.

As the investigation continues, the focus remains on the recovery of the five victims and the safety of the campus community during what should have been a time of celebration and new beginnings.
