# Chengdu Auto Show Opens with Record 58 New Car Debuts

The 29th Chengdu International Auto Show officially opened on August 21 at the China Western International Expo City, bringing together nearly 120 domestic and international mainstream auto brands and approximately 1,600 vehicles across a 220,000-square-meter exhibition area. As the first A-level auto show in China's second half of 2026, the event features 58 new car debuts, pre-sales, and launches, according to [Xinhua News](https://www.news.cn/photo/20260821/af1f48ce4e854ee08c230f5ed0ab50e3/c.html).

## A Barometer for China's Auto Market

The show arrives at a pivotal moment for China's automotive industry. Data from the China Association of Automobile Manufacturers shows that auto production and sales in January-July 2026 reached 17.567 million and 17.602 million units respectively, both down 3.7% year-on-year. As the first major industry gathering of the second half, the Chengdu show serves as a critical barometer for market performance ahead of the traditional "Golden September, Silver October" sales season.

The 2025 edition of the show generated 33,745 orders worth 5.753 billion yuan, with 910,200 visitors, underscoring the event's commercial significance. This year, the emphasis on sales is even more pronounced. As [Time Weekly](https://time-weekly.com/post/332139) reported, one industry insider observed, "It feels like everyone is here to sell cars this time," reflecting a broader shift from showcase to sales venue.

## Domestic Giants Dominate with Full-Hall Presence

Chinese automakers have taken center stage at this year's show, with BYD and Chery each securing entire exhibition halls. BYD's Hall 9 features the global debut of the third-generation Tang SUV, a large five-seat technology flagship with an 850km range and second-generation blade battery. The company also unveiled its D+ class flagship sedan, the BYD Han EV, which opened pre-sales at 249,900-299,900 yuan. According to [TOM Automotive](https://car.tom.com/202608/1039622963.html), the Han EV boasts a 1,008km CLTC pure electric range, a 0.197 drag coefficient, and 5-minute fast charging from 10% to 70%, positioning it as a direct challenge to established luxury sedans.

Chery's Hall 5 showcases the global debut of the new Arrizo 7, the pre-order launch of the QQ3 EV Modern Edition, and the iCAR V25 debut. The Zongheng G700 Moab Edition also launched at 389,900 yuan. Great Wall Motors occupies Hall 1 with the H10 delivery launch, the new Tank 300 family, and GWM Cannon pickup launches.

## Luxury Brands Respond with China-Specific Models

Traditional luxury automakers are fighting back with models tailored specifically for the Chinese market. BMW opened pre-sales for its new-generation iX3 long wheelbase version at 269,900-339,900 yuan. The China-exclusive model features a 3-meter wheelbase, 800V architecture, and a CLTC range exceeding 1,000km. As [ChinaEVHome](https://chinaevhome.com/2026/08/19/bmw-ix3-posts-1030-km-extreme-range-ahead-of-chengdu-auto-show/) reported, the iX3 completed a 1,030.2km internal road range test ahead of the show, setting a new record for BMW's battery-electric lineup. The move comes as BMW Group sales in China fell 20.4% year-on-year to 262,000 vehicles in H1 2026, highlighting the mounting pressure on traditional luxury brands.

Mercedes-Benz unveiled the new long-wheelbase GLE SUV in its global debut, with body length extended to 5,051mm and wheelbase to 3,115mm. The model integrates the Momenta R7 world model and Doubao AI assistant, with a September launch scheduled. Audi also launched the E7X Pioneer quattro Launch Edition at 309,800 yuan.

## HarmonyOS Intelligent Mobility Showcases Full Lineup

HarmonyOS Intelligent Mobility brought its complete product family to the show, featuring the AITO M9 Ultimate extended flagship SUV, the Stelato V8 family smart flagship MPV with a 5,335mm length and 3,250mm wheelbase, the Luxeed RX coupe SUV with innovative dual-eye rearview mirrors, and the Stelato G9 hardcore off-road SUV. The comprehensive display underscores the growing influence of Huawei's automotive ecosystem in China's smart vehicle market.

## A Flying Car Steals the Spotlight

One of the most talked-about exhibits came from ARIDGE (formerly XPENG AeroHT), which brought its Land Aircraft Carrier - a six-wheel truck carrying a folding two-seat eVTOL aircraft. As [Tarantas News](https://tarantas.news/en/posts/id46263-nobody-expected-a-flying-car-in-chengdu-aridge-just-brought-one-anyway) reported, the ground module features 6x6 drive, rear-steering wheels, and an 800V silicon-carbide platform with a gasoline range extender, claiming a range of over 1,000km. The aircraft itself seats two and stays aloft on six rotors, with the ground module doubling as a charging station.

## Sales-Focused Atmosphere and Celebrity Endorsements

The commercial intensity of this year's show is unmistakable. Live-streaming hosts broadcast from nearly every major booth, with sales teams working the exhibition floor to convert visitors into customers. At the Voyah booth, actress Madina placed an on-site order for the ZhuiGuang S, declaring that "Chinese cars are not inferior to any imported cars." According to [Cover News](https://www.163.com/dy/article/L4U3VHF40514D3UH.html), the ZhuiGuang S has accumulated 11,656 orders in just five days since its August 15 launch, with the model featuring Huawei Qiankun four-laser radar and HarmonyOS cockpit 5.2.

SAIC MG's MG 07 launch set a new record, breaking the MG4's benchmark with over 10,000 orders in just 1 minute and 57 seconds, at a starting price of 105,900 yuan. The [ITHome coverage](https://www.ithome.com/0/992/714.htm) of the Zeekr booth highlighted multiple model showcases, including the Zeekr 001 5th Anniversary Edition and a children's concept car, alongside a mysterious "robot" that turned out to be a human cosplayer.

## Implications for the Industry

The Chengdu Auto Show's transformation from a showcase to a sales battleground reflects deeper structural changes in China's auto industry. The dominance of new energy vehicles among the 58 debuts signals that the NEV revolution has become the industry's primary competitive arena. The aggressive pricing strategies, rapid product cycles, and emphasis on intelligent driving technologies - from Huawei's Qiankun ADAS to Momenta's world models - demonstrate how Chinese automakers are redefining the competitive landscape.

For international brands, the show highlights the challenges of competing in the world's largest auto market. BMW's 20.4% sales decline and the aggressive localization strategies of luxury brands underscore the pressure to adapt. The show's success in generating orders will be a key indicator of whether China's auto market can reverse its 3.7% decline in the second half of 2026.

## What to Watch

As the show continues through August 30, industry observers will be watching several key indicators: order volumes across major brands, the reception of new energy vehicle debuts, and the effectiveness of the sales-focused approach in converting show visitors into customers. The performance of the Chengdu show will provide crucial signals for the industry's trajectory through the critical "Golden September, Silver October" sales season and beyond.
