# China's Education Ministry Unveils Plans for 42nd Teachers' Day Celebrations

China's Ministry of Education has issued a formal notice outlining comprehensive plans for the nation's 42nd Teachers' Day, scheduled for September 10, 2026. The announcement, published on the ministry's official website on August 21, establishes this year's theme as "Vigorously Promote the Spirit of Educators, Jointly Build a Culture of Respecting Teachers and Valuing Education," according to [Xinhua News](https://education.news.cn/20260821/35a2d2b5359d45d787a2af8bc7b60c19/c.html).

## A Continuing Policy Focus on the Teaching Profession

The notice, dated August 18 and issued under document number Teacher's Office Letter [2026] No. 6, is addressed to provincial education departments, the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps Education Bureau, and all universities directly under the ministry or jointly established with provinces. It outlines four primary areas of work: promoting exemplary teachers, fostering respect for the profession, launching measures to strengthen teacher development, and optimizing services to benefit educators.

The theme continues the "spirit of educators" framework first introduced by President Xi Jinping in September 2023, when he sent a letter to the National Conference of Outstanding Teacher Representatives. The concept, which encompasses ideals of patriotic dedication, moral integrity, and innovative teaching practice, has been the central focus of Teachers' Day celebrations since its introduction.

## New Institutional Initiatives

Among the most significant announcements is the planned launch of a **National Action for Teacher Development** and the establishment of a **National Academy for Outstanding Teachers**, initiatives designed to accelerate teacher training reform. The ministry will also deepen reforms to the National Training Plan, upgrade the national teacher management information system, and enhance the teacher development center on the national smart education platform, as detailed in the [official notice](http://www.moe.gov.cn/srcsite/A10/s7002/202608/t20260821_1447456.html).

According to [Guangming Daily](https://news.gmw.cn/2026-08/22/content_38957391.htm), the ministry will produce a special documentary titled "Xi Jinping's Care for Teachers" showcasing the president's commitment to the profession. Additional media initiatives include a national "Most Beautiful Teacher" selection campaign, a "Telling My Education Story" national showcase, and the fourth batch of "Huang Danian-style Teacher Teams" at universities. A teacher-themed film, "Growing Children," is also scheduled for release.

## Welfare, Rights, and Legal Protections

The notice introduces a **"Teacher Benefits Package"** developed in collaboration with social resources, offering exclusive discounts on transportation, cultural enrichment, and teaching materials. The ministry will also strengthen the legal protection of teachers' rights, pledging to seriously address acts that interfere with normal teaching order, insult or harm teachers, and spread rumors about educators, according to [China Education Daily](https://edu.youth.cn/wzlb/202608/t20260822_16828235.htm).

Efforts to improve teacher welfare include refining the performance-based salary distribution mechanism for compulsory education schools and ensuring salary commitments are fulfilled. The ministry will also optimize its "China Teacher" mini-program platform to provide psychological support resources, including educational videos and mental health guidance manuals.

## Anti-Formalism Emphasis

The notice places particular emphasis on avoiding formalism and waste in celebration activities. Local education departments and schools are instructed to organize events following principles of practicality, simplicity, and warmth, while strictly adhering to requirements for reducing burdens at the grassroots level. The directive aims to ensure that teachers can "teach with peace of mind, enthusiasm, comfort, and tranquility," as stated in the notice.

## Historical Context and Forward Look

Teachers' Day has been celebrated annually on September 10 since its establishment in 1985, reflecting China's deep-rooted cultural tradition of respecting teachers and valuing education. This year's celebration comes amid ongoing efforts to implement the Education Development 15th Five-Year Plan and related policies aimed at strengthening the teaching workforce.

The ministry's emphasis on both institutional development and welfare improvements signals a comprehensive approach to addressing challenges facing the teaching profession, including workload concerns and social status. As [China Education Online](https://www.eol.cn/news/yaowen/202608/t20260821_2766747.shtml) reported, the notice also calls for continued implementation of the National Silver Age Teacher Action Plan, which encourages retired teachers to return to service.

With the celebrations scheduled for September 10, education authorities nationwide are expected to roll out their respective programs in the coming weeks, guided by the ministry's framework for honoring educators and strengthening the profession for the year ahead.
