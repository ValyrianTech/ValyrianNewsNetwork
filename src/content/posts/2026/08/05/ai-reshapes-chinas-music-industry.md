# AI Reshapes China's Music Industry Across Value Chain

Artificial intelligence is no longer just an auxiliary tool for cultural content production in China's music industry—it has become an important technological variable driving the optimization of industry structure and the reshaping of the value chain, according to a [commentary published by Economic Daily](https://www.news.cn/tech/20260805/167c247a2f144c0d8c7ca612ffd4fabd/c.html) and republished by Xinhua News on August 5, 2026.

The analysis comes as China's music industry shows robust growth, with the 2026 China Music Industry Development Report revealing that the sector grew 5.3% year-on-year in 2025, with the core layer industry's share exceeding 30% for the first time. Digital music grew 12% year-on-year, while the music performance market expanded 9.5%, establishing both as primary growth engines.

## Policy Framework Driving AI Integration

The State Council's "Opinions on Deeply Implementing the 'AI+' Action" (国发〔2025〕11号), issued in August 2025, explicitly calls for using artificial intelligence to assist in creating more cultural content with Chinese cultural elements and markers, and to strengthen the cultural industry. The [full text of the policy document](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/202508/content_7037861.htm) outlines a comprehensive strategy for AI integration across six key areas, including people's livelihood and welfare.

The Ministry of Culture and Tourism has also deployed an "AI + Culture and Tourism" application pilot program, which specifically proposes to enhance AI technology's generation capabilities in music, fine arts, and traditional opera, supporting the creation of cultural content with distinctly Chinese characteristics.

## From Single-Point Generation to Full-Chain Empowerment

According to the Economic Daily analysis, AI applications in China's music industry are transitioning from single-point generation to full-chain empowerment, covering creation, production, distribution, consumption, and operations.

In the creation and production segment, AI composition, intelligent arrangement, sound synthesis, and virtual singer technologies are lowering production barriers and increasing content production efficiency. In the distribution and consumption segment, intelligent recommendation, short video background music, live streaming interaction, online karaoke, and immersive performances are expanding the ways audiences engage with music, shifting the experience from single-work appreciation to personalized, interactive, and scenario-based engagement.

At the industry operations level, AI is entering music library management, style recognition, user demand analysis, market prediction, and copyright monitoring—pushing the industry from traffic-driven expansion toward coordinated development driven by content, technology, data, and copyright.

## What AI Stimulates Is Not Just Song Generation

The commentary emphasizes that what artificial intelligence stimulates is not a single "song generation" function, but composite functions of intelligent creation, precise distribution, personalized consumption, scenario transformation, IP incubation, industry collaboration, and copyright governance.

For the music industry, the significance of AI extends beyond improving song generation efficiency. It is about promoting music resources to enter diverse scenarios such as performing arts, cultural tourism, education, brand communication, and public cultural services at lower cost and higher efficiency.

## Three Priorities for Unlocking AI's Potential

The Economic Daily analysis outlines three key priorities for leveraging AI's empowerment effect in the music industry:

**First, consolidate the industry foundation.** This includes improving music work metadata, training data authorization, AI-generated content labeling, algorithm recommendation transparency, and revenue distribution mechanisms—establishing clear rights boundaries and standardized industry order.

**Second, expand application scenarios.** The article calls for expanding AI applications in digital music, performing arts economy, short video live streaming, digital cultural tourism, and public cultural services, promoting the transformation of music resources from single work forms to digital cultural products that can be disseminated, experienced, and consumed.

**Third, strengthen talent development.** The commentary emphasizes cultivating composite talents in music, technology, copyright, and operations, while supporting collaborative innovation among enterprises, universities, platforms, and cultural institutions.

## The Path Forward

"Only by combining technological efficiency, content quality, cultural value, and governance capability can artificial intelligence transform from a tool that can generate to an industry capability that can add value," the commentary concludes, "becoming an important force to activate new momentum in the music industry."

As China's music industry continues its upward trajectory, the integration of AI across the value chain represents both an opportunity and a challenge. The policy framework established by the State Council and the Ministry of Culture and Tourism provides the structural support needed, but success will ultimately depend on how effectively the industry can balance technological innovation with cultural preservation, content quality, and robust governance.

With digital music and live performances already serving as major growth engines, and AI applications expanding across every segment of the industry, China's music sector appears well-positioned to harness the transformative potential of artificial intelligence in the years ahead.
