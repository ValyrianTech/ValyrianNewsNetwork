# China's Military Documentary Finale Reveals AI Systems

China has released the sixth and final episode of its military documentary series "Zhisheng" (Winning), titled "Lianzhan Liansheng" (Joint Operations, Joint Victory), which highlights the People's Liberation Army's decade-long transformation toward integrated joint warfare. The episode, broadcast on August 4-5, showcases AI-enabled strike-planning systems, drone integration, and striking footage of an H-6N bomber carrying a large missile under its fuselage, according to [Xinhua News](https://www.news.cn/politics/20260805/e7c7c85841d94b519d586aecb3e4a13d/c.html).

## A Decade of Joint Operations Reform

The documentary series, produced by the Central Military Commission Political Work Department, the Central Cyberspace Affairs Commission, and China Media Group, was released to mark the PLA's 99th anniversary on August 1. The final episode centers on the joint operations command system established in February 2016, when President Xi Jinping awarded military flags to the five theater commands—a historic step that replaced the previous military region structure.

The episode follows personnel who participated in this transformation, including Chang Gaoxiang of the Southern Theater Command Joint Operations Command Center, who joined the command restructuring a decade ago. The documentary candidly depicts the challenges of the transition, including a first joint exercise where commanders struggled when battlefield situations deviated from planned scenarios, prompting a fundamental rethinking of single-service thinking.

## AI-Enabled Strike Planning System

A centerpiece of the episode is the revelation of an AI-enabled "intelligent strike-planning system" developed by a team led by Senior Colonel Deng Jianping at a PLA Air Force base. As reported by [Global Times](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202608/1367381.shtml), the system supports target prioritization, mission allocation, strike sequencing, and force coordination across more than 100 tactical units.

"The hardest part isn't writing code or building models. It's thoroughly understanding battlefield logic. Targets, firepower, penetration and damage assessment – the entire strike chain," Deng told the documentary, as reported by the [South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/news/china/military/article/3362824/chinese-military-unveils-ai-system-plan-and-coordinate-mass-air-strikes). He added that even a "slight flaw in the logic" could make the difference between victory or defeat.

The system passed its final live-fire test with missiles and bombs successfully striking designated targets, and has since been used in multiple Air Force missions. Chinese military affairs expert Zhang Junshe told Global Times that by deeply integrating AI with battlefield logic, the system can "turn advanced individual weapons into coordinated combat power, marking a shift from calculations performed primarily by personnel to AI-assisted computation and planning."

## H-6N Bomber and JL-1 Missile Footage

Perhaps the most significant visual revelation is footage of two J-20 stealth fighters escorting an H-6N bomber carrying a large missile under its fuselage. According to [Global Times](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202608/1367509.shtml), military experts identify the weapon as likely the JL-1 air-launched long-range ballistic missile, capable of anti-ship or ground-strike missions.

Wang Yanan, editor-in-chief of Aerospace Knowledge magazine, told Global Times that air-launched weapons offer stronger penetration capabilities and greater concealment compared with traditional land-based ballistic missiles. "More critically, the enemy cannot predict the H-6N's launch position, making it extremely difficult to defend against," Wang said. The J-20 escort provides a "stealth protective umbrella" for the non-stealth H-6N at medium to long range.

The H-6N's 3,500 km combat radius combined with the missile's range extends total strike distance to approximately 7,500 km, a capability that analysts note pushes US carrier strike groups further from China's coast.

## Drone Integration and Sea-Air Joint Operations

The episode also highlights the integration of unmanned aerial vehicles into joint operations. Li Zhengwei of the Southern Theater Command Air Force brought joint operations command experience to drone force development, transforming UAVs from single-mission platforms to system-support roles. The documentary shows drones becoming critical nodes for intelligence gathering, situational awareness, and firepower coordination across multiple service branches.

In a naval exercise segment, the Nanning warship successfully intercepted incoming missiles using an integrated intelligent air defense system, demonstrating the sea-air joint operations capability. The episode emphasizes protecting China's "ancestral sea" in the South China Sea, including footage of driving away foreign aircraft and ships.

## Broader Context: PLA Modernization Push

The documentary series arrives amid heightened regional tensions and as the PLA advances toward its 2027 centenary goals. As [Global Times](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202607/1367292.shtml?id=12) reported, President Xi Jinping recently urged efforts to fulfill the goals for the centenary of the PLA and make "decisive progress" in basically realizing the modernization of national defense and the armed forces.

The series, which premiered on July 30, has featured equipment from all major PLA branches—including Type 191 long-range rocket artillery, the Type 076 amphibious assault ship, and new reconnaissance-strike drones. According to analysis from [Army Recognition](https://www.armyrecognition.com/news/aerospace-news/2026/china-unveils-new-ai-assisted-system-for-planning-and-coordinating-large-scale-air-strikes), the AI-assisted system reflects China's broader transition toward networked, intelligent warfare, where battlefield advantage increasingly depends on resilient command-and-control and rapid data processing.

The documentary's candid portrayal of training failures and real-world challenges represents a notable departure from traditional propaganda narratives. As one analysis noted, the series was created to directly respond to public skepticism about whether the PLA, which has not engaged in large-scale combat for decades, can still fight and win wars.

## What to Watch For

As the PLA approaches its centenary in 2027, the capabilities showcased in the documentary finale signal an accelerating shift toward AI-enabled, system-of-systems warfare. Military experts suggest the intelligent strike-planning system could significantly compress the sensor-to-strike cycle, potentially reshaping regional military calculations. The extended strike range demonstrated by the H-6N and JL-1 combination pushes US carriers further back from China's coast, with implications for Indo-Pacific security dynamics.

The documentary's message is clear: future confrontations with powerful adversaries will not be decided by equipment alone, but by the system-of-systems confrontation supported by joint operations personnel. As the narration concludes, "Ten years of joint operations path, step by step toward victory. Joint operations, joint victory, mission accomplished!"
