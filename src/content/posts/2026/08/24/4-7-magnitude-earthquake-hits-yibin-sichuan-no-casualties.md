# 4.7-Magnitude Earthquake Hits Yibin, Sichuan; No Casualties

A 4.7-magnitude earthquake struck Changning County in Yibin, Sichuan Province, at 08:26 Beijing time on August 24, 2026, according to the China Earthquake Networks Center. The epicenter was located at 28.33°N, 104.98°E, with a focal depth of 5 kilometers, as [Xinhua News](https://www.news.cn/politics/20260824/a3ebd5f6c72a45aead7412c1fae3e5af/c.html) reported. No casualties have been reported so far, and authorities are actively assessing the situation.

## Emergency Response Activated

Following the earthquake, both the Sichuan Earthquake Agency and the China Earthquake Administration activated Level IV emergency responses, as [CCTV News](https://news.cctv.com/2026/08/24/ARTIKlM3JMr8jgPyelN9MlIM260824.shtml) confirmed. The Sichuan Earthquake Agency dispatched a team of three personnel with one vehicle from the Zigong Earthquake Monitoring Center to the epicenter to carry out emergency response work.

Changning County officials confirmed that shaking was felt but no casualty reports had been received, and teams were actively assessing the disaster situation, according to a detailed report from [Xinhua News](https://www.news.cn/local/20260824/611acf92298f498caebd56e5bde4704b/c.html). Shaking was felt across a wide area including Yibin, Luzhou, Leshan, Zigong, and Chengdu, with communications and power supply reported as normal.

"When the earthquake happened, I had just stood up, and the shaking was noticeable," a resident of Yibin city told [China News](https://www.chinanews.com/sh/2026/08-24/10682982.shtml).

## False 7.7-Magnitude Warning Sparks Controversy

The earthquake response was complicated by a significant error from a private institution. The Chengdu High-Tech Disaster Reduction Institute initially issued a 7.7-magnitude earthquake warning through its early warning network, which was later determined to be a major error. The institute's warning magnitude sequence was 7.7 (5 seconds after the quake), 6.4 (6s), 3.7 (7s), 4.1 (8s), and 3.9 (16s), converging to 3.9 at 17 seconds, according to the institute's statement as reported by [China News](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/sh/2026/08-24/10682973.shtml).

The Sichuan Earthquake Agency stated that the Chengdu High-Tech Disaster Reduction Institute **used the name** of the "China Earthquake Early Warning Network" without authorization to publish the 7.7-magnitude warning through channels including Honor and Vivo phones and Xiaotiancai watches. The agency had already **terminated** the third-party authorization to the institute on July 22, 2026, as [CCTV News](https://news.cctv.com/2026/08/24/ARTItBnp6EgdETBzbPQ0SdVK260824.shtml) reported.

The official China Earthquake Early Warning Network produced a warning of 5.4 magnitude in the first moments after the earthquake, which was much closer to the actual 4.7 magnitude. The Chengdu High-Tech Disaster Reduction Institute apologized for the error, acknowledging it was the largest deviation in 15 years of service since September 2011.

"For this significant deviation, the Chengdu High-Tech Disaster Reduction Institute expresses its apology. It will quickly identify the cause, improve technology, and improve services," the institute stated, as reported by [Tencent News/Jimu News](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260824A04LCP00).

## Seismic Context in the Yibin Region

The Yibin area has experienced significantly increased seismic activity in 2026, with 45 earthquakes recorded in 32 days leading up to early August. A 5.5-magnitude earthquake struck nearby Gaoxian on June 29, 2026, and a 5.0-magnitude earthquake occurred on July 8, 2026.

Experts note that the region sits on the Huaying Mountain basement fault zone, which has been active for over 2 million years. Southern Sichuan has numerous fold structures and pre-existing faults, which can produce earthquakes when subjected to changes in medium properties and stress field disturbances, as explained by experts in [Sichuan Online](https://sichuan.scol.com.cn/ggxw/202607/83284460.html).

The region has a history of significant seismic events, including the 2019 Changning 6.0-magnitude earthquake that killed 11 people. Technical analysis of the false warning suggests the error may have been caused by instrument malfunction or insufficient station density at the private institute, as [163.com analysis](https://m.163.com/dy/article/L53GPEB60556LTNU.html) noted.

## Looking Ahead

Authorities continue to monitor the situation and assess potential damage in the affected areas. The incident has raised questions about the reliability of private earthquake early warning systems and the importance of using official channels for earthquake alerts. The Chengdu High-Tech Disaster Reduction Institute has pledged to investigate the cause of the error and improve its technology and services.

As seismic activity in the Yibin region continues, officials are urging residents to rely on official earthquake warning channels and to remain prepared for potential aftershocks.
