# China, UAE to Hold Falcon Shield-2026 Air Force Training

China and the United Arab Emirates have announced they will conduct the Falcon Shield-2026 joint air force training exercise in Hotan, Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region, from late August through mid-September. The exercise marks the fourth iteration of the Falcon Shield series and reflects the deepening military cooperation between the two nations, according to [Xinhua News](https://www.news.cn/world/20260821/3973919301ed451db127d2c508b242c2/c.html).

The Chinese Ministry of National Defense confirmed the announcement on Friday, stating that the training will include command simulations, flight training, and live-force exercises in mixed formations. The ministry emphasized that the exercise aims to enhance the tactical capabilities of participating units and deepen traditional friendship and practical cooperation between the two militaries, as [reported](https://english.news.cn/20260821/e11335fae30644efb95a6eb2578e90ba/c.html) by Xinhua's English service.

## A Growing Partnership

The Falcon Shield series has become a flagship cooperative project between the Chinese and UAE air forces since its inception in 2023. The first exercise was held in Xinjiang in August 2023, marking the first-ever joint air force exercise between the two countries. It followed a February 2023 deal for China to export domestically developed L15 advanced trainer jets to the UAE, with 12 aircraft initially ordered and an option for 36 more, as [Global Times](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202307/1295381.shtml) reported at the time.

The second iteration, Falcon Shield-2024, took place in Xinjiang from late June to late July 2024, with the UAE's deputy military attache in China attending the opening ceremony. According to the [Ministry of National Defense](http://www.mod.gov.cn/gfbw/jsxd/16323390.html), the exercise aimed to enhance mutual understanding and trust, deepen exchanges and cooperation between the two air forces, and elevate strategic coordination.

In December 2025, the exercise moved to the UAE for the first time. During Falcon Shield-2025, China's J-10C fighter jets, KJ-500 airborne early warning aircraft, and YY-20A aerial refueling tankers deployed to the Gulf nation for the first time. Both sides operated in integrated formations, focusing on command simulations and air superiority missions, with additional exchanges on night combat operations, unmanned systems, and battlefield search and rescue, according to [Defense Ministry spokesperson Zhang Xiaogang](https://www.chinanews.com.cn/gn/2025/12-25/10540210.shtml).

## Strategic Implications

The Falcon Shield-2026 comes at a time when the PLA Air Force is simultaneously conducting joint training with multiple countries. In August 2026 alone, the Chinese air force is engaged in exercises with Belarus (Swift Eagle 2026), Thailand (Falcon Strike 2026), and Egypt (Eagles of Civilization 2026), as [Global Times](https://www.globaltimes.cn/page/202608/1368649.shtml) reported. The concurrent exercises across three regions demonstrate the PLA Air Force's growing strategic reach and capacity for global engagement.

The deepening China-UAE military relationship is part of a broader strategic partnership that extends well beyond defense. China was the UAE's largest trading partner in 2022, while the UAE was China's biggest partner in the Arab world. Energy trade is substantial, with the UAE exporting $17.4 billion in crude petroleum and $4.12 billion in petroleum gas to China in 2022, according to [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2024/07/china-uae-hold-air-force-drills-xinjiang-defense-relations-grow).

The expanding military ties have drawn attention from Washington. The Biden administration warned the UAE in 2021 about a suspected Chinese military base being built in the Gulf state, and despite the UAE's September 2024 upgrade to a US "major defense partner," analysts noted this would not deter continued China cooperation, as [Al-Monitor](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2025/12/china-uae-hold-third-air-force-drills-defense-ties-warm-what-we-know) observed.

The rotating host arrangement—with exercises held in China in 2023, 2024, and 2026, and in the UAE in 2025—suggests both sides view the Falcon Shield series as a long-term, institutionalized cooperation mechanism. The UAE's pursuit of military ties with China alongside its US security partnership reflects a deliberate multi-alignment strategy in the Gulf.

## What to Watch

As the Falcon Shield-2026 exercise gets underway in Xinjiang, observers will be watching for several developments: the specific aircraft platforms deployed by both sides, the scale of the exercise compared to previous iterations, and any announcements regarding future cooperation. The continued expansion of this exercise series signals that China-UAE defense cooperation is likely to deepen further, with potential implications for regional security dynamics in both the Gulf and the broader Indo-Pacific.

With the exercise running through mid-September, the coming weeks will provide a clearer picture of how this partnership continues to evolve and what it means for the strategic landscape of the Middle East and beyond.
