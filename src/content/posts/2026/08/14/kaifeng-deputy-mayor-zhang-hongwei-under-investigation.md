# Kaifeng Deputy Mayor Zhang Hongwei Under Investigation for Serious Violations

Zhang Hongwei, Executive Deputy Mayor of Kaifeng City in China's Henan Province, is under disciplinary review and supervision investigation for suspected serious disciplinary and legal violations, according to an announcement from the Henan Provincial Commission for Discipline Inspection. The announcement, [reported by Xinhua News](https://www.news.cn/legal/20260814/beb3d65060f1413fa790cce1b83d3c26/c.html), was published on August 14, 2026.

## Official Announcement

The provincial disciplinary body stated that Zhang, who serves as a member of the Standing Committee of the CPC Kaifeng Municipal Committee, Deputy Secretary of the Party Group of the Municipal Government, and Executive Deputy Mayor, is suspected of "serious disciplinary and legal violations." The investigation is being conducted by the Henan Provincial Commission for Discipline Inspection and Supervision, as [confirmed by Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103318011.html).

Zhang was investigated while still in office, a pattern that has become increasingly common in China's ongoing anti-corruption campaign. The announcement was also carried by [21st Century Business Herald](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20260814/herald/456559f075a5493f4ef3ee24b278f202.html), which cited the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection (CCDI) website as its source.

## Career Background

Born in November 1972, Zhang holds a university degree and a Master's degree in Management Science. According to [ZAKER News](https://app.myzaker.com/news/article.php?pk=6a7e88968e9f095cd0706974), his career began in the Zhengzhou Municipal Party Committee Organization Department, where he held various positions including Principal Staff Member, Deputy Division Chief, and Office Director.

Zhang's career progressed through several key roles in Henan Province:

- **Xinmi City**: He served as Standing Committee Member and Organization Department Head, then Deputy Secretary of the Municipal Party Committee, and later Deputy Secretary and Mayor.
- **Zhengzhou Jinshui District**: In May 2020, he was appointed Secretary of the Jinshui District Party Committee, and in 2023 he became a member of the Standing Committee of the Zhengzhou Municipal Party Committee while continuing as Jinshui District Party Secretary.
- **Kaifeng City**: In March 2024, Zhang was transferred to Kaifeng as a member of the Standing Committee of the CPC Kaifeng Municipal Committee and Deputy Secretary of the Party Group of the Municipal Government. He was elected Deputy Mayor in April 2024, as [documented by Jiefang Daily](https://www.jfdaily.com/wx/detail.do?id=734990).

## Role in Zhengzhou-Kaifeng Integration

As Executive Deputy Mayor, Zhang played a significant role in Kaifeng's day-to-day government operations and was actively involved in the Zhengzhou-Kaifeng integration initiative. In October 2024, he publicly outlined the "six integrations" strategy covering planning, transportation, industry, science and technology innovation, ecology, and public services, according to [Dahe Caifang Cube](https://app.dahecube.com/nweb/news/20241025/215394n1e6b0b42294.htm). He emphasized building the Zhengzhou-Kaifeng automobile industry belt and creating a 100-billion-yuan automotive manufacturing cluster around the Chery Automobile Kaifeng base.

## Broader Anti-Corruption Context

Zhang's investigation comes amid an intensified anti-corruption campaign in China in 2026. The campaign has expanded from individual cases to a systematic "retroactive investigation" model, with multiple senior officials at central and local levels investigated or disciplined in rapid succession. Key features of the current phase include high temporal concentration, broad sectoral coverage from financial regulation to local government systems, and clear retroactive investigation patterns.

Henan Province has seen multiple officials investigated in 2026, reflecting the sustained high-pressure anti-corruption efforts being driven at both the national and provincial levels. The "Study-Inspect-Reform" campaign has been instrumental in maintaining this momentum.

## Implications and Outlook

The investigation of Zhang Hongwei could have significant implications for Kaifeng's municipal government operations. As Executive Deputy Mayor, he oversaw the city's day-to-day administration and was a key figure in advancing the Zhengzhou-Kaifeng integration projects. The case may affect the pace of these initiatives and raises questions about the extent of the investigation's reach.

The case adds to the growing list of Henan officials investigated in 2026 and demonstrates the continued reach of China's anti-corruption campaign into local government systems. Observers will be watching whether the investigation expands to include other officials in Kaifeng or Zhengzhou, and what specific allegations emerge as the disciplinary review progresses.

For now, the investigation remains ongoing, with the Henan Provincial Commission for Discipline Inspection and Supervision conducting the review. The outcome will likely depend on the findings of the disciplinary proceedings, which in similar cases have ranged from administrative penalties to criminal prosecution depending on the severity of the violations uncovered.
