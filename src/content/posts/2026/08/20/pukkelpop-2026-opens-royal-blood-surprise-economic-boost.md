# Pukkelpop 2026 Opens: Campers Arrive, Royal Blood Surprise, Economic Boost for Limburg

The 41st edition of Pukkelpop officially kicked off Thursday morning as the first campers streamed through the gates at the festival grounds in Kiewit, Hasselt. The sold-out festival, one of Europe's premier music events, runs from August 20-23 with approximately 66,000 visitors expected daily, according to [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/liveblog/eerste-kampeerders-aangekomen-britse-band-royal-blood-speelt-vr~1787214104615/).

## Campers Race for Prime Spots

Camping gates opened early Thursday morning, but many festival-goers arrived hours before to secure prime spots. A group of girls from Bonheiden told [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/08/20/eerste-campinggangers-druppelen-binnen-op-pukkelpop-wekker-sto/) they got up at quarter past five to make the journey. "We hope to find a good spot on the campsite," they said. "We plan to set up our tents and then try to get some more sleep."

Thibo and Thibaut from Genk, attending their third Pukkelpop, came prepared with a handcart for their gear. "This is our 3rd Pukkelpop, so we know what works by now," they said. "That's why we're here so early. Better early than late!"

Others came with even more ambitious plans. Rafael and Louis brought approximately 112 cans of half-liter Cara pils along with a cooler they plan to keep stocked with ice for the full four days. "That way we'll hopefully survive the weekend," they joked.

## Royal Blood Surprise Set Confirmed

Festival organizers confirmed that British rock duo **Royal Blood** will play a surprise concert on Friday in the Marquee tent. The slot at 17:00 had been kept open in the lineup, fueling speculation about a mystery act. The Brighton-based band, known for hits like "Out of the Black" and "Figure It Out," previously played Pukkelpop in 2024 as a surprise replacement for Queens of the Stone Age as festival closer.

The surprise announcement is one of several last-minute lineup changes. American DJ John Summit cancelled his Friday appearance due to unforeseen circumstances, with **Diplo** stepping in as replacement, as [RTBF](https://www.rtbf.be/article/pukkelpop-le-dj-john-summit-annule-sa-venue-diplo-major-lazer-le-remplace-11771473) reported. Diplo will now perform twice on Friday—once with Major Lazer on the Main Stage and again as a solo DJ set in the Boiler.

Friday headliner Yungblud is also facing challenges. The 29-year-old British singer revealed on Instagram that he has a leg injury and is using crutches, but confirmed he will still perform. "Leg is done for, but I'm still performing," he wrote. "I'm coming and it will be insane," as [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/08/17/yungblud-been-om-zeep-pukkelpop/) reported.

## Economic Impact: Tens of Millions for Limburg

Beyond the music, new data reveals Pukkelpop is a significant economic engine for the Limburg region. According to calculations by Voka - KvK Limburg, the festival generates tens of millions of euros in added value for the province, as [reported by VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/08/19/pukkelpop-tientallen-miljoenen-euro-s-meerwaarde-voka-economie-l/).

"Tens of thousands of visitors spend money, thousands of people are working, and a whole chain of entrepreneurs and self-employed people is involved," said Dario D'Arpino of Voka. The economic benefit comes primarily from businesses that carry out additional activities through the festival, including hospitality, transport, and many other sectors.

D'Arpino noted that significantly more businesses are now active in and around the festival compared to ten years ago. "If you compare Pukkelpop to ten years ago, there are now many more businesses active in and around the festival," he said. "It's more than just a music festival."

The festival also serves as a springboard for new companies. Hasselt-based The Safe Group, which develops crowd management software, gained international visibility and credibility through its work at Pukkelpop.

## A Festival with Deep Roots

Founded in 1985 by the Humanistische Jongeren van Leopoldsburg, Pukkelpop has grown from a small one-day event into one of Europe's most important music festivals, according to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pukkelpop). The festival takes its name from "De Pukkel," the youth center where it originated. Over four decades, it has earned a reputation for discovering artists early—Nirvana, Amy Winehouse, Foo Fighters, Billie Eilish, and Dua Lipa all played Pukkelpop in their early days.

This year's edition features approximately 185 acts across 8 stages, including headliners Tyler, The Creator, Zara Larsson, Yungblud, Deftones, and Florence + The Machine. The festival continues through Sunday, August 23, with the full schedule and lineup details available via the VRT NWS liveblog.

## What to Watch For

As the festival gets underway, several storylines will unfold over the coming days: how Yungblud performs with his injury, whether Royal Blood's surprise set lives up to expectations, and how the festival's economic impact continues to grow. With the event sold out and thousands already on site, Pukkelpop 2026 is poised to be one of the most memorable editions in its 41-year history.
