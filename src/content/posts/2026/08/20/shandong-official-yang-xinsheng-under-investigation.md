# Shandong Deputy Mayor Yang Xinsheng Under Investigation for Discipline Violations

Yang Xinsheng, Deputy Mayor of Heze City and Party Secretary of Yuncheng County in Shandong Province, is under investigation for suspected serious violations of discipline and law, according to an official announcement from the Shandong Provincial Commission for Discipline Inspection. The announcement was published on August 20, 2026, and reported by [Xinhua News](https://www.news.cn/20260820/1c5cf4c2df444435b0f9a53f6cd22653/c.html).

## Investigation Details

The Shandong Provincial Commission for Discipline Inspection stated that Yang is currently undergoing disciplinary review and supervisory investigation. The announcement follows the standard format used by Chinese disciplinary authorities, citing "suspected serious violations of discipline and law" as the basis for the probe. [CCTV News](https://news.cctv.com/2026/08/20/ARTIKfutOI7eR5VJWQMDopdF260820.shtml) also reported the development, citing the Central Commission for Discipline Inspection (CCDI) website.

The investigation was announced simultaneously through multiple official channels, underscoring the seriousness of the case. [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/brief/103325909.html) also reported the news on the same day, confirming the disciplinary action against the senior county-level official.

## Career Background

Yang Xinsheng, born in January 1973, built a lengthy career in local government and party disciplinary work across Shandong Province. According to [Dazhong News](https://dzrb.dzng.com/general/0/NEWS2027857FHGZADAWTGIDO), he previously served as Standing Committee Member and Secretary-General of the Zibo Municipal Commission for Discipline Inspection, giving him insider knowledge of the anti-corruption system.

His career trajectory included leadership positions in three different counties across three different cities in Shandong. He served as Deputy Secretary of Gaoqing County and then County Magistrate, followed by Party Secretary of Gaotang County in Liaocheng City. In late 2024, he was promoted to the deputy department-level position of Deputy Mayor of Heze City while continuing to serve as Party Secretary of Yuncheng County.

[Qilu Net](https://heze.iqilu.com/hzshizheng/2024/1223/5759688.shtml) reported that Yang began serving as Party Secretary of Yuncheng County on December 21, 2024, following a public notice from the Shandong Provincial Party Committee Organization Department announcing him as a candidate for a deputy department-level leadership position. [Jinan Times](https://www.jinantimes.com.cn/news-171-5011220.html) confirmed that the Heze Municipal People's Congress Standing Committee appointed him as Deputy Mayor on December 24, 2024.

## Recent Activities

Prior to the investigation, Yang was actively involved in local governance and development initiatives. [China.com.cn](http://stzg.china.com.cn/2025-02/28/content_43039347.htm) reported on his February 2025 inspection tour of rural revitalization work in Huang'an Town, Chenpo Township, and Shuibao Township, where he emphasized the importance of industrial development and agricultural modernization.

Yang also played a prominent role in cultural and tourism events. In February 2026, he attended the 17th Heze Lantern Festival lighting ceremony in Yuncheng County, where he stated: "Not only should tourists have fun, but we must also make everyone feel comfortable," according to [163.com](https://m.163.com/dy/article/KL16QOLU0530WJIN.html). In October 2025, he spoke at the 40th anniversary celebration of the Songjiang Wushu School, noting that its development "resonates with Yuncheng's cultural heritage and Heze's educational and sports revitalization," as reported by [Tencent News](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20251011A07BTB00).

Most recently, in December 2025, Yang presided over a press conference on Yuncheng County's achievements during the 14th Five-Year Plan period, as covered by [The Paper](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_32249461).

## Analysis and Implications

This case is notable because Yang previously served in the disciplinary inspection system as a Standing Committee Member and Secretary-General of the Zibo Municipal Commission for Discipline Inspection. His background makes this a case of a former anti-corruption insider now facing investigation, which may carry particular significance in China's ongoing anti-corruption campaign.

The investigation represents the continuing anti-corruption drive targeting local government officials across China, with Shandong Province actively conducting such probes under the national campaign led by the CCDI. The simultaneous announcement through multiple official channels indicates the gravity of the case.

## What's Next

The specific allegations against Yang have not been publicly detailed, and the investigation remains ongoing. Observers will be watching for potential outcomes, which could range from party disciplinary action to criminal prosecution depending on the findings. The investigation may also have implications for ongoing development projects and cultural tourism initiatives in Yuncheng County, where Yang held concurrent leadership responsibilities.

As the case progresses, further details about the alleged violations and any related investigations of other officials in Heze or Yuncheng may emerge through official channels.
