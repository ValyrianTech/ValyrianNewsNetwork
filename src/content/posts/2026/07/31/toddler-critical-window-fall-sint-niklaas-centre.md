# Toddler Critical After Window Fall at Sint-Niklaas Centre

A two-year-old girl is in life-threatening condition after falling from a second-floor window at a Red Cross reception centre in Sint-Niklaas, East Flanders. Emergency services rushed the child to hospital on Thursday evening, and Belgian public broadcaster [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/07/31/meisje-kind-2-twee-raam-val-sint-niklaas/) reported on Friday morning that she was still fighting for her life.

## Context

The accident happened at the Red Cross reception centre on Kasteelstraat in Sint-Niklaas, an asylum reception facility run by Rode Kruis-Vlaanderen (Red Cross Flanders). The centre forms part of Belgium's federal reception network coordinated by Fedasil, the Federal Agency for the Reception of Asylum Seekers.

## Key Developments

The incident occurred around 18:00 local time on Thursday 30 July, when the girl fell from a window on the second floor and landed in the inner courtyard of the centre, [Het Laatste Nieuws reported](https://www.hln.be/sint-niklaas/meisje-2-in-levensgevaar-na-val-uit-raam-van-opvangcentrum-in-sint-niklaas~a7981c83/). Emergency services responded in large numbers, and several police teams were dispatched after the incident caused unrest at the centre.

Sint-Niklaas local police confirmed the accident. Regional broadcaster [TV Oost](https://www.tvoost.be/nieuws/2-jarig-meisje-in-levensgevaar-na-val-van-tweede-verdieping-in-opvangcentrum-sint-niklaas-212859) reported that authorities described the fall as a tragic accident.

The East Flanders prosecutor's office (parket Oost-Vlaanderen) has opened an investigation into the exact circumstances. Spokesperson Lisa De Wilde said the most likely explanation was a "tragic accident." Speaking to the [Belga news agency](https://www.msn.com/nl-be/openbare-veiligheid-en-noodgevallen/veiligheids-en-gezondheidswaarschuwingen/meisje-in-levensgevaar-na-val-uit-raam-in-opvangcentrum-in-sint-niklaas/ar-AA298ghe), De Wilde added: "The girl is still in life-threatening condition. The precise circumstances are still unclear, but the most likely line is that this was a tragic accident. Thorough investigation will have to provide more clarity."

## Analysis

Window falls involving young children are a recognised category of preventable household accidents. Safety guidance typically stresses window locks, guards, and adequate railing heights in homes where small children live. The incident at the Sint-Niklaas centre is likely to renew attention on safety standards in Belgium's collective reception facilities, although Rode Kruis-Vlaanderen had not published an official response as of Friday morning.

## What's Next

The girl remains in hospital in critical condition. The prosecutor's investigation is expected to establish precisely how the fall happened, including whether any safety measures were neglected. Preliminary indications, however, point to a tragic accident rather than any criminal intent.
