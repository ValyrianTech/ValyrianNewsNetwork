# Antwerp Rabbi Files Bribery Complaint Against Trump Ring

An Antwerp-based rabbi has filed a formal legal complaint against US President Donald Trump, alleging that a diamond ring gifted to him by the Antwerp diamond sector constitutes "outright bribery." Rabbi Moshe Friedman argues the gift was intended to influence Belgium's ongoing judicial investigation into controversial ritual circumcisions, a case that has already strained diplomatic relations between Belgium and the United States.

## The Gift and the Complaint

The Antwerp World Diamond Centre (AWDC) presented Trump with a ring containing 321 natural diamonds, 56 sapphires, 13 emeralds, and 6 rubies, set in 18-karat gold, to commemorate the 250th anniversary of American independence. Designed by Antwerp jeweler David Gotlib and certified by HRD Antwerp, independent jewelers estimate its value at $25,000 to $35,000 (€21,000 to €30,000). The ring was handed to US Ambassador to Belgium Bill White at the "America 250" celebration in Brussels' Jubelpark on June 28.

According to [Het Laatste Nieuws](https://www.hln.be/binnenland/antwerpse-rabbijn-dient-klacht-in-tegen-trump-nadat-diamantsector-hem-een-ring-met-321-diamanten-schonk-regelrechte-omkoping~a1881ab5/), Friedman told HUMO magazine: "When an exceptionally valuable gift is given to a president who is considered an important political ally, I find it legitimate to ask questions about the intention behind it." He added: "It is a clear case of bribery, period."

## The Circumcision Investigation

Friedman himself initiated the original complaint in 2023 against two mohels (ritual circumcisers), alleging that his three sons suffered medical consequences from their circumcisions, which he described as "traumatizing and unnecessary." That complaint triggered a broader judicial investigation into ritual circumcision practices in Antwerp. Friedman's allegations include children being infected with herpes and HIV, and even deaths resulting from procedures, as [VRT NWS reported](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/02/17/moshe-friedman-diende-klacht-in-tegen-omstreden-besnijdenissen/) in February.

## Diplomatic Tensions

US Ambassador Bill White has been heavily involved in the matter, calling the Belgian investigation "absolutely anti-Semitism" and urging Belgian politicians to pressure the judiciary to drop the case. White claims he received direct instructions from Trump. Belgian Foreign Minister Maxime Prévot (Les Engagés) rebuked White, calling his statements "unacceptable" and summoning the ambassador.

Friedman pointed to White's involvement as central to his complaint. "Ambassador Bill White has repeatedly stated that from the beginning he received direct orders from Trump and acted solely on the basis of those instructions," Friedman told HUMO.

## Tariff Context

The ring gift came months after the Belgian diamond industry successfully secured a zero percent import tariff on Antwerp's annual export of more than $2 billion (€1.7 billion) of polished diamonds to the US, following negotiations with the Trump administration. As [Euronews reported](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/07/03/belgian-diamond-group-that-won-tariff-relief-gifts-trump-lavishly-encrusted-ring), the AWDC said it provided "input" to the European Commission as it negotiated with Trump on a broad tariff deal in 2025, but did not itself lobby the administration.

## Legal and Ethical Questions

The complaint raises complex legal questions, including whether a Belgian court can hear a case against a sitting US president and whether a publicly presented ceremonial gift constitutes bribery. US presidents have considerable discretion to accept gifts from domestic and foreign sources, though gifts from foreign governments are prohibited by the Foreign Emoluments Clause without congressional assent. Four US ethics experts told the Associated Press that Trump has broken with decades-old custom in accepting such valuable gifts.

## A Polarizing Figure

Friedman is a polarizing figure. According to Knack magazine, he is a "self-declared rabbi" who is not recognized by mainstream Jewish institutions and has been accused of negationism (Holocaust denial) and anti-Zionism. His claims about circumcision abuses have divided opinion, with some viewing him as a courageous whistleblower and others as a provocateur.

## What's Next

The complaint could further strain US-Belgium relations if it gains traction, and may set a precedent for foreign citizens filing complaints against US officials. The White House has not yet issued a formal response to the complaint. Meanwhile, the fate of the ring itself remains unclear — a White House official told Euronews that the ring had not yet been presented to Trump as of early July, and a photo posted by Ambassador White wearing the ring has since been deleted.

The case also threatens to damage the reputation of the Antwerp diamond sector, a historic global hub for the diamond trade that has operated in the city for centuries. As the legal process unfolds, the intersection of commerce, diplomacy, and religious practice in this affair is likely to remain under intense scrutiny.
