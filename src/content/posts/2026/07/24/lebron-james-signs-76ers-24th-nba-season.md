# LeBron James Signs with 76ers for Record 24th NBA Season

NBA all-time leading scorer LeBron James has agreed to a two-year, $8 million contract with the Philadelphia 76ers, he announced Friday via social media, beginning his record-extending 24th season in the league. The 41-year-old chose the 76ers over the Cleveland Cavaliers, Miami Heat, Golden State Warriors, and Minnesota Timberwolves, according to [The Athletic](https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/7417464/2026/07/24/lebron-james-contract-76ers-free-agency/), citing championship aspirations as his driving motivation.

“I believe I can help make the Philadelphia 76ers a championship team and I am so excited to energize a new fan base and start this incredible journey one last time,” James wrote on X. “This is my last decision. I’m not going for money … I still want to compete, to win and to have a chance at the feeling of winning another championship.”

## A Legendary Career Continues

James, who turns 42 in December, ends an eight-year tenure with the Los Angeles Lakers that included the franchise’s 2020 NBA championship, a Western Conference finals appearance, and the night he [became the NBA’s all-time leading scorer](https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/4165535/2023/02/07/lebron-james-points-scoring-record-kareem-abdul-jabbar/). After the Lakers’ elimination on May 11, James seriously considered retirement, telling his followers on X: “I thought I was done when the season ended. I wasn’t ready to announce it … I was pretty sure I played my last game.”

James informed the Lakers earlier this summer that [he would not return](https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/7302889/2026/06/30/lebron-james-returns-leaving-lakers-nba/) for a ninth season with the franchise, opening a free agency period that dominated NBA headlines for weeks. Averaging 20.9 points, 7.2 assists, and 6.1 rebounds last season while shooting 51.5 percent from the field, James proved he remains highly productive even in a reduced role alongside Luka Dončić.

## The Recruitment

The 76ers’ pursuit of James was spearheaded by two of their stars. Joel Embiid and Tyrese Maxey placed multiple recruiting calls over several weeks, making the case that Philadelphia offered James his best path to a fifth championship, sources told The Athletic. The case became significantly more compelling when first-year president of basketball operations Mike Gansey pulled off a blockbuster trade, acquiring 2024 Finals MVP Jaylen Brown from the Boston Celtics for Paul George and four future draft picks.

“Now, we’ve got a pretty interesting roster,” a team source told The Athletic.

The only public pitch came from Bob Myers, the president of sports for Harris Blitzer Sports & Entertainment, who [appeared on the podcast of James’ agent, Rich Paul](https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/7431541/2026/07/08/lebron-james-sixers-pitch-bob-myers/), earlier in July. “If he was here, I’d say, ‘I honestly believe this is your best chance to win,’” Myers told Paul and co-host Max Kellerman.

## Championship Calculus

James’ decision to take the veteran minimum — just $8 million over two years — sends an unmistakable signal about his priorities. “All of it was in the service of winning a championship,” a source close to James told The Athletic. James joins a roster featuring Embiid, Brown, Maxey, second-year guard VJ Edgecombe, and head coach Nick Nurse. He is expected to start at power forward.

The 76ers now boast arguably the most talented top-end lineup in the Eastern Conference. The franchise has not won an NBA championship since 1983, and the addition of James — even at age 41 — immediately reshapes title expectations. According to [The Athletic’s analysis of shifting title odds](https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/7468190/2026/07/24/lebron-nba-title-odds-sixers-knicks-betting/), Philadelphia is now positioned as a favorite to emerge from the East.

## What This Means for the NBA

The move reshuffles the league’s hierarchy. The Eastern Conference gains another superstar as the West loses one. The 76ers, Knicks, and Celtics now form a formidable trio of contenders in the East. For James, winning a fifth title with a third different franchise would further cement a legacy already defined by unprecedented longevity.

There are risks. Embiid’s injury history, James’ age, and the draft capital sacrificed in the Brown trade create a narrow championship window of one to two years. But for a player who has redefined expectations for what is possible at every stage of a career, betting against LeBron James has rarely been wise.

## Looking Ahead

James will debut for the 76ers when the 2026-27 NBA season tips off this fall, wearing a new uniform for only the fourth time in his career. The basketball world will be watching closely to see whether this final chapter adds another championship ring to a career already filled with them.

As James himself put it: “I still want to compete, to win.” In Philadelphia, he believes he has found his best chance.
