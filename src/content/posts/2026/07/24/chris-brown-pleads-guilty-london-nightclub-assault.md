# Chris Brown Pleads Guilty in London Nightclub Assault Case

Grammy-winning R&B singer Chris Brown pleaded guilty to affray on Friday at Southwark Crown Court in London, admitting his role in a violent bottle attack at a Mayfair nightclub in February 2023. The plea resolves a case that had been heading toward trial on more serious charges, which were dropped in exchange for the guilty plea.

According to [BBC News](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c3w01e37v9vo), Brown, 37, and his vocal coach Omololu Akinlolu, 40 — a rapper who performs as Hoody Baby — each entered guilty pleas to affray in a hearing that lasted less than five minutes. Judge Tony Baumgartner scheduled sentencing for October 26, 2026. The maximum penalty for affray under UK law is three years in prison.

## The Attack

The incident occurred in the early hours of February 19, 2023, at the Tape nightclub in Hanover Square, Mayfair, shortly after Brown performed at Cardiff's Principality Stadium. Prosecutors said Brown approached music producer Abraham "Abe" Diaw on the dance floor and struck him over the head with a glass bottle multiple times. Akinlolu joined the assault, punching Diaw in the head.

The [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/uk-news/2026/jul/24/singer-chris-brown-pleads-guilty-to-affray-over-london-club-confrontation) reported that Diaw attempted to flee but was chased, pushed to the floor, and kicked while seated defenseless. The Crown Prosecution Service described it as a "vicious and unprovoked attack" captured on CCTV surveillance cameras. Diaw was treated at St Mary's Hospital for lacerations to his head and torn ligaments in his leg.

## From Arrest to Plea

Brown was arrested more than two years after the attack. Metropolitan Police detectives detained him at 2 a.m. on May 15, 2025, at the Lowry Hotel in Manchester, where he had arrived by private jet for UK dates on his "Breezy Bowl" tour. He was initially remanded in custody at Forest Bank prison in Salford, spending six days behind bars before being released on bail with a £5 million ($6.7 million) security fee.

The singer had originally pleaded not guilty to charges of assault, attempting to cause grievous bodily harm, and possession of an offensive weapon. Prosecutors dropped those charges on Friday in exchange for the affray plea.

## Prosecution Response

Claire Campbell, Senior Crown Prosecutor for CPS London South, said in a statement that the attack was "entirely unacceptable" and that the prosecution proves "no one is above the law." Detective Constable Zara Ripamonti of the Metropolitan Police called it "a disgraceful, unprovoked assault that put a man in hospital."

As [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/europe/singer-chris-brown-pleads-guilty-affray-altercation-london-rcna589037) reported, Brown appeared at Friday's hearing wearing a tan suit, gold-rimmed sunglasses, and a St. Louis Cardinals baseball cap. After the hearing, he signed autographs for fans outside the courthouse.

## Legal History and Career

The case adds to Brown's well-documented legal troubles spanning nearly two decades. In 2009, he pleaded guilty to felony assault of his then-girlfriend, pop star Rihanna, hours before they were due to perform at the Grammy Awards. He was sentenced to 180 days of community labor and five years' probation.

More recently, in July 2026, a court ordered Brown to pay $13 million to a housekeeper who was mauled by his dog. Despite his legal difficulties, Brown has maintained a successful music career, winning the Grammy for Best R&B Album in 2025 for "11:11 (Deluxe)." He is currently touring with Usher.

According to [Vulture](https://www.vulture.com/article/chris-brown-guilty-night-club-assault-london.html), Diaw has also filed a separate civil lawsuit against Brown. That case, along with the outcome of the October sentencing, could further shape the trajectory of an entertainer who continues to draw both devoted fans and intense scrutiny over his history of violence.

## What’s Next

Brown and Akinlolu are both scheduled to be sentenced on October 26, 2026. Brown's bail conditions allow him to leave the UK but require him to return by October 25. The judge will have discretion to impose any sentence up to the three-year maximum for affray. The outcome will be closely watched as it marks another significant chapter in the legal saga of one of R&B's most successful — and controversial — figures.
