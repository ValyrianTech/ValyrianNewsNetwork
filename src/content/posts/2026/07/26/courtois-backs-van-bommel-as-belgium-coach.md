# Courtois Backs Van Bommel as Belgium Coach, Keeps Door Open

Thibaut Courtois has publicly endorsed Mark van Bommel's appointment as the new head coach of the Belgian national team, calling the Dutchman "a good choice" while leaving the door open to continue his international career with the Red Devils. The 34-year-old Real Madrid goalkeeper became the first Red Devil to react to Van Bommel's appointment in an interview with [Het Laatste Nieuws](https://www.hln.be/rode-duivels/belangrijk-dat-hij-zon-palmares-heeft-thibaut-courtois-ziet-hoe-mark-van-bommel-met-status-respect-afdwingt-als-bondscoach-van-rode-duivels~a83cbb89/).

## Context: A New Era for the Red Devils

Mark van Bommel, 49, was officially announced as Belgium's new national coach on July 21, signing a contract through to Euro 2028. He replaces Rudi Garcia, whose contract was not renewed after Belgium's quarterfinal exit at the 2026 World Cup against Spain. According to [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/07/21/rode-duivels-hebben-nieuwe-bondscoach-nederlander-mark-van-bomm/), Van Bommel brings a staff that includes former Netherlands international Boudewijn Zenden, Reinier Robbemond, and ex-Red Devil Maarten Martens.

Van Bommel previously led Royal Antwerp to a historic league and cup double in 2023, cementing his reputation as one of Belgium's most successful recent coaches. His playing career included stints at PSV, Barcelona, Bayern Munich, and AC Milan, with 79 caps for the Netherlands.

## Courtois: Full Support and a Cautious Future

Speaking at the Keepers Elite-Demodag in Maastricht, Courtois made clear he approves of the choice. "I don't know him personally, but everyone knows his story at Antwerp," Courtois told Het Laatste Nieuws. "Toby (Alderweireld) speaks highly of him. And if Toby says that, I believe it."

The goalkeeper emphasized the value of Van Bommel's playing pedigree. "With such a palmares, as a coach you can demand things more quickly from your group. It's easier for players to listen. He knows what it's like to play so many matches. Moreover, with that status you command a lot of respect from the players."

On the question of Van Bommel's Dutch nationality — a point of controversy among some French-speaking Belgian commentators — Courtois dismissed the concern. As reported by [Voetbalprimeur](https://www.voetbalprimeur.be/nieuws/2116222/courtois-ziet-geen-probleem-in-komst-van-bommel-als-bondscoach.html), Courtois noted: "It doesn't matter that he's Dutch. We all play internationally. We'll make him Belgian for two years. We just want to win. Roberto Martinez was Spanish, Domenico Tedesco Italian, Rudi Garcia French..."

## International Future: Still Uncertain

Despite his endorsement of the new coach, Courtois stopped short of confirming his continued participation with the national team. The goalkeeper had previously indicated he wanted to skip the upcoming Nations League campaign to rest, with a possible return for Euro 2028 qualifiers.

"I will have a conversation with Vincent (Mannaert, Sports Director) and the coach," Courtois said. "We'll see how things are. Whether he only wants players who are 100 percent committed or not. If that's the case, I'll have to think about what I still want."

Courtois is currently pain-free after a quadriceps injury sustained during the World Cup quarterfinal against Spain. He denied any lingering tension with former coach Garcia over the substitution during that match. "There's absolutely no bad blood between the coach and me," he confirmed. "Rudi and I spoke afterward about his decision. He only wanted to play with players who were 100 percent fit. I said, 'No problem.'"

## Analysis: A Defining Relationship for Belgium's Future

The relationship between Courtois and Van Bommel will be one of the defining dynamics of this new era for Belgium. As one of the world's best goalkeepers, Courtois' presence or absence in the squad dramatically alters Belgium's prospects. Van Bommel's first major test as national coach will be managing this situation while integrating younger players into a team transitioning from its Golden Generation.

Van Bommel has committed to learning French to address concerns from Walloon critics about his appointment. The language issue reflects Belgium's perennial linguistic divide, but Courtois' public support — and his dismissal of the controversy — helps ease the narrative.

## What's Next

Courtois will report to Real Madrid for preseason on August 10, while Van Bommel officially takes over as Red Devils coach in mid-August. A conversation between Courtois, Van Bommel, and Sports Director Vincent Mannaert is expected in the coming weeks to determine the goalkeeper's international future. Belgium's Nations League campaign and the road to Euro 2028 — hosted in the United Kingdom and Ireland — will shape the next chapter for this evolving squad.
