# Daredevils Parachute from Space Needle in Illegal Stunt

Two men climbed over the glass barrier of Seattle’s iconic Space Needle on Friday evening and parachuted 520 feet to the ground in a daring illegal BASE jump, sparking a citywide manhunt by the Seattle Police Department. The suspects, described as white males in their late 20s, remain at large as of Sunday and face trespassing charges, according to [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/space-needle-seattle-base-jump-93e51a529d950e68835f9da70da53503).

## The Incident

The jumpers purchased tickets and entered the Space Needle at approximately 8:50 p.m. PT on Friday, July 24, according to police. Security cameras captured the men on the top-level observation deck, appearing to scout the area before entering a restroom and emerging wearing helmets and backpacks, [KOMO News](https://komonews.com/news/local/two-people-jump-from-seattle-pace-needle-bite-of-seattle-torchlight-seafair-weekend-summer-in-seattle-tourist-social-media-video) reported.

The suspects then sprinted to the north-facing glass window overlooking Lake Union, climbed over the barrier, and jumped off, performing a back flip before deploying their parachutes. The incident was reported to police at 9:21 p.m., and officers arrived minutes later to find Space Needle security uncertain of where the men had landed. The suspects had vanished by the time police arrived.

The entire spectacle was captured on multiple cellphone videos that quickly went viral on TikTok. One eyewitness, posting under the handle @.sherpachai, wrote: “These guys literally sprinted out the bathrooms, scaled the walls, and jumped off the space needle while I am enjoying my view of Seattle!!”

## Landing and Escape

According to the [New York Post](https://nypost.com/2026/07/25/us-news/space-needle-daredevils-pull-off-jaw-dropping-illegal-base-jump/) citing KIRO 7 and TikTok videos, one jumper landed on the roof of the Hyatt hotel at 201 5th Avenue, while the other touched down near Seattle Center, where the Bite of Seattle food festival was underway. The busy weekend event, combined with the Seafair Torchlight Parade drawing hundreds of thousands of visitors, provided ample cover for the jumpers to disappear before police could locate them.

## Security and Legal Response

A spokesperson for the Space Needle confirmed the attraction is cooperating with authorities. “We are aware of an illegal incident at the Space Needle and are working closely with the Seattle Police Department,” the landmark said in a statement. “We are reviewing security footage and assisting with the active investigation.”

The jumpers face trespassing charges, according to Seattle Police Public Affairs Officer Brian Pritchard. The Seattle Police Department continues its investigation, and authorities have not yet publicly identified the suspects.

The incident has raised questions about security at the 605-foot landmark, which was built for the 1962 World’s Fair. Its observation deck is surrounded by high glass windows open to the sky above—a design feature that, while providing spectacular views, also creates vulnerability. Social media reactions have been mixed, with some expressing amazement at the audacity of the stunt and others voicing frustration over potential security tightening. One commenter wrote on TikTok: “Great… way to ruin the space needle because now they will impose bag searches and it will be more expensive.”

## Context and Precedent

BASE jumping—an acronym for Buildings, Antennas, Spans, and Earth—is an extreme sport that involves parachuting from fixed structures at significantly lower altitudes than traditional skydiving. The Space Needle’s observation deck sits at 520 feet, well within the typical range for such jumps, which carry a significantly higher risk of injury or death than skydiving. Unlike skydivers, BASE jumpers use a single specialized parachute container with no reserve, leaving no margin for error.

This is not the first time Seattle has seen such a stunt. In 2024, jumpers parachuted from the newly built 37-story Graystone building in the city and successfully evaded police—a precedent that may have emboldened this week’s daredevils.

## What’s Next

As of Sunday, the suspects remain unidentified and at large. The Space Needle is reviewing its security protocols, and the incident may lead to enhanced measures such as bag searches, increased surveillance, or modifications to the observation deck’s open-air design. Police have not indicated whether additional charges beyond trespassing—such as reckless endangerment or public nuisance—may be filed if the men are apprehended.

The search continues, and authorities are urging anyone with information to come forward. For now, the mystery of who pulled off one of the most audacious stunts in the Space Needle’s history—and how they managed to vanish into a festival crowd—remains unsolved.
