# Fauci COVID Diaries Reveal Fame Fixation as Deaths Mounted

Newly released personal diaries from Dr. Anthony Fauci reveal that as COVID-19 deaths mounted around the world, the former White House chief medical advisor became increasingly fixated on his own growing celebrity, according to [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/fauci-newly-released-covid-diaries-reveal-bizarre-fixation-fame-pandemic-deaths-mounted). The diaries, released July 25 by Senate Homeland Security Committee Chairman Rand Paul (R-KY), offer a strikingly personal account of Fauci's mindset during the most intense months of a pandemic that ultimately claimed more than 7 million lives worldwide.

## Background: The Diaries and Their Release

The journals chronicle Fauci's day-to-day role during the COVID-19 crisis, documenting conversations with President Donald Trump, governors, and world leaders, as well as his media appearances and personal observations. Paul, a longtime critic of Fauci who frequently clashed with him over the federal pandemic response and the origins of the virus, made the diaries public as part of an ongoing oversight investigation by the Senate Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs Committee. The release comes just days before Fauci is scheduled to testify before the committee after being subpoenaed by Paul.

## Key Diary Entries: A Recurring Theme of Fame

A recurring theme throughout the diaries is Fauci's growing awareness of his public profile during the darkest days of the pandemic. On April 7, 2020, as the virus was spreading rapidly across the United States, Fauci wrote: "Press still hot and heavy about me. Multiple stories per day directly or indirectly involving me."

By May 21, 2020 — after recording that nearly 98,000 global deaths and more than 27,000 U.S. deaths had occurred — Fauci's self-assessment had escalated dramatically. According to the diaries, he wrote that his "national and international fame is explosive and really unimaginable" and that it was "not hyperbole" to say he was "the most famous and talked about person in the country."

He also noted that Trump "seems to be enamored of me even though I am taking the spotlight away from him," describing their relationship as "a very unique and interesting relationship." Fauci added that Secret Service agents had become so familiar with him that they often waved him through White House security without checking his identification.

As the pandemic wore on, Fauci's fame continued to attract unusual attention. He recorded in his diary that he had received an invitation to appear on "Dancing with the Stars" — even as the United States was recording what he described as a record 40,000 new COVID-19 cases in a single day.

## Strained Relationship with the White House

The diaries also document Fauci's growing frustration with the Trump administration's handling of the pandemic. On July 2, 2020, after warning that the country could be entering an "exponential" increase in cases heading into the Fourth of July weekend, Fauci wrote that "POTUS and his people have essentially dropped out and do not even recognize that an outbreak is ongoing." He complained that the White House was denying requests for him to appear on major television programs and criticized the administration's public messaging.

Fauci also defended himself against what he described as the "countless" profiles written about him, insisting he had "no desire to conflict with, embarrass or show up the POTUS." He wrote that White House officials were upset that the coverage made it appear he was becoming "a star that outshines" the president.

## Broader Context: COVID-19's Toll

The pandemic during which these diary entries were written ultimately caused severe loss of life. According to the [Centers for Disease Control and Prevention](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/nvss/vsrr/covid19/index.htm), roughly 1.2 million people died from COVID-19 in the United States alone, while global death tolls exceeded 7 million.

## Looking Ahead: Fauci's Upcoming Testimony

The release of the diaries adds a new dimension to the political scrutiny surrounding Fauci, who served as director of the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases for nearly four decades. The entries provide an unusually intimate window into the mindset of one of the most prominent figures in the U.S. pandemic response, capturing not only the government's internal deliberations but also Fauci's private concerns and his complicated relationship with the Trump White House.

Fauci is scheduled to testify before the Senate Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs Committee on July 29, where he is expected to face questions not only about the diaries but also about the origins of COVID-19 and the federal government's pandemic response. The diaries, taken together, are likely to feature prominently in the hearing as lawmakers examine Fauci's judgment and priorities during a national health emergency.
