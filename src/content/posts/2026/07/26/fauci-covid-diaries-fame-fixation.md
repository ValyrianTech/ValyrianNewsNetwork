# Fauci's COVID Diaries Reveal Fame Fixation Amid Deaths

Newly released personal diaries from Dr. Anthony Fauci, the former White House chief medical advisor, reveal a striking preoccupation with his own growing celebrity during the height of the COVID-19 pandemic, even as the virus claimed hundreds of thousands of lives. The journals were made public by Senate Homeland Security Committee Chairman Rand Paul, R-Ky., as part of an ongoing oversight investigation, according to [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/fauci-newly-released-covid-diaries-reveal-bizarre-fixation-fame-pandemic-deaths-mounted).

## Background

Fauci, who led the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases for nearly four decades, served as a leading public face of the federal government's pandemic response under both President Donald Trump and President Joe Biden. The diaries chronicle his day-to-day role during the crisis through conversations with Trump, governors, world leaders, and media figures, offering an unusually personal window into his experience as the pandemic unfolded.

The release comes days before Fauci is scheduled to testify before the Senate Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs Committee after being subpoenaed by Paul, a longtime critic who frequently clashed with Fauci over the federal response and the origins of the virus.

## Diary Entries Reveal Fame Fixation

A recurring theme throughout the diaries is Fauci's growing awareness of his own public profile during the pandemic's most intense months, which ultimately claimed more than 7 million officially reported lives worldwide, including roughly 1.2 million in the United States, according to the [Centers for Disease Control and Prevention](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/nvss/vsrr/covid19/index.htm).

On April 7, 2020, after recording the latest pandemic developments, Fauci wrote: "Press still hot and heavy about me. Multiple stories per day directly or indirectly involving me."

By May 21, after documenting that nearly 98,000 global deaths and more than 27,000 U.S. deaths had been recorded, Fauci wrote that his "national and international fame is explosive and really unimaginable" and that it was "not hyperbole" to say he was "the most famous and talked about person in the country."

He added that Trump "seems to be enamored of me even though I am taking the spotlight away from him," describing their relationship as "a very unique and interesting relationship." Fauci also noted that Secret Service agents had become so familiar with him that they often waved him through White House security without checking his identification.

## Growing Fame and White House Tensions

As the attention mounted, Fauci's diary documented how his prominence had expanded beyond public health. A month later, as the United States recorded what Fauci described as a record 40,000 new COVID-19 cases in a single day, he wrote that he had been invited to appear on "Dancing with the Stars."

At the same time, the diaries reveal Fauci's growing frustration with the Trump White House. On July 2, 2020, after warning that the country could be entering an "exponential" increase in cases heading into the Fourth of July weekend, Fauci wrote, "POTUS and his people have essentially dropped out and do not even recognize that an outbreak is ongoing."

He complained that White House communications officials were restricting his television appearances, questioned the administration's public messaging, and expressed frustration that media coverage increasingly portrayed him as being at odds with Trump. Fauci wrote that White House officials were upset because news coverage made it appear he was becoming "a star that outshines" the president.

Fauci also defended himself against what he described as the "countless" profiles written about him, insisting several were published without interviewing him. He wrote that he had "no desire to conflict with, embarrass or show up the POTUS" and eventually decided to stop participating in profile interviews altogether.

## Analysis and Implications

The diaries offer an unusually personal account of Fauci's mindset during one of the most consequential public health crises in modern U.S. history. They capture not only the government's internal deliberations and his private concerns about the pandemic but also his complicated relationship with the Trump White House and his extraordinary public visibility.

The juxtaposition of Fauci's focus on his celebrity status against the backdrop of mounting COVID-19 fatalities has drawn sharp criticism. The diaries' release is likely to intensify the ongoing debate over Fauci's role during the pandemic, his handling of the public health response, and his transparency with Congress.

## What's Next

Fauci is expected to testify before the Senate Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs Committee in the coming days, where he will likely face questions about the diary entries, his relationship with the Trump administration, and the broader federal response to COVID-19. The testimony follows a subpoena from Sen. Rand Paul, who has signaled that the diaries and other newly released documents will be central to the committee's inquiry.
