# LeBron James, Jaylen Brown Headline 76ers' Roster Revolution

The Philadelphia 76ers have executed one of the most dramatic roster transformations in NBA history, acquiring LeBron James on a bargain veteran minimum contract and trading for 2024 Finals MVP Jaylen Brown in a single offseason that has instantly reshaped the Eastern Conference hierarchy.

The moves come after Philadelphia was swept in the 2026 Eastern Conference Semifinals by the eventual NBA champion New York Knicks — a humbling exit that prompted President of Basketball Operations Daryl Morey to overhaul the roster. As [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/sports/nba/76ers/2026/07/29/lebron-james-76ers-offense-options-jaylen-brown/91084006007/) noted, the new-look starting five of Tyrese Maxey, VJ Edgecombe, Jaylen Brown, LeBron James, and Joel Embiid "rises to the top tier" of the conference.

## The Signing and the Trade

James, the NBA's all-time leading scorer, signed a two-year, $8 million contract — the lowest salary of his legendary career. [The Athletic](https://www.nytimes.com/athletic/7471625/2026/07/26/lebron-james-sixers-deal-official-dalen-terry-waived/) confirmed the NBA officially updated its transaction log on July 26, listing: "Philadelphia 76ers signed forward LeBron James to a Contract." To accommodate the deal, the 76ers waived wing Dalen Terry, who averaged 4.1 points, 1.6 rebounds and 1.6 assists in 14 games for Philadelphia last season.

The James signing was the second blockbuster of the 76ers' offseason. On July 1, Philadelphia traded Paul George and a package of draft picks to the Boston Celtics for Brown, ending the forward's decade-long tenure in Boston. According to [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/jaylen-brown-paul-george-celtics-76ers-trade-5ecadfddba89a65c960d4742e2b9463c), the deal included draft capital that could become two first-round picks and two second-round picks. Boston had attempted to trade Brown for Giannis Antetokounmpo earlier in the offseason before pivoting to the Philadelphia deal.

Philadelphia also signed veteran guard Kentavious Caldwell-Pope, reuniting him with James — the two were teammates on the 2020 Los Angeles Lakers championship team. In a subsequent salary cap move, [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/nba/article/76ers-reportedly-trade-f-johni-broome-pick-to-clippers-for-cash-to-get-under-both-aprons-after-lebron-james-signing-210308814.html) reported that the 76ers traded forward Johni Broome and a second-round pick to the Los Angeles Clippers for cash considerations to get under both the first and second salary aprons.

## An Offensive Revolution

The offensive possibilities are staggering. Four of the five starters — Maxey, Brown, James, and Embiid — are capable of being primary scorers, while three can initiate the offense. USA Today's Lorenzo Reyes highlighted that "the reality is the Sixers should have a much more fluid offense with multiple ball-handlers, because when teams boast different players with the ability to create, it leads to tremendous offensive versatility."

The transition game could be particularly devastating. Last season, the top three players in the NBA in converted transition field goals were Maxey (171), Brown (167), and James (164). The league leaders in total fastbreak points were Maxey (382) and James (343), with Brown ranking 10th at 253. All three now wear the same uniform.

Brown, who ranked third in the NBA in usage rate (35.1) last season — a career-best — will need to adapt his game most significantly. As USA Today noted, "Brown will have to keep finding ways to move without the ball, cut to the basket and find soft gaps in defenses for perimeter shots." Playing alongside James and Maxey, however, should allow Brown to let the ball find him rather than forcing creation.

At 41 years old and entering his record 24th NBA season, James is expected to transition from primary scorer to elite distributor. USA Today observed that "the thing James does best is distribute," noting that he "wields exceptional court vision and anticipation and has an encyclopedic archive of sets and offensive actions."

## Contract Flexibility and Risks

James' contract notably lacks a no-trade clause — he was ineligible because NBA rules require four consecutive seasons with the team signing the contract. As the [NY Post](https://nypost.com/2026/07/27/sports/lebron-james-76ers-contract-does-not-have-a-no-trade-clause-heres-why-philadelphia-could-still-trade-him/) reported, the deal includes a player option for Year 2 and a 15% trade kicker, but "without James' power to veto a trade, Philadelphia retains the right to trade the four-time NBA Champion should their season go sideways." At roughly $4 million per season, the NY Post described James as "the biggest bargain in professional sports," noting his value in ticket sales, merchandise, and sponsorships far exceeds his salary.

## Questions Ahead

While the offensive firepower is undeniable, concerns remain. Philadelphia's defense may need work with so many new pieces developing chemistry. Nick Nurse faces the challenge of managing rotations among four high-usage stars. Philadelphia's offensive rating last season was 114.3, ranked 16th in the NBA — a number that should improve dramatically, but defensive cohesion remains an open question.

## What's Next

The 76ers' overhaul has dramatically shifted the balance of power in the Eastern Conference. The defending champion Knicks, a Celtics team that pivoted to Paul George, and the Milwaukee Bucks all face a significantly tougher path through Philadelphia. If the experiment succeeds, the 76ers could be championship favorites for years. If it falters, the lack of a no-trade clause gives Philadelphia a rare escape hatch — a fitting insurance policy for a team that has left nothing to chance this offseason.
