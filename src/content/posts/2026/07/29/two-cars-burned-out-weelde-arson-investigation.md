# Two Cars Burned Out in Weelde as Arson Investigation Begins

Two vehicles were completely destroyed by fire in the early hours of Wednesday morning in Weelde, a town in the municipality of Ravels, Antwerp province. The Antwerp public prosecutor’s office has opened an investigation into suspected arson and is appealing for witnesses.

The fire was discovered around 3:00 AM at the intersection of Molenstraat and Scheyfstraat, opposite the town hall in a residential area. A local resident returning from his night shift noticed the flames, alerted emergency services, and moved his own vehicle which was parked nearby, according to [RTV](https://www.rtv.be/actualiteit-actualiteit-en-nieuws/twee-voertuigen-uitgebrand-weelde-parket-start-onderzoek).

## Context

Weelde is a small town in the Kempen (Campine) region of Antwerp province, near the Dutch border. The resident who discovered the blaze described the neighborhood as a “very quiet neighborhood,” underscoring the shock such an incident has caused in the close-knit community.

## Key Developments

Firefighters arrived promptly at the scene but were unable to prevent both vehicles from sustaining severe damage. One car was significantly damaged at the front, while the other was mainly affected at the rear.

“Two cars are burned out. The investigation into the cause of the fire is still ongoing,” said Lieselotte Claessens, spokesperson for the Antwerp Public Prosecutor’s Office, as reported by [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/07/29/weelde-2-auto-s-uitgebrand-onderzoek/). “The lab and a fire expert were on site.”

The forensic laboratory and a fire expert were dispatched to examine the scene. While VRT NWS reports that arson is suspected but not yet confirmed, RTV states that investigators have determined the fires were set intentionally. [HLN](https://www.hln.be/ravels/twee-autos-uitgebrand-in-weelde-parket-onderzoekt-mogelijk-kwaad-opzet~a376a174/) reports that foul play has not been ruled out. The differing accounts may reflect the evolving nature of the investigation as more information becomes available.

## Analysis

Vehicle arson is a recurring concern in Belgium, particularly in the Antwerp region. While this incident is relatively small in scale compared to other major fire events reported across the country in July 2026, its impact on the local community of Weelde is significant. The targeted or random nature of the fire remains unknown, and no motive has yet been suggested by authorities.

Authorities have not disclosed the ownership of the two vehicles or whether they were specifically targeted. No suspects have been named at this stage.

## What’s Next

The investigation remains active, with forensic analysis ongoing. Prosecutors are appealing to the public for any information that could assist the inquiry. Residents with relevant details or surveillance footage from the area are encouraged to come forward as authorities work to determine whether this was a targeted act or a random incident of arson.
