# Kavinsky, Synthwave Pioneer Behind 'Nightcall,' Dies at 50

Kavinsky, the French electronic musician whose synthwave anthem "Nightcall" became an indelible part of modern pop culture through the film *Drive*, has died at age 50. He was found dead at his home in Paris on the evening of July 28, according to [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/entertainment/music/2026/07/29/kavinsky-death-dj-nightcall-drive-french/91088773007/). The Paris prosecutor's office has opened an investigation into the cause of death, with French media reporting that he had complained of headaches in preceding days and authorities suspect a possible stroke.

## The Man Behind the Mask

Born Vincent Pierre Claude Belorgey on July 31, 1975, in Seine-Saint-Denis, France, Kavinsky was more than a musician—he was a carefully constructed alter ego. According to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kavinsky), the character had red eyes and blue skin, with a fictional backstory of having crashed his Ferrari Testarossa in 1986 before returning as a zombie to make electronic music. "Showing my own face holds absolutely no interest for me," Belorgey once said. "I'd much rather make music and tell a story."

Kavinsky emerged in the mid-2000s as part of the second wave of the influential "French Touch" electronic music scene, alongside artists like Justice and SebastiAn. He toured with Daft Punk in 2007 and released his debut EP "Teddy Boy" in 2006 on the Record Makers label.

## 'Nightcall' and Global Breakthrough

Kavinsky's defining moment came when his 2010 single "Nightcall" was used in the opening credits of Nicolas Winding Refn's 2011 film *Drive*, starring Ryan Gosling. The atmospheric track, co-produced by Daft Punk's Guy-Manuel de Homem-Christo and featuring vocals from Brazilian singer Lovefoxxx, became a cult classic. As [BBC News](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c204n74xyv3o) reported, Kavinsky said watching the film for the first time and seeing where his song was placed was "one of the best moments of my life."

He released his debut studio album *OutRun* in 2013, followed by *Reborn* in 2022 after a seven-year hiatus. The latter included a collaboration with Canadian superstar The Weeknd on a remix of "Odd Look," further cementing his cross-generational appeal.

## Olympic Resurgence

In a remarkable second act, Kavinsky performed "Nightcall" with Belgian singer Angèle and French band Phoenix at the closing ceremony of the Paris 2024 Olympics. The performance triggered a massive global resurgence, making the track the most Shazamed song in a single day in the app's history. A new official studio version featuring Angèle and Phoenix was released in September 2024, achieving Diamond certification from SNEP and reaching No. 3 on the French singles chart.

## Tributes from Icons

The news of Kavinsky's death prompted an outpouring of tributes from both French political leaders and international music stars. French President Emmanuel Macron praised Kavinsky as "a source of French pride forever." Culture Minister Catherine Pégard wrote that "France has lost one of its most unique voices," adding that his music will "continue to transcend generations and borders."

David Guetta called the musician "a true icon of French electronic music," thanking him "for creating a sound that was entirely your own." The Weeknd, who collaborated with Kavinsky, wrote on X: "An inspiration to so many, including myself. I will continue to honour your name and your music. Rest easy, Kavinsky." Paris Mayor Emmanuel Grégoire paid tribute to "a child of Paris" who "carried the colours of the French electronic scene high across the world."

## A Lasting Legacy

Kavinsky's music—a fusion of pulsing synthwave, French house, and cinematic nostalgia—helped define a genre and introduced an entire generation to the sounds of 1980s-inspired electronic music. His "Nightcall" has been streamed hundreds of millions of times, covered by artists from London Grammar to Inhaler, and sampled by Childish Gambino. The song has also appeared in television series like *The Lincoln Lawyer* and *Riverdale*.

The investigation into the exact cause of his death remains ongoing. What is certain, however, is that Kavinsky's music will continue to transcend the boundaries of time and genre, a testament to an artist who crafted a world as unique as the character he created.
