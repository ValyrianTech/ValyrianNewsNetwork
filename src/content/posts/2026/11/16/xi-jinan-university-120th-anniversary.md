# Xi Hails Jinan University's 120th Anniversary

President Xi Jinping has sent a congratulatory letter to Jinan University on the occasion of its 120th anniversary, extending his greetings to all faculty, staff, students and alumni, Xinhua reported. In the letter, released on the morning of November 16, Xi framed the Guangzhou-based institution's mission around its distinctive role as a bridge to overseas Chinese communities and to the young people of Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan.

## What the Letter Says

According to the [Xinhua news report](https://www.news.cn/politics/leaders/20261116/c264eb9bb4984be6b314f215351e0690/c.html), Xi — in his concurrent roles as General Secretary of the Communist Party of China Central Committee, State President and Chairman of the Central Military Commission — offered his congratulations on the university's founding anniversary.

The letter strikes a forward-looking tone. "At this new starting point, I hope Jinan University will be guided by the Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, carry forward its patriotic tradition, maintain its distinctive educational characteristics, strengthen the training of young talent from Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan and overseas Chinese communities, inherit and promote Chinese culture, and promote friendly exchanges between China and other countries," Xi wrote, per the [full text of the letter](https://www.news.cn/politics/leaders/20261116/20c926b98fbd4d61b304fa22a52a6400/c.html).

He added that the university should aim to raise the quality of education and teaching, contributing to "rallying overseas Chinese hearts and strengths and advancing Chinese-style modernization."

## A Century of Overseas Chinese Education

Founded in 1906 as Jinan Xuetang in Nanjing, Jinan University is often described as China's first institution of higher learning dedicated to overseas Chinese education. Over the past 120 years it has weathered an unsettled history — repeatedly relocating across Shanghai, Jianyang in Fujian, and finally Guangzhou — while building a reputation as a flagship destination for students of Chinese descent from around the world.

Xinhua noted that the university has, over those 120 years, "trained large numbers of outstanding talents for more than 170 countries in the world and for the Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan regions." By the university's own accounting, that figure exceeds 500,000 graduates across the region and the global Chinese diaspora.

The institution now operates across what it calls "three cities, five campuses" — spanning Guangzhou, Zhuhai and Shenzhen in the prosperous Pearl River Delta.

## A Message Rooted in United-Front Priorities

The anniversary letter is a routine item in China's leadership protocol, but its content is closely watched because it signals governing priorities. The phrase at its centre — "training of young talent from Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan and overseas Chinese communities" — sits squarely within Beijing's long-running united-front agenda of cultivating cultural and political ties with these populations.

Xi's personal engagement with the university is not new. He inspected Jinan University in 2018 and delivered a speech that the school has since described as charting the course of its modern development. The 2026 letter reads as both an affirmation of that direction and a restatement of expectations.

Notably, the letter places a university anniversary within the broader national narrative of "Chinese-style modernization," the overarching policy framing of the Xi era. That rhetorical choice elevates a purely academic milestone into a statement about national development and the role of the diaspora in it.

## Coordinated Dissemination

The letter was rapidly syndicated across the party and state media system — from the CCP member education portal to the Central Party School, the political-legal system's China Peace Net, and the Liaison Office of the Central People's Government in Hong Kong — a pattern characteristic of directive-style communications carrying institutional weight.

The university was already marking its 120th year with an extensive programme of events. A year earlier, at its 119th anniversary on November 16, 2025, the institution launched a countdown and unveiled the anniversary theme "120-year Jinan, its teachings reach the four seas," alongside a commemorative announcement across its campuses. A "High-Quality Development Conference" was scheduled for the anniversary date itself.

## What to Watch

The immediate question is whether the letter translates into concrete commitments — expanded enrolment quotas for Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan students, new overseas-Chinese programmes, or dedicated funding streams. Reporting on donations earmarked for the anniversary has circulated, but figures should be treated cautiously pending confirmation from primary university or official sources.

Beyond the campus, the letter will be read for signals about Beijing's united-front priorities heading into 2027, particularly in how it chooses to engage the younger generation of the Chinese diaspora — a constituency that Jinan University, with its 120-year mandate, is uniquely positioned to reach.
