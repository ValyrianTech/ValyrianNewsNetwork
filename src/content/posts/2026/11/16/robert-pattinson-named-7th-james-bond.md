# Robert Pattinson Named the 7th James Bond

Robert Pattinson. Robert Pattinson. After years of rampant speculation, Amazon MGM Studios announced Monday that the 40-year-old English actor will become the seventh official James Bond, succeeding Daniel Craig in one of cinema's most iconic roles. The casting ends the longest hiatus in the franchise's 64-year history and marks the first Bond selection made without the hand-picking of the Broccoli family, which ceded creative control to Amazon in 2025.

The news, [reported by the Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/new-james-bond-4545b257a9924895a69623332db50c97), was unveiled on the official 007 social media accounts at roughly 3 p.m. GMT. All prior content was abruptly wiped from the accounts, replaced by a repeating typographic message — "Identity confirmed" — before a first-look image of Pattinson in Bond's trademark tuxedo, holding a gun, was released.

"James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honour of a lifetime," Pattinson said in a statement. "To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement."

## A long search, a star-powered choice

Pattinson emerged from a drawn-out casting process overseen by producers Amy Pascal, the former Sony Pictures chief behind "Spider-Man," and David Heyman, steward of the "Harry Potter" and "Paddington" franchises. Casting director Nina Gold ran the screen tests, and the final round reportedly came down to a shortlist that included Callum Turner, Jack Lowden, Nicholas Hoult and Josh O'Connor.

According to [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/11/robert-pattinson-james-bond-role-amazon-mgm-studios-1237225184/), producers had long been expected to favor a younger, less-proven actor, but instead chose "one of the best-known actors of his generation." The outlet reported that the studio wanted at least one major star in the mix — and Pattinson, already headlining another franchise in "The Batman," fit the bill.

At 40, Pattinson is relatively old to begin as 007, though Craig was 38 when he debuted in 2006's "Casino Royale."

## From Twilight to 007

Pattinson's career has traced an unusual arc. He broke through as Cedric Diggory in "Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire" and achieved global superstardom as vampire Edward Cullen in the "Twilight" series, before deliberately pursuing auteur-driven independent films — the Safdie brothers' "Good Time," Robert Eggers' "The Lighthouse" and Bong Joon Ho's "Mickey 17" among them.

He returned to blockbuster franchise work with "The Batman" in 2022, and has drawn rave reviews in 2026 for his villainous turn in Christopher Nolan's "The Odyssey" and his portrayal of "To Catch a Predator" host Chris Hansen in "Primetime." He is currently in production on "The Batman Part II."

Director Denis Villeneuve, who will helm the still-untitled 26th Bond film, said the choice was difficult but correct. "We were surrounded by extraordinary talent, but there can only be one 007," he said. "He has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new. I cannot wait to begin."

## A new era under Amazon

The casting arrives at a pivotal moment for the franchise. Amazon acquired Bond's longtime studio, MGM, for $8.5 billion in 2022, and in 2025 paid more than $1 billion to take creative control from Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson, the longtime stewards. Bond 26 is the first film since 1962's "Dr. No" in which a member of the Broccoli family did not handpick the star.

"Today marks a defining moment for our studio and a significant milestone in the history of James Bond as we proudly introduce Robert Pattinson as the next 007," said Mike Hopkins, head of Prime Video and Amazon MGM Studios.

Some fans remain skeptical of the Amazon-controlled era, but the studio has moved to reassure traditionalists. The creative team — Villeneuve directing, Steven Knight ("Peaky Blinders") writing the script, Pascal and Heyman producing — together with the classic 007 imagery of the reveal, signals continuity with the Eon era.

## What's next

No production start date has been announced, though shooting is expected to begin in 2027. According to a Puck report cited by the AP, Pattinson is signed to a three-movie deal, though Amazon MGM declined to comment on the terms. Producers must now fill out the rest of the cast, including a new "Bond lady," the villain, and the roles of M and Q.

As [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/film/2026/nov/16/new-james-bond-announced-and-the-names-pattinson-robert-pattinson) noted, the official Bond films have grossed $7.8 billion worldwide, making the franchise the fifth highest-grossing in history. With Pattinson's appointment, Amazon has placed one of its most valuable properties in the hands of a proven star with blockbuster appeal and indie credibility — a bet that the next phase of 007 will be as lucrative as it is scrutinized.
