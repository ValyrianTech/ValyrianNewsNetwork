# GTA VI Finally Arrives After a 13-Year Wait

After more than a decade of anticipation, *Grand Theft Auto VI* is finally here. The game became playable at midnight local time on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with players in Asia and other regions starting hours before the U.S. release, according to [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/grand-theft-auto-6-video-game-release-6e255c930da6de820e539721f4682253). The launch caps a 13-year wait since *Grand Theft Auto V* and is poised to become one of the biggest entertainment releases in history.

The stakes are enormous. Rockstar Games is following one of the bestselling video games ever made, and the question hanging over launch day is whether the blockbuster franchise can meet the cultural moment it has built.

## Back to Vice City

The game returns to Vice City, Rockstar's fictionalized version of Miami, and expands into the fictional state of Leonida, a stand-in for Florida. At its center are two new protagonists: Lucia Caminos, recently released from prison, and Jason Duval, a former soldier drawn into drug running in the Leonida Keys. Their relationship-driven story marks a major departure from *GTA V*, which followed three playable protagonists.

Rockstar has promoted Leonida as its largest and most detailed open world yet, spanning beaches, highways, wetlands, nightlife districts and smaller South Florida-inspired communities. Developers immersed themselves in the region, driving through Miami and the Florida Keys, flying over the Everglades, and researching the area's history and architecture.

The game's scale extends to its technical footprint. "The game file size at 212 gigabytes is unprecedented," said Kurt Rodgers, network strategy manager for Chorus New Zealand. "That's more than a quarter of the average household's data usage in an entire month, all in one go."

## A Franchise of Rare Scale

The *Grand Theft Auto* franchise has sold more than 470 million units worldwide, and *GTA V* alone accounts for more than 230 million of those and generated $1 billion in retail sales in just three days. The long wait for a sequel is partly a product of *Grand Theft Auto Online*, the multiplayer component that has kept players engaged for years through updates and in-game purchases.

The hype has been quantifiable. The first *GTA VI* trailer drew more than 93 million YouTube views in its first 24 hours, while parent company Take-Two Interactive has said the second trailer generated more than 475 million views across platforms in a single day. The game also earned eight nominations for the Game Awards, announced Tuesday, including game of the year, game direction and narrative. The awards will be presented Dec. 10.

## Miami Leans In

Miami and surrounding communities have embraced their connection to Vice City, using the release as a marketing opportunity. Miami-Dade County entered into a $3.9 million promotional deal with Rockstar that includes a giant "Welcome to Vice City" display atop the Miami Heat's Kaseya Center, and the NBA team is scheduled to wear black-and-pink Vice City uniforms on the eve of release, [AP reported](https://apnews.com/article/grand-theft-auto-vi-miami-vice-city-1441b5de7d3f458ad08626193d645a46).

The campaign has not been universally welcomed. Some South Florida officials have questioned whether public resources should be used to promote a franchise known for car theft, gunplay and other criminal activity. Supporters counter that the global spotlight offers the region a rare chance to capitalize on its cultural cachet.

Local players are watching closely. "I've seen the trailers, the story looks phenomenal," said lifelong Miamian Felix Jarquin. "I'm waiting to see how they incorporate that to the environments that we have here in Miami. I want to see, did they do their homework?"

## A Global, Rolling Launch

In Wellington, New Zealand, players stayed up to be among the first to try the game. Adam Simmons, 50, a director of photography, launched it in his living room as soon as it unlocked. "The gameplay dynamics are just amazing and the graphics are great and you can tell that the storytelling is really deep," he said. "You really feel like you are in a world."

In Seoul, a giant advertisement draped the side of a building, while Warner Music planned a launch party themed around the game's soundtrack. Rockstar also commissioned an original adaptive score from Grammy-winning French electronic duo Justice, designed to shift depending on where players are and what they are doing. A 30-track *Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score* is scheduled for release Dec. 11.

## What's Next

Despite years of trailers, Rockstar has kept significant portions of the game under wraps, including the full voice cast behind Lucia and Jason. Actor Stephen Root has confirmed to the AP that he appears in the game, though his role has not been publicly identified. A PC release is expected in 2027.

For now, the answers will emerge from players in Asia and Europe as midnight arrives in their regions, followed by North America hours later. Whether *GTA VI* can convert its extraordinary hype into lasting cultural and commercial staying power is the open question — and the world is about to find out.
