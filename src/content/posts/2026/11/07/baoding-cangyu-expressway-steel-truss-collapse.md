# Steel Truss Collapse Halts Hebei Expressway After Fanfare

A steel truss bridge span being installed over the Cangyu Expressway (G1812 Baocang Expressway) in Baoding, Hebei province, collapsed onto the roadway on the morning of November 7, 2026, injuring four people and severing traffic in both directions on a major regional artery. The incident occurred one day after the provincial government publicly announced that the bridge was on the verge of completion, according to an [official notice](https://news.sina.com.cn/zx/2026-11-07/doc-inixwxpy4714164.shtml) issued by the Qingyuan District People's Government.

## What Happened

The collapse struck at roughly 10:50 a.m. at the construction site of an overpass spanning the expressway in the Cangzhou direction at kilometer marker K105+550. Per the official notice, the bridge body detached during steel truss jacking operations — the technically demanding process of pushing the structure into its final position.

Preliminary verification found that three construction workers and one passing truck driver were injured, with two of the construction workers reported to be seriously hurt. All injured were sent to hospital for treatment.

"After the incident, the district Party committee and government attached great importance and immediately organized health, emergency, public security, and fire departments to carry out casualty treatment, traffic diversion, and hazard inspection," the notice stated. "At present, on-site handling is being intensified to ensure the Cangyu Expressway resumes traffic as soon as possible."

## Traffic Disruption

The collapse halted traffic in both directions, according to [China Road Network via Jiemian](https://k.sina.cn/article_6192937794_17120bb4202002w8r4.html), which reported at 12:36 p.m. that the highway was closed at K105+550 with restoration time undetermined. Earlier reports described a passing truck's cab being damaged as the bridge came down, and the road beneath was left blocked and congested.

## The Timing

The disruption is made more pointed by what preceded it. On November 6 — the day before the collapse — the Hebei provincial government's official website published an announcement titled "South Third Ring about to be connected; Baoding's third ring about to 'complete the circle.'" The report described how, on November 5, the steel truss beam had been slowly jacked into position over the expressway, marking what officials called the final key node of the Baoding South Third Ring project.

Wang Ming, deputy general manager of Baotong Highway Survey and Design Co., Ltd. under Baoding Transport Development Group, was quoted in that announcement describing the remaining work. "The longitudinal jacking of the steel truss bridge is nearing completion; after the lateral shifting is in place, full connection of the South Third Ring will be achieved in the coming days," he said, as reported by [Wind News](https://baijiahao.baidu.com/s?id=1881181088252271257).

The collapse therefore occurred during the very final phase of positioning — the lateral shifting of the truss — the step officials had just publicly framed as the last hurdle before completion.

## Analysis: A Credibility Test

The coincidence of timing has made the incident a sensitive one. The juxtaposition of a celebratory announcement with a structural failure within 24 hours has fueled public skepticism online, where commenters on the Sina report called for accountability and a thorough investigation.

Technically, the failure occurred during jacking and lateral shifting, one of the most challenging phases of bridge erection. During these operations, the structure sits on temporary supports and is subject to transient loads that differ sharply from its final in-service condition. Investigators will likely examine the jacking equipment, temporary support design, weld and connection quality, and construction sequencing.

Several questions remain unresolved. The root cause — whether design, construction method, equipment failure, material defect, or a supervision lapse — has not been determined. The construction contractor and supervision unit were not named in the available reporting. It is also unclear whether the project was under pressure to meet a public deadline tied to its high-profile "ring completion" narrative.

## What's Next

On-site crews are working to restore traffic on the Cangyu Expressway, though no timeline has been given. The Qingyuan District authorities said the cause investigation and aftermath work are continuing. For Baoding, the incident interrupts a flagship infrastructure milestone at the moment it was being celebrated — and places scrutiny on the safety and oversight standards behind the city's push to complete its third ring. What the investigation reveals about accountability and process will matter well beyond this single span.
