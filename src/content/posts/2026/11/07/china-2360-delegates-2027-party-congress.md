# China Sets 2,360 Delegates for 2027 Party Congress

The Central Committee of the Communist Party of China has issued a notice formally deploying the election of delegates to the party's 21st National Congress, setting a quota of 2,360 representatives to be chosen by 38 electoral units nationwide. According to [Xinhua News Agency](https://www.news.cn/politics/zywj/20261107/52f31843c5b2424c9cc5733fff7e1838/c.html), the congress will convene in Beijing in the second half of 2027, a timetable fixed at the 20th Central Committee's Fifth Plenary Session.

The notice, released on 7 November 2026, is a routine but consequential procedural milestone. It launches an eight-month selection process running until the end of June 2027 that will determine who attends the party's highest decision-making forum — and, in turn, who participates in choosing the next Central Committee.

## A Standard Document With a Disciplined Message

On its face, the notice follows the well-worn template of pre-congress organizational documents: it fixes the delegate quota, names the electoral units, describes the selection methodology, and sets the vetting criteria and deadline. Yet its emphasis reveals much about the political mood heading into 2027.

The notice repeatedly stresses that "political standards come first" (政治标准放在首位), requiring delegates to demonstrate fidelity to Xi Jinping's leadership through the party's established formulations — the "two establishes," the "four consciousnesses," the "four confidences," and the "two upholds." Those judged politically unqualified face a "one-vote veto" (一票否决).

Equally pointed is the integrity screening. The notice orders strict measures to prevent the "nominated while ill" and "elected while ill" (带病提名、带病当选) — party shorthand for candidates carrying undisclosed corruption or disciplinary problems. This disciplinary focus echoes the Fifth Plenum, which in October adopted a major decision on "persistently advancing full and rigorous party governance," as [China Peace](https://www.chinapeace.gov.cn/chinapeace/c100007/2026-10/29/content_12861394.shtml), the outlet of the party's Political and Legal Affairs Commission, reported.

## How Delegates Are Chosen

The selection method is described as "bottom-up, combining upper and lower levels, repeated deliberation, and level-by-level selection." Candidates are subject to differential vetting (差额考察) with advance notice of inspection, and the final vote is a differential — or competitive — election in which the margin must exceed 15 percent.

The notice also sets structural requirements aimed at breadth. It calls for guaranteed representation from the production and work front line, recommends outstanding workers, farmers, and technical personnel, and requires that women and ethnic minority delegates occupy a certain proportion. Representatives are to be drawn from across the economy, science and technology, defense, political-legal affairs, education, propaganda, culture, health, sports, and social management — plus the "new-employment groups" (新就业群体), a category that captures platform-economy workers such as delivery riders, ride-hailing drivers, and livestreamers.

According to [Xinhua](https://m.12371.gov.cn/content/2026-11/07/content_517601.html), Xi Jinping personally chaired the Politburo Standing Committee and Politburo meetings that set the overall requirements and goals for the work, underscoring the leadership's direct involvement in the process.

## Learning From Past Scandals

Perhaps the most striking passage is the notice's explicit invocation of past electoral-fraud cases. It directs party organizations to use the vote-buying and election-sabotage scandals in Hengyang (Hunan), Nanchong (Sichuan), and Liaoning as "warning education" — cautionary tales to be studied and heeded.

Those cases, which surfaced between 2011 and 2016, involved large-scale bribery of delegates and led to mass disqualifications and prosecutions. Their renewed citation signals that the leadership remains alert to electoral corruption as it approaches the 2027 congress, and that discipline and supervision will be watchwords throughout the selection window.

## Continuity, With a Modest Expansion

The framework is notably consistent with the last congress cycle. The 20th Congress, held in 2022, also drew delegates from 38 electoral units and used the same above-15-percent differential margin, with 2,300 slots. The new figure of 2,360 represents an increase of 60 — an incremental adjustment consistent with gradual party membership growth rather than a signal of political change.

## What to Watch

For outside observers, the notice itself offers few surprises; its value lies in the signals it sends. The heavy stress on political loyalty and anti-corruption suggests the 21st Congress will be framed around internal consolidation and party discipline, continuing the theme set at the Fifth Plenum.

The next milestone is the completion of delegate elections by the end of June 2027. The congress itself, expected in the second half of 2027, will then elect the new Central Committee — the event that will ultimately reveal the leadership composition and policy direction for the years ahead. Until then, the selection process will unfold largely behind the party's internal organizational machinery, with the roster of 2,360 delegates as the first concrete indicator of how the next chapter takes shape.
