# 3 Injured in Des Moines Post Office Shooting; Suspect Held

Three people were injured in a shooting Thursday morning at the main U.S. Postal Service facility in Des Moines, Iowa, and a suspect was taken into custody shortly afterward, authorities said. Police said none of the injuries were believed to be life-threatening.

The shooting broke out on the second floor of the post office at 1165 Second Avenue, just north of downtown Des Moines, at about 9:10 a.m. local time, according to [AP News](https://apnews.com/article/post-office-shooting-des-moines-iowa-24f2e650c51325042d567b89aae5a53c). Officers arrived within roughly three minutes of the first 911 calls.

## What Authorities Have Said

"They're all at the hospital, they are being cared for right now," Sgt. Paul Parizek, a Des Moines Police Department spokesperson, said of the victims, according to the AP.

Parizek said the department "received multiple phone calls" from people inside the post office reporting that someone was shooting on the second floor. The suspect was pointed out by witnesses as people tried to leave the building and was arrested east of the post office, the [Des Moines Register](https://www.desmoinesregister.com/story/news/crime-and-courts/2026/11/05/active-shooter-post-office-des-moines-shooting/92624715007/) reported. Police said they did not believe there was a second shooter but were following protocol and searching the area to make sure no one else was hiding.

The facility where the shooting occurred is the city's main post office — a four-story regional sorting hub that also has a walk-in public service counter. It sits directly across the street from MercyOne Des Moines Medical Center, about a mile from the Iowa State Capitol. At least one injured person transported themselves to the hospital across the street.

## Multi-Agency Response and School Precaution

The incident drew a large, rapid response from multiple agencies. Along with the Des Moines Police Department, law enforcement on scene included the FBI, the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives, the Johnston and Pleasant Hill police departments, the Polk County Sheriff's Office, and the Iowa State Patrol, [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/05/us/des-moines-iowa-post-office-shooting) reported.

Des Moines Public Schools said police notified the district at 9:20 a.m. Its nearby elementary school, welcome center, and nutrition center were placed in "secure status," meaning no one was allowed in or out of the buildings. The district said the secure status was lifted after police notified them at 10 a.m. that a suspect was in custody. Second Avenue reopened to traffic around 10:45 a.m., though a heavy police presence remained and the post office stayed closed.

## Official Reactions

Iowa Gov. Kim Reynolds said she was in contact with the Department of Public Safety about the shooting. "The suspected shooter is in custody and the investigation is ongoing," she posted online, according to the AP. "We are monitoring the situation and our prayers are with everyone involved. Grateful for the immediate response by law enforcement."

U.S. Rep. Zach Nunn, the Republican whose district includes Des Moines, wrote on Facebook that "my prayers are with the victims, their families, and everyone impacted by this tragedy. Grateful to the law enforcement officers and first responders on the scene," CNN reported.

## Investigation Ongoing

Authorities had not publicly released the suspect's identity, a possible motive, or the circumstances that led to the shooting as of Thursday afternoon. The names of the injured were also not immediately made public, and charges were pending.

The shooting occurred the same week as the 2026 U.S. midterm elections, though there was no indication in available reporting that the two were connected. The case remains under investigation, and additional details — including the suspect's identity and any charges — are expected to emerge in the days ahead.

As of Thursday evening, officials said the scene was secure. Anyone with information was encouraged to contact the Des Moines Police Department.
