# Tencent's Hy4 Model Adds Vision as China AI Race Heats Up

Tencent Holdings Ltd. on Wednesday launched its latest large language model, Hunyuan Hy4, adding visual understanding and the ability to operate computers for the first time, as the Shenzhen-based tech giant seeks to narrow the gap with leading global AI developers, [Caixin Global reported](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2026-11-05/tencent-launches-upgraded-hy4-ai-model-with-visual-understanding-102501068.html). The release underscores Tencent's push to embed advanced AI in enterprise software and convert technical gains into commercial value amid intense competition from U.S. and domestic Chinese rivals.

## A Step Up From the Preview

Hy4 is the successor to Hy4 Preview, which Tencent released and open-sourced on Aug. 28, 2026. According to [Tencent's official announcement](https://www.tencent.com/tencent-launches-hy4-joining-the-ranks-of-the-worlds-leading-open-source-ai-models/), the full model carries forward the preview's mixture-of-experts architecture — 770 billion total parameters, 49 billion activated, and a context window of up to one million tokens — while adding capabilities that were absent from the earlier iteration.

The most notable addition is visual understanding, a first for the Hunyuan line. Tencent says Hy4 now supports fine-grained image processing, complex chart analysis, and mathematical visual reasoning. The model also introduces computer-use abilities, allowing it to interact with both command-line and graphical interfaces and to perform work and everyday tasks — capabilities Tencent says were sharpened through specialized training on professional software including Blender and CAD.

Beyond the headline features, Tencent claims broad improvements in instruction following, long-context comprehension, and long-horizon agentic tasks. It also reports meaningful gains on hallucination: the model cut its hallucination rate from 12.95% in Hy4 Preview to 7.2%, while errors stemming from unsupported assumptions and guessing fell from 23.1% to 5.6%.

## Built for Productivity — and Priced to Match

Tencent is positioning Hy4 less as a research artifact than as an engine for enterprise productivity, a strategy it describes as "model-product co-design." The model is being integrated across Tencent's software stack, including WorkBuddy, CodeBuddy, Yuanbao, the WeCom AI Assistant (Dayuan), and ima, with integration underway for QQ, Honor of Kings, Game of Peace, WeRead, and Tencent News. Its API is also being offered through third-party platforms such as OpenRouter, Hermes, and OpenCode.

On WorkBuddy benchmarks, Tencent reports Hy4 scored an average of 83.18 with a 96.15% pass rate, ranking first in nine of twelve scenarios in the mainland Chinese market. The company also cites gains in financial analysis and game development — areas where it trained the model on real-world workflows.

Despite the improvements, Tencent is holding pricing steady. Hy4 costs approximately US$0.90 per million input tokens, US$2.70 per million output tokens, and US$0.045 per million cached input tokens, unchanged from the preview. A more cost-efficient companion model, Hy4 Flash, is slated for release soon. To drive adoption, WorkBuddy and CodeBuddy will be free from Nov. 4 to 15, and Hy4 will be available on Tencent Cloud TokenHub at 50% off through Dec. 31, 2026.

## An Open-Source Play

Tencent frames Hy4 as "among the world's leading open-source AI models" and says it plans to release the model weights and system cards in the coming days, with an emphasis on cybersecurity and biological safety safeguards. The [earlier preview release](https://news.aibase.com/news/30698) was open-sourced across HuggingFace, GitHub, ModelScope, and GitCode, and this open-weight posture has become a defining feature of China's AI strategy.

As Caixin noted, Chinese models held the top four OpenRouter token rankings for the week of Nov. 4 — a marker of how prominent open-weight Chinese models have become in global developer usage. The pre-launch signal came at the Tencent Global Digital Ecosystem Summit on Oct. 29, when Senior Executive Vice President Dowson Tong announced that Hy4 and Hy4 Flash were imminent, [according to IT之家](https://www.ithome.com/1/019/328.htm).

## What It Means

The release reflects a broader dynamic in the global AI race. Chinese developers are releasing capable open-weight models at a rapid clip, often at aggressive prices, while U.S. frontier labs push the performance frontier. Tencent's strategy of tying its model tightly to productivity software — and giving it away cheaply to seed adoption — is a bet that commercial integration, not benchmark leadership alone, will determine who captures the enterprise market.

Investors offered a modest but positive reception. Tencent's Hong Kong-listed shares closed up 1.1% at HK$401.6, giving the company a market capitalization of about HK$3.65 trillion, Caixin reported.

## What to Watch

The key open questions are how Hy4 performs outside Tencent's own benchmarks — which are company-reported and not independently verified — and how quickly the promised model weights and safety documentation materialize. The arrival of Hy4 Flash will also test whether Tencent can defend its cost-efficiency claims against rivals such as DeepSeek, Kimi, and Zhipu's GLM line, all of which are competing hard for the same developers. For now, Hy4 signals that Tencent intends to be a serious contender — not just a participant — in the race to build and monetize the next generation of AI.
