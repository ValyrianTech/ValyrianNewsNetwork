# Petrol Jerrycan and Fireworks Found at Antwerp Church, One Person Arrested

A petrol-filled jerrycan with fireworks taped to it was found in front of a Catholic church in Antwerp on Sunday morning, prompting a bomb-disposal response and the arrest of a 23-year-old man, [VRT NWS reported](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/11/01/jerrycan-benzine-vuurwerk-kerk-lange-beeldekensstraat-antwerpen/). The federal prosecutor's office is leading the investigation, and authorities say the suspect's involvement is still under examination.

## What Happened

A passer-by alerted Antwerp police at around 8:00 a.m. on Sunday, 1 November, after spotting the jerrycan on the street in front of the Church of the Sacred Heart of Jesus (Kerk van het Heilig Hart van Jezus) on Lange Beeldekensstraat, in the city's Seefhoek district.

According to police spokesperson Kim Bastiaens, quoted by [HLN](https://www.hln.be/antwerpen/23-jarige-man-opgepakt-in-antwerpen-na-vondst-van-explosief-pakket-op-straat-voorbijganger-spot-jerrycan-met-vuurwerk-en-slaat-alarm~a72f7c1f/), the alert came in at 8 a.m. "It was a passer-by who warned us about a jerrycan on the street," Bastiaens said. "The DOVO bomb disposal service, the judicial lab and civil protection came to the scene to safely remove the explosive and for the forensic investigation."

The street was sealed off for roughly two to three hours between De Coninckplein and Dambruggestraat while the bomb disposal unit DOVO, forensic investigators and Civil Protection worked at the scene. The perimeter was later lifted and the street reopened.

During the morning, police arrested a 23-year-old man and seized a car. Authorities stressed that his involvement in the incident remains under investigation, and no motive has been officially confirmed. The [Belga news agency](https://www.msn.com/nl-be/nieuws/nationaal/jerrycan-met-benzine-en-vuurwerk-aangetroffen-in-antwerpen-een-persoon-gearresteerd/ar-AA2gwMQc) reported that the jerrycan was found in the Antwerp Seefhoek and confirmed the arrest and the seizure of a vehicle.

## Why the Church Matters

The Church of the Sacred Heart of Jesus, at Lange Beeldekensstraat 22, is a parish church begun in 1933. Since 2020 it has been shared between the local Roman Catholic community and the Belarusian Greek-Catholic community, which holds the parish of St. Josaphat and celebrates Sunday Divine Liturgy there. That shared use is relevant to the investigation: an attack on the building could potentially be aimed at either or both congregations.

## The Investigation

The involvement of the federal prosecutor's office signals that the case is being treated with elevated seriousness. In Belgium, the federal prosecutor typically handles cases with potential terrorism, organised-crime or cross-jurisdictional dimensions — though its involvement does not by itself confirm a terrorism angle, and no such link has been officially stated.

Several key questions remain unanswered. It is not yet established whether the device was intended as a targeted threat against the Belarusian community, a religiously motivated act, an act of vandalism or something else entirely. Authorities have not disclosed the suspect's identity, nationality, mental state or prior record, nor what role the seized car may have played. Whether the petrol-and-fireworks combination constituted a functional improvised device will depend on DOVO's technical assessment. It also remains to be seen what charges, if any, the federal prosecutor will bring.

## What to Watch

The coming days are likely to bring clarity on the suspect's profile and the direction of the federal probe. Statements from the Belarusian parish, the Catholic Church in Belgium and local authorities are still awaited, and community reaction in the diverse Seefhoek neighbourhood will be closely watched. For now, the street has reopened, one man is in custody, and investigators are working to determine what — if anything — the device was meant to target.
