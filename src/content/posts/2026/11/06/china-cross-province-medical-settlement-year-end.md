# China to Fully Enable Cross-Province Medical Settlement

China will fully implement direct settlement for cross-province medical treatment and medicine purchases before the end of 2026, the National Healthcare Security Administration (NHSA) announced, a step that would let patients use their medical insurance seamlessly outside their home province. The pledge, made at an NHSA press conference on November 5, centres on the upgraded "Medical Insurance Wallet 2.0," a virtual wallet on the national unified medical insurance platform, according to [Xinhua News](https://www.news.cn/politics/20261106/137fe180f9d942e183e117b64fa450d5/c.html).

## What the Wallet Does

The Medical Insurance Wallet is a mechanism for cross-province mutual aid of workers' individual medical insurance accounts. Established on the national unified medical insurance information platform, it uses virtual-quota management: a "mutual-aid initiator" sets a shared quota for an "assisted person," allowing funds from the worker's personal account to pay for near relatives' out-of-pocket costs at designated medical institutions and pharmacies, as well as to cover insurance enrolment fees.

Fan Weidong, director of the NHSA Medical Insurance Center, said the wallet breaks the geographic limits that previously confined mutual aid to within a single province. Workers' personal-account funds "can not only be shared with family within a province but also across provinces, converting 'dormant' personal-account funds into a 'family health fund,'" he said.

## Rapid Take-Up Since September Launch

The Wallet 2.0 version went live on September 29, 2026. As of November 4, it had facilitated 2.0261 million cross-province mutual-aid transactions worth 1.582 billion yuan, with cumulative spending on medical treatment and medicine reaching 53.23 million yuan and 727,000 person-times of resident insurance fees paid on behalf of relatives, totalling 277 million yuan.

The upgrade expanded eligibility in three ways: the circle of eligible relatives widened from spouses, parents and children to siblings, grandparents and grandchildren; the permitted uses grew from medical care and medicine purchases to resident medical insurance and long-term care insurance enrolment payments; and the operation was made more convenient through stronger identity verification, mutual-aid relationship binding, quota control and risk-warning functions.

## Broad Institutional Coverage

Nationwide, 749,500 designated medical institutions and pharmacies now support local wallet consumption, including 361,900 medical institutions (80.8 percent of the total) and 387,600 retail pharmacies (89.28 percent). Twenty-seven provinces have achieved full coverage of medical institutions, while 27 provinces have full coverage of retail pharmacies. A total of 344 pooling regions, or 87 percent of the national total, already support using Wallet 2.0 to pay insurance enrolment fees. Because institutions must gradually develop software interfaces, availability varies by location, and patients are advised to confirm in advance.

## A Growing Insured Population

Zhang Chenguang, deputy director of the NHSA's planning, finance and legal affairs department, said basic medical insurance coverage reached 1.328 billion people by the end of September 2026, including 391 million workers and 937 million residents—already exceeding the full-year 2024 level. By the end of October, coverage had reached the full-year 2025 level, and exceeding last year for the full year "is now a foregone conclusion," marking two consecutive years of positive growth.

In 2025, workers' personal accounts were used for 34.2578 million person-times of near relatives' intra-province resident insurance contributions, totalling 14.227 billion yuan. In Hunan province alone, by November 4, 11.3571 million people had enrolled for 2027 resident insurance; 64,214 of them paid through the wallet (25.6856 million yuan) and 971,700 through intra-province workers' account mutual aid (387 million yuan).

## What's Next

Fan said the NHSA will guide localities to accelerate the rollout of Wallet 2.0, offer offline assistance for the elderly and disabled, strengthen public information efforts, and reinforce fund supervision using big data and artificial intelligence to enhance anomaly monitoring and risk warning. The agency also aims to improve cross-province fund settlement and clearing mechanisms to "guard the people's 'money for seeing a doctor' and 'money for saving lives.'"

The reform has advanced in stages—intra-province mutual aid first, then cross-province sharing formalised nationwide in January 2026 by the NHSA and Ministry of Finance, the 2.0 upgrade in September, and now full direct settlement targeted for year-end. With 1.328 billion people covered and a vast migrant workforce moving between provinces, the portability of medical benefits has become a major livelihood issue—and a test of how efficiently China's pooled insurance funds can be put to work for families.
