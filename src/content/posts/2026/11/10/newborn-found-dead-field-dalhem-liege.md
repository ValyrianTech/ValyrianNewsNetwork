# Newborn Found Dead in a Field in Dalhem, Liège

The body of a newborn baby girl was discovered in a field near Warsage, a village in the municipality of Dalhem in Belgium's Liège province, on the afternoon of Sunday 8 November. The Liège public prosecutor's office has opened a judicial investigation into the death, and two local people were taken in for questioning, [VRT NWS reported](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/11/10/babylijkje-gevonden-in-veld-vlakbij-luik/).

## What Happened

According to the mayor of Dalhem, Benoit Defauwes (MR), the discovery was made at a spot known locally as 'Queue du Bois', at the edge of a meadow. The farmer's son spotted an unusual plastic garbage bag, poked at it with a piece of wood, and found the newborn's head inside. He immediately alerted the police.

As [L'Avenir/Belga](https://www.lavenir.net/regions/liege/dalhem/2026/11/10/macabre-decouverte-pour-un-promeneur-un-bebe-mort-retrouve-dans-une-prairie-a-dalhem-en-province-de-liege-LFUJ4G7P4RHIJG3I3S7Q7G6AXE/) reported, the infant was a baby girl who still had her umbilical cord attached. Police and the mayor went to the scene at once, and the fire service was brought in to search the area, including with drones.

According to the initial findings of the forensic physician, the body had been left there recently. The exact circumstances of the death — and how the infant came to be in the field — remain unknown.

## The Mayor's Account

Mayor Defauwes described receiving the news while attending a commemoration walk near a war memorial in Aubel. "Around 14:45 I noticed that the police duty officer had tried to reach me twice," he told VRT NWS. "When I called back, he brought me the tragic news that a baby's body had been found in a garbage bag."

He said the discovery had shaken the community. Calling it "staggering" that such a thing could happen in a quiet, rural area, he added: "This stirs up a lot of emotions in the village."

## Investigation and Questions

The Liège prosecutor's office has confirmed that a judicial investigation is under way but has released no further details — standard practice in the early phase of such inquiries. VRT NWS reported, citing its own information, that two people from the neighbourhood were taken in for questioning on the evening of Monday 9 November and were still being held by police at the time of reporting. It is not known whether they represent a lead to the baby's mother or parents. The French-language outlet L'Avenir did not report any detentions.

Several central questions remain open. A forensic examination and possible autopsy will be decisive in establishing whether the baby was born alive, and what caused her death. The identity of the mother is the central investigative lead. How prosecutors ultimately frame the case — as homicide, abandonment, or a non-culpable tragedy — will shape any charges and the wider public debate.

## A Rare but Recurring Tragedy

Belgium has provisions intended to prevent precisely this kind of outcome. Mothers may give birth anonymously under the so-called 'accouchement sous le secret', with a medical pathway designed to allow a child to be safely surrendered rather than abandoned. The discovery of a newborn in a garbage bag raises difficult questions about whether such mechanisms are known, understood, and used.

The abandonment of newborns is rare in Belgium and neighbouring countries, but when it does occur it tends to provoke significant public shock and appeals for the mother to come forward.

## What to Watch For

The coming days are likely to centre on the results of the forensic examination, the questioning of the two local people, and whether the mother can be identified. Dalhem, a rural municipality near the Pays de Herve plateau close to the Dutch and German borders, now finds itself the focus of a national story — and a community waiting for answers about how a newborn came to be left in a meadow.
