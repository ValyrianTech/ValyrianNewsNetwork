# Woman, 90, Drowns in Pond in Her Own Garden in Waasmunster

A woman of around 90 years old died on the morning of Thursday, 19 November after falling into a small pond in the garden of her home in Waasmunster, a municipality in East Flanders. She was discovered by her home nurse, who alerted the emergency services, according to [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/11/19/vrouw-90-verdronken-vijver-waasmunster/). Fire brigade and medical teams attempted to resuscitate her at the scene, but their efforts came too late.

## What happened

The woman, who lived alone, was found in a small pond at the rear of her garden on the Sint-Annastraat. Reporting by [HLN](https://www.hln.be/waasmunster/bejaarde-vrouw-verdrinkt-in-vijver-achter-haar-woning-in-waasmunster~a5d9e167/) indicates that it was her home nurse who discovered her in the water and raised the alarm. Emergency services arrived swiftly and began CPR, but the resuscitation attempt was unsuccessful and the woman died at the scene.

## A presumed accident

There is no indication of foul play. Current reporting treats the death as a tragic accident. VRT NWS reports that the woman had most likely slipped and fallen into the cold water and was probably no longer able to get out of the pond on her own. The circumstances surrounding the incident are still being examined, but neither source indicates that a criminal investigation has been opened.

## A neighbourhood in shock

Local residents reacted with dismay to the news. Neighbours described the woman as a gentle and sweet neighbour, HLN reported. Online, readers of the regional coverage expressed their condolences, with messages such as "Strength to the family. Sad" and "To meet your end like this after 90 years is terrible."

## A recurring household danger

The case highlights a recurring public-safety concern: the risk that garden ponds pose to elderly people who live alone. A slip near the water's edge, particularly into cold water, can quickly become life-threatening for someone who cannot self-rescue — and such drownings are typically treated by authorities as accidental. HLN has previously reported on elderly people found drowned in garden ponds in other Flemish municipalities, underscoring how often such household accidents occur.

## What's next

The precise circumstances of the death remain under examination. For now, the focus is on supporting the woman's family and the tight-knit Waasmunster community coming to terms with a sudden loss — and, more broadly, on the quiet but persistent danger that domestic ponds can present to vulnerable residents living on their own.
