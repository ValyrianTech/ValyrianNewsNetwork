# DC Gym Shooting Near White House Leaves 1 Dead, 4 Hurt

A gunman opened fire inside a fitness center in Washington, D.C.'s Logan Circle on Thursday morning, killing one man at the gym and wounding four other people before fleeing and taking his own life in a nearby apartment, police said. The shootings occurred around 8 a.m. roughly a mile north of the White House, rattling a busy, affluent neighborhood and triggering a large-scale police response.

## What Happened

According to [The Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/dc-shooting-logan-circle-3cee6a4ada875fcc18e932e536c63dcd), the gunman killed a man at the VIDA fitness center before fleeing and shooting four more people outside. Interim Metropolitan Police Department Chief Jeffery Carroll said one victim died at the gym and another was in critical condition.

The suspect later shot himself inside a seventh-floor apartment where he had been speaking by phone to police negotiators, Carroll said. A law enforcement official, speaking to the AP on condition of anonymity, confirmed the self-inflicted death.

## Witness Accounts

Witnesses described a sudden, chaotic scene. James Spellman, who was at the gym, said about 50 people were there when the shooting began.

"Within seconds there was a man who fell to the ground, and a pool of blood came out," he said. "One gym member rushed to help him. He was instantly dead. He had been shot in the neck."

Anthony Mirones, who was in the gym's cardio area, said people had to step over the victim's body as they fled. Another witness, Ryan Dubicki, told the AP he heard "about five bangs" from his nearby apartment and initially thought it was construction.

## A Neighborhood on Lockdown

Much of the 1500 block of 15th Street Northwest was cordoned off as police urged people to avoid the area, which includes the gym, St. Luke's Episcopal Church, residences and a Whole Foods supermarket. Schools were locked down, and SWAT teams and other officers trained their weapons on the roof of an apartment building and the street below.

Mayor Muriel Bowser said she was "devastated" by the loss of life in a "senseless act of gun violence." The federal Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives sent agents to assist, and the FBI's Washington field office said it was helping local police with the investigation.

## Broader Context

The shooting underscores persistent concerns about gun violence in the United States and arrives in a high-profile location just steps from the seat of federal government. Logan Circle has seen violence before: in early August 2025, a onetime member of President Donald Trump's Department of Government Efficiency was assaulted in the neighborhood during an attempted carjacking, according to the AP.

Days after that incident, Trump imposed direct federal control over the city, deploying hundreds of National Guard members to reduce crime—a deployment that continues today. The latest shooting is likely to renew debate over the federal role in policing the capital and the broader question of how to address gun violence in dense, high-traffic urban areas.

## What's Next

Police have not publicly released the suspect's identity or a definitive motive, and the investigation remains ongoing. Authorities have said they are examining a possible personal grievance connected to the apartment building involved. Carroll is expected to provide further updates as investigators piece together the sequence of events and interview witnesses.

For now, the neighborhood is left to reckon with a morning that turned a routine gym visit into the latest chapter in the capital's long struggle with gun violence.
