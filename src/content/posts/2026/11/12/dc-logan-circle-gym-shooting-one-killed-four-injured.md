# DC Shooting Near Logan Circle Gym Kills One, Injures Four

A gunman opened fire inside and outside a gym in Washington, D.C.'s Logan Circle neighborhood on Thursday morning, killing one person and wounding four others before barricading himself in an apartment above the fitness center and dying of a self-inflicted gunshot wound, police said.

Five people were shot, not counting the shooter, according to [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/washington-dc-shooting-gym-vida/). One victim was killed, one was in critical condition and three suffered non-life-threatening injuries. The shooting took place at and just outside Vida Fitness on the 1500 block of 15th Street NW, roughly one mile north of the White House.

## What Happened

Interim Police Chief Jeffery Carroll said the department received the call just before 8 a.m. and that officers responded within 30 seconds. The person who died was shot inside the gym, while other victims included members of a construction crew across the street and a person in the lobby or alley outside, Carroll said.

According to CBS News national security correspondent Nicole Sganga, the suspect opened fire in and around the gym, then retreated into a parking garage and ascended to the seventh floor of the apartment building above, where he barricaded himself in a unit believed to be his. The suspect is believed to have used a handgun.

Gunshots were heard for the better part of two hours as police negotiated with the man. Carroll said the suspect fired through the apartment door at officers on two occasions, and that while on the phone with negotiators, he shot himself. Officers forced entry after hearing the gunshot and found him suffering from a self-inflicted wound.

A fire was also set inside the building at some point during the incident, triggering sprinklers. Carroll said police were not certain who started it but that authorities "would kind of expect" it was the suspect.

## Motive Under Investigation

Carroll described the suspect as a White man and said police knew his identity but did not publicly release it. He is believed to have been a member of the gym. The chief said an investigation into motive was ongoing but that police were aware of a conflict between the man and the apartment building's homeowners association.

## A Heavily Federal Response

Multiple federal agencies responded because of the location's proximity to the White House, not because of any connection to the executive residence, officials said. The Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives dispatched its Washington Bureau, the FBI sent its Washington Field Office to assist, and the Secret Service, U.S. Marshals and U.S. Park Police also responded. Officials said there were no Secret Service protectees involved.

Streets were closed, residents were asked to avoid the area and a shelter-in-place order was issued. Nearby schools were placed on lockdown. Vida Fitness emailed members that its Logan Circle location would be closed until further notice.

## Witnesses Describe Chaos

Jennifer Christine, who was on a treadmill inside the gym when the shooting began, described a frantic escape. "There was no way of getting out of the gym other than going past that scene," she told reporters. "It was like chaos. It was horrific, honestly."

Another witness said he heard a loud bang that he first mistook for a ball popping before seeing blood on the floor as the gym was evacuated. "We all just started running and people are scattering everywhere," he said. Max Smith, who sheltered in a nearby bagel shop with about 10 other bystanders, told media he heard multiple shots around 8:30 a.m. "I'm very shaken up," he said. "You know, you don't expect it."

## What To Watch

Police have not released the suspect's name, and the reported homeowners association dispute has not been confirmed as the formal motive. The incident underscores how quickly violence can erupt in one of the capital's most densely populated and heavily patrolled commercial districts, drawing an unusually large federal response within blocks of the White House.

Authorities are asking anyone with information to come forward as the investigation into the events of Thursday morning continues.
