# Belfius App Hit by Payment Outage, Cause Unknown

Belgian bank Belfius is grappling with a mobile app outage that has left many customers unable to access their accounts or complete payments, the bank confirmed on Friday. The cause of the disruption remains unclear.

According to [Het Laatste Nieuws](https://www.hln.be/binnenland/belfius-bevestigt-technische-problemen-bankapp-voorlopig-onbereikbaar~afd5056b4/), the problems began around 10:30 on Friday morning, when customers started reporting that the Belfius banking app would not open. Those who managed to log in encountered an error message or a loading bar that stalled when they attempted to make a payment.

"We are doing everything we can to resolve this as quickly as possible," Belfius spokesperson Ulrike Pommée told the newspaper.

## Scope Limited to the App

For now, the disruption appears confined to the mobile application. Card payments and cash withdrawals continue to function, according to the bank. Reports of the outage mounted on the crowd-sourced tracking site Downdetector and via the newsroom's tip line.

The French-language outlet [7sur7](https://www.7sur7.be/belgique/des-problemes-de-paiement-constates-chez-belfius-la-cause-encore-floue~afd5056b4/) reported first signals at around 10:30 and quoted Pommée as saying a solution was being sought. The bank has not disclosed what triggered the fault.

## A Sensitive Subject

Belfius, the former Dexia Bank Belgium and wholly state-owned since its 2012 rebrand, relies on its app as a primary channel for millions of retail customers. The outage has stirred frustration among users who recall previous disruptions. One reader comment flagged by HLN captured the mood: "Yet another time. Never an explanation, never compensation." That sentiment reflects recurring irritation rather than a confirmed link to earlier incidents.

## What to Watch

The key questions remain unanswered: what caused the fault, whether the bank's website has also been affected, and how long a full restoration will take. The bank has so far declined to say whether the problem was an internal systems failure or something else. Customers can still pay by card and withdraw cash in the meantime, but those relying on the app for transfers face continued disruption until service is restored.
