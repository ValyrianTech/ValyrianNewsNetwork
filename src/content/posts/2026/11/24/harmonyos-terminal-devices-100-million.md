# HarmonyOS Terminal Devices Surpass 100 Million Units

Huawei Device Co., Ltd. announced on Tuesday that more than 100 million phones, tablets and computers now run its HarmonyOS operating system, a milestone that cements the self-developed platform's position within China's device ecosystem. The figure covers devices running HarmonyOS 5, 6 and 7, according to [Xinhua News](https://www.news.cn/tech/20261124/c1449f3e0fe24233a97e58d967b8a5f0/c.html), which reported the announcement from Shenzhen.

The company also disclosed that more than 460,000 applications and services are now available on the platform, and that registered developers have passed 12 million — numbers that together describe an ecosystem moving from survival to scale.

## A milestone five years in the making

HarmonyOS was first released in 2019, after U.S. restrictions cut Huawei off from Google's Android ecosystem. The company unveiled a native, Android-independent version — HarmonyOS NEXT — in October 2024, and by November 2026 the installed base across its consumer device lines had crossed the 100 million threshold.

Industry observers treat terminal scale as the decisive test of whether a new operating system can become self-sustaining. As the Xinhua report put it, an operating system is the infrastructure of the digital world, and the scale of terminal devices determines whether an ecosystem can be established at all.

## Beyond the phone

The reach of HarmonyOS now extends well past consumer handsets. OpenHarmony, the open-source foundation that underpins the platform, has gathered more than 14,000 contributors and produced more than 120 industry distributions, with deployments in the power, water and industrial sectors. That spread reflects a deliberate strategy of embedding the operating system into specific devices and business scenarios, from grid equipment to water utilities.

Partners have contributed more than 160 innovation capabilities that are now integrated into over 1,600 applications, with usage of the related functions rising 90-fold in a single year. More than 58,000 partners have connected to over 120 features such as tap-to-share and live window. On the consumer side, HarmonyOS applications now cover local services, outbound travel, e-commerce and retail, office productivity, transportation and gaming — with developers combining technical capabilities and business scenarios to produce a set of innovative and platform-exclusive experiences.

## 'A firm foothold'

Xia Yubin, director of the OpenHarmony Technology Club at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, framed the 100 million figure as a turning point. A brand-new, self-developed system surviving against entrenched incumbents is extremely difficult, he said, and the breakthrough carries threefold significance: a technological breakthrough achieved through independent innovation under constraints on key technologies; ecosystem prosperity driven by the virtuous cycle between applications and users; and talent cultivation that combines basic research with industrial practice.

That framing places the announcement squarely within China's broader push for technological self-reliance and the domestic substitution of foreign foundational software — an effort sharpened by export-control tensions.

## What to watch

The headline number is company-reported, relayed through the state-run Xinhua news agency, and independent third-party verification was not available. The 100 million figure also refers specifically to HarmonyOS 5, 6 and 7 devices rather than the cumulative total of all HarmonyOS installs since 2019, a distinction worth keeping in mind when comparing it with earlier projections.

The more consequential question is whether scale translates into sustained developer momentum. With 12 million registered developers and more than 460,000 applications, the platform now has the raw ingredients of a self-reinforcing ecosystem. Whether that ecosystem can generate the revenue and export reach that would make it a genuine global alternative to Android and iOS remains open — but the 100 million mark means that debate is no longer hypothetical.
