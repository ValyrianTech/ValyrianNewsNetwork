# China-ASEAN Week Opens in Chongqing, Ten Plans Released

China and the ten member states of ASEAN opened the 2026 China-ASEAN Week in Chongqing on November 23, releasing a package of ten cooperation plans spanning trade, technology, and people-to-people exchanges, according to [People's Daily](http://world.people.com.cn/n1/2026/1124/c1002-40839455.html). The event, organized around the theme "Deepening Strategic Cooperation, Connecting and Sharing Prosperity," arrives as the two sides mark the 35th anniversary of their dialogue relations and the fifth anniversary of their comprehensive strategic partnership.

## A Milestone Year for Dialogue

The opening ceremony served as a platform to showcase the depth of a relationship that has become central to regional economic life. According to the report carried by [Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/20261123/cedccf86a15b4476824b0681eb3e9ccc/c.html), China and ASEAN have been each other's largest trading partners for many consecutive years, a status the wire service described as a benchmark for regional practical cooperation.

The ten plans reach across a deliberately broad field: the economy and trade, science and technology innovation, connectivity, education, culture and tourism, agriculture, the rule of law, healthcare and medical services, media, and youth exchanges. The breadth signals an effort to bind the relationship together through many channels rather than relying solely on commerce.

## The Healthcare Plan and Its Targets

Among the plans, the most detailed is the Healthcare Co-building Plan, which proposes jointly building a China (Chongqing)-ASEAN Medical Exchange and Cooperation Center. The plan envisions exploring a medical cooperation alliance and working together in surgery, pediatrics, traditional Chinese medicine, health checkups, and mental health. It also commits to training 100 ASEAN medical personnel — a concrete, countable deliverable in a package that otherwise emphasizes frameworks and intent.

## Why Chongqing

The choice of Chongqing as host is itself a statement. The southwestern Chinese metropolis is a strategic node for the China-Singapore (Chongqing) Connectivity Initiative and the New International Land-Sea Trade Corridor, which links western China to Southeast Asia by rail and sea. Hosting the Week there underscores the city's role as a gateway between China's interior and the ASEAN economies to its south, tying the diplomatic calendar to the physical infrastructure that increasingly connects the two regions.

## A Calendar Built Around "Five Homelands"

The Week is structured around the vision of building what officials frame as the "Five Homelands" — a region that is peaceful, tranquil, prosperous, beautiful, and friendly. To fill that vision, organizers laid out six major categories comprising 30 flagship activities.

## Analysis: Deliverables in a Banner Year

The timing carries symbolic weight. Dialogue relations were established in 1991, and in November 2021, at the 30th anniversary summit, leaders announced the upgrade to a comprehensive strategic partnership. The 2026 plans are positioned as deliverables that give that partnership substance in its fifth year.

What distinguishes this package is not any single initiative but the ambition to coordinate across so many domains at once. Economic ties between China and ASEAN are already anchored by the China-ASEAN Free Trade Area and its ongoing upgrade — commonly referred to as FTA 3.0 — which frames much of the trade cooperation the plans reference. By adding healthcare, education, media, and youth exchanges to the agenda, the two sides appear to be building resilience into the relationship: a web of commitments that can absorb friction in any one area.

The healthcare plan offers a template for how the broader agenda might work in practice. A named center, a defined set of medical specialties, and a target number of trainees move the cooperation from declaration to specification. Whether the other nine plans contain comparable specifics is not yet clear from the available reporting.

## What's Next

The Week unfolds against a broader round of China-ASEAN diplomacy, with related engagements — including the 2026 China (Chongqing)-ASEAN Logistics Industry Cooperation Conference — running in the same period. The immediate test will be whether the ten plans translate into funded programs and measurable outcomes, and whether the healthcare commitments in particular move from announcement to implementation. For now, the message from Chongqing is one of continuity and consolidation: a relationship that has traded on scale for years is now being asked to deliver on depth.
