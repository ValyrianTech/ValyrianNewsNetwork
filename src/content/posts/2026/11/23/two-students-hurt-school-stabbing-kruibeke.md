# Two Students Hurt in School Stabbing in Kruibeke

Two students were injured on Monday morning in a stabbing inside a classroom at the Sint-Jorisinstituut in Bazel, a village within the municipality of Kruibeke in East Flanders. Both boys were taken to hospital with what initial reports described as light injuries, and the East Flanders prosecutor's office has confirmed the facts and opened an investigation.

## What Happened

The incident occurred around 9:30 a.m. in a classroom of the fifth year of secondary education, according to [HLN](https://www.hln.be/beveren-kruibeke-zwijndrecht/twee-leerlingen-gewond-bij-steekincident-op-school-in-kruibeke~acd66298/), the regional outlet that first reported the case. According to initial information, one student first stabbed himself and then stabbed another student in the class. Both involved are boys.

Two ambulances and a mobile medical emergency team (MUG) were dispatched to the scene to provide first aid, and both students were subsequently taken to hospital. The precise circumstances leading up to the incident remain unclear, HLN reported.

## School Response and Investigation

The school's management attributed the incident to the "psychological vulnerability" (*psychische kwetsbaarheid*) of one of the students, HLN reported. The pupil guidance centre (CLB) and the social cell of the Scheldewaas police zone have been mobilised to support the affected class.

The East Flanders prosecutor's office confirmed the facts but declined to comment further because minors are involved, while confirming that an investigation has been started.

## A School in a Recently Merged Municipality

The Sint-Jorisinstituut is a Catholic secondary school located at Kruibekestraat 55a in Bazel. As HLN's reporting notes, Bazel is a *deelgemeente*—a sub-municipality—of Beveren-Kruibeke-Zwijndrecht, the East Flanders municipality in which Kruibeke is now incorporated. HLN files this report under its "Beveren-Kruibeke-Zwijndrecht" regional section, reflecting that municipal structure.

## Analysis: A Wider Conversation

The incident touches on two recurring themes in Flemish public debate. The first is school safety: reaction to the HLN report has centred on questions about entrance controls and whether screening measures should be introduced at school gates—a discussion that resurfaces in Belgium after incidents of this kind.

The second is youth mental health. The school's explicit reference to psychological vulnerability reflects growing attention in Flanders to the well-being of adolescents, and to the role schools play, often through the CLB, in early detection and support. Framing the event in these terms is significant: it situates the stabbing within a mental-health context rather than a purely disciplinary or criminal one, without prejudging the prosecutor's findings.

## The Legal Dimension

Because both students are minors, the case falls under the protections of Belgium's youth justice system. Prosecutors have said little beyond confirming an investigation is underway, which is standard practice where minors are concerned. How the case proceeds—and whether it is referred to a juvenile court—will depend on the outcome of that inquiry.

## What to Watch

Several questions remain open. The medical condition of the two students, the exact sequence of events, and whether investigators will corroborate the school's attribution of the incident to psychological vulnerability all await confirmation. As the story develops, statements from local authorities, Flemish education officials, and the Catholic education network may follow. For now, the priority for the school community is the welfare of the class and of the two boys in hospital.
