# Mechelen Fire Forces Evacuation of 10 Residents

A fire broke out in a vacant, under-renovation building on the Augustijnenstraat in Mechelen in the early hours of Sunday morning, forcing the evacuation of around ten residents from three nearby buildings, according to [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/11/08/brand-mechelen0/). No one was injured.

The blaze was reported at approximately 06:30 and spread to the roof of an adjoining, occupied building, prompting emergency services to clear residents from three buildings opposite the Thomas More University College campus. Those evacuated were sheltered in the Thomas More building across the street.

## How the incident unfolded

"The fire started this morning around 6:30 in a vacant building on the Augustijnenstraat, close to the Zandpoortvest," police spokesperson Dirk van de Sande told VRT NWS. "That house was being renovated."

Fire crews brought the blaze under control during the morning. Van de Sande explained that the decision to evacuate followed the fire's spread: "The emergency services then evacuated the residents of nearby houses because the fire spread to the roof of the house next door."

"The neighbours were received in the Thomas More University College across the street," he added. No injuries were reported.

## Traffic disruption

As a result of the incident, the Zandpoortvest was closed to traffic for around three hours while crews worked, forcing vehicles to divert.

## Investigation underway

A forensic laboratory and a fire expert are now on site to determine the cause of the blaze in the vacant building, VRT NWS reported. The building where the fire originated was undergoing renovation. No cause has yet been established, and the investigation is ongoing.
