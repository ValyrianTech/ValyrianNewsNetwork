# RAYE and Rosalía Lead the 2027 Grammy Nominations

British singer-songwriter RAYE leads the field for the 69th annual Grammy Awards with 11 nominations, tying the record Beyoncé set in 2025 for the most nods earned by a woman artist in a single cycle, the [Recording Academy](https://www.grammy.com/news/2027-grammys-nominations-announcement/) announced Monday. Spanish star Rosalía follows with nine, setting up a showdown between two of pop's most ambitious European artists when the awards are handed out on Sunday, Feb. 7, 2027, in Los Angeles.

The nominations, revealed across genres and dominated by women, mark one of the most eclectic and female-heavy ballots in the ceremony's recent history.

## A record year for women

RAYE's total rests on a breakthrough year in which her song "Where Is My Husband!" became a chart and social-media phenomenon. The track is nominated for both Record and Song of the Year, while her album *This Music May Contain Hope.* is up for Album of the Year, according to [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/16/nx-s1-6037518/2027-grammy-nominations-complete-list).

Rosalía's nine nominations — the second-most of any artist — arrive for *Lux (Complete Works)*, a classically influenced album in which she sings in 14 languages across multiple genres. Her nominations span Album, Song and Record of the Year, plus pop, alternative, Latin and global categories. As the [Los Angeles Times](https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/music/story/2026-11-16/grammys-2027-nominations-raye-rosalia-ella-langley-olivia-rodrigo-phoebe-bridgers) noted, Rosalía's momentum follows her Album and Song of the Year wins at the Latin Grammys just days earlier, on Nov. 13 — a striking turn for an artist who had previously received only four Grammy nominations in her career.

The spread of nominations extends well beyond the two leaders. Ella Langley, Miranda Lambert, Olivia Rodrigo and Phoebe Bridgers each earned seven, while Bruno Mars — the only male artist in the Album of the Year field — Leon Thomas and Olivia Dean each collected six.

## The major-category races

The Album of the Year contest is unusually open. Only two of the eight nominees — Taylor Swift and Bruno Mars — have previously won in the category. The nominees are Phoebe Bridgers' *Lost Weekend*, Olivia Dean's *The Art of Loving*, Ella Langley's *Dandelion*, Bruno Mars' *The Romantic*, RAYE's *This Music May Contain Hope.*, Olivia Rodrigo's *you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love*, Rosalía's *Lux (Complete Works)* and Taylor Swift's *The Life of a Showgirl*.

Record of the Year pits "Where Is My Husband!" and "Berghain" — Rosalía's operatic collaboration with Björk and Yves Tumor — against Olivia Dean's "Man I Need," Ella Langley's "Choosin' Texas," Bruno Mars' "I Just Might," Olivia Rodrigo's "The Cure," Taylor Swift's "The Fate of Ophelia" and Tame Impala's "Dracula" with JENNIE.

Langley's "Choosin' Texas," co-written with Miranda Lambert, has been a defining commercial force: it set a new record for the longest-running No. 1 in the history of Billboard's pop chart, holding the top spot for 27 weeks. The country star is nominated in all four of the Grammys' most prestigious categories — Album, Record and Song of the Year, plus Best New Artist — a combination last achieved by a country artist when Bobbie Gentry did it in 1968.

## Genre diversity — and a notable milestone

The Best New Artist field reflects the academy's widening taste: alongside Langley are the New York rock band Geese, the experimental act Angine de Poitrine, alt-pop's Slayyyter, Sienna Spiro, Don Toliver, Remi Wolf and The Womack Sisters.

Further down the ballot, the Contemporary Country Album category is made up entirely of women nominees for the first time in Grammy history, and the awards' newest category — Best Asian Pop Music Performance — drew its own competitive slate.

Beyoncé, meanwhile, added to her legacy with a nomination for Best R&B Solo Performance for "Morning Dew (Donk)" — her 100th career Grammy nod, per the Los Angeles Times.

## A new broadcast era

The 2027 ceremony carries significance beyond the trophies. In a historic shift, the Grammys return to ABC for the first time since 1972 and will stream live across ABC, Disney+ and Hulu from Crypto.com Arena, the academy said — part of a new 10-year deal with Disney after five decades on CBS. The Premiere Ceremony will stream earlier the same day on Disney+ from the Peacock Theater.

Winners are chosen by the Recording Academy's Voting Members, a body of nearly 16,000 music makers spread across 12 fields and 100 categories. Final-round voting runs from Dec. 10, 2026, through Jan. 7, 2027.

"It's a privilege to congratulate all the incredible music creators recognized in this year's Grammy nominations," Grammys CEO Harvey Mason jr. said in a statement. "From first-time nominees to artists reaching career milestones, it's inspiring to see musicians at every stage of their careers represented."

## What to watch

With RAYE chasing a record-tying haul and Rosalía riding the momentum of her Latin Grammy sweep, the Feb. 7 ceremony sets up a compelling question: whether the academy's voters will reward a British pop breakthrough or a Spanish genre-blurring auteur in the night's biggest categories. Bruno Mars, as the lone man in Album of the Year, and a deep country contingent led by Ella Langley will also test how far the ballot's diversity translates into gold.

Two months of campaigning — and one pivotal night in Los Angeles — will decide it.
