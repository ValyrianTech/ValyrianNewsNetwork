# BLG Win League of Legends Worlds, Ending China's 5-Year Wait

Chinese esports club BLG (Bilibili Gaming) won the 2026 League of Legends World Championship early Sunday, defeating Korea's Gen.G 3–1 in New York to claim the organization's first world title and ending a five-year drought for China's LPL league. The victory, sealed in the early hours of Nov. 15 Beijing time, sparked widespread celebration across Chinese media and gaming communities.

According to [CCTV](https://sports.cctv.com/2026/11/15/ARTI5VvqhhtEckKGKjisvgy2261115.shtml), the state broadcaster, it is "the first time an LPL club has lifted the Summoner's Trophy in five years, since EDG in 2021." Gen.G took the opening game of the best-of-five final, but BLG rallied to win three straight — a comeback the Chinese press described as "letting one go, then chasing three."

## A Historic Run Through Korea

The final was the culmination of an extraordinary knockout campaign. Along the way, BLG defeated four LCK (Korean) teams in succession — a feat the Chinese media dubbed "一穿四," or "one piercing four," for sweeping an entire rival region. Per [IT之家](https://www.ithome.com/1/027/258.htm), BLG beat HLE in the qualifying stage, swept DK 3–0 in the quarterfinals, edged defending champion T1 3–1 in the semifinal, and then overcame Gen.G in the final.

The semifinal win over T1 was especially significant. T1, led by the legendary Faker, had never lost a best-of-five series to an LPL team at Worlds. As [直播吧](https://news.zhibo8.com/zuqiu/2026-11-15/6af8fe0e2308enative.htm) noted, BLG became "the first LPL team to defeat T1 in a best-of-five at the World Championship," avenging its loss to the same opponent in the 2024 final, when it fell 2–3.

## A Broken Drought

The title restores LPL supremacy after several years of LCK dominance. China's previous world championships came in 2018 (iG), 2019 (FPX), and 2021 (EDG). The gap of more than 1,800 days since that last win had become a sore point for Chinese fans, who watch the finals overnight because the tournament is typically staged in the West.

BLG was founded in December 2017, when the video platform Bilibili acquired the team IMay and renamed it Bilibili Gaming, then joined the LPL the following year. Sunday's title is its first Summoner's Cup.

## A Comeback Story

The run was not without adversity. According to 直播吧, BLG nearly crashed out during the Swiss stage, entering a do-or-die match with a 2–2 record before dispatching HLE to reach the knockout rounds. The trajectory — from a near-elimination scare to a decisive climb through top Korean opposition — made the win feel like a scripted redemption after the heartbreak of 2024.

## Analysis: National Pride in Esports

The victory carries weight beyond the game itself. Esports occupies a mainstream place in Chinese popular culture, and state outlets including CCTV reported the result prominently. The lopsided attention reflects both the scale of League of Legends' audience in China and the symbolic value of defeating Korean teams, a rivalry that has long defined competitive League of Legends. The "one-piercing-four" framing — beating an entire region's representatives — resonated as a point of national sporting pride.

In 2026, that story found its happiest ending.

## What's Next

For BLG, the win caps a years-long climb to the top and secures its place in the history of the LPL, which now holds four world titles. For the LPL more broadly, the result may mark a turning point in its long rivalry with the LCK — though Korean teams, and T1 in particular, are unlikely to cede ground quietly. The next test will come at the 2027 World Championship, when BLG will return as a defending champion for the first time and China will look to build on a long-awaited breakthrough.
