# Mariah Carey to Headline Exclusive Francofolies de Spa Show

Global pop superstar Mariah Carey will perform an exclusive, one-off concert at the Francofolies de Spa festival in Belgium on Wednesday 14 July 2027, organisers announced on Monday morning. The show, staged on the Place Royale in the Ardennes spa town, will be Carey's only date across Belgium, France and the Netherlands that summer, according to [RTBF](https://www.rtbf.be/article/mariah-carey-donnera-un-concert-exclusif-aux-francofolies-de-spa-en-2027-11812574).

## A New Night on the Calendar

To accommodate the booking, the festival has added a standalone "Collector" night on 14 July, two days before its usual three-day open-air run on 16-18 July 2027. The addition marks a significant coup for a festival traditionally rooted in French-language song. The announcement was released by the festival's [official channels](https://francofolies.be/news-details/mariah-carey-sera-a-spa-le-14-juillet-pour-un-concert-exclusif/), which billed the evening as a unique Belgian date for the American singer.

## Why Carey, Why Now

Festival director Yoann Frederic said the booking reflects a deliberate effort to broaden the event's reach beyond a single genre. "Mariah Carey is very, very rare on stage," he told [RTBF](https://www.rtbf.be/article/on-n-a-pas-resiste-a-l-envie-les-francofolies-de-spa-s-expliquent-sur-la-venue-de-mariah-carey-l-ete-prochain-11812947). "She decided to embark on a summer tour in Europe, it happened she was available on our dates, and so she was proposed to us. We could not resist the urge to welcome a star of this calibre to Spa."

Frederic framed the move as part of a wider strategy. "It was our wish not to become a ghetto of French-language song, of a single musical genre, but to allow ourselves to open up to artists and opportunities," he said, while insisting the festival would retain "a majority of francophone artists, a large part of them Belgian." The 2027 booking follows the festival's decision to host Sting in summer 2026, his first performance in Wallonia.

He also pushed back against reducing Carey to a single seasonal hit. "We often tend to reduce the artist to a single song. But she is an artist of phenomenal stature," he said, describing her as one of the three divas alongside Celine Dion and Whitney Houston who wrote her own material.

## A Career Spanning Generations

Carey, now 57 — she turned 57 in March 2026 — is among the best-selling music artists of all time, with more than 200 million albums sold, five Grammy Awards and 19 Billboard Hot 100 number-one singles over a career exceeding 35 years. Beyond her perennial holiday anthem, her catalogue includes "Hero," "Fantasy," "We Belong Together" and "Obsessed."

She remains active: in 2025 she released her 16th studio album, "Here For It All," her first collection of original songs in seven years, and in February 2026 she performed at the Milano Cortina Winter Olympics opening ceremony. According to RTBF, it had been almost a decade since Carey last performed in Belgium.

## Tickets and Access

Tickets for the Carey show go on sale Friday 13 November 2026 at 10:00, through a dedicated ticketing channel separate from the main festival pass. Crucially, the three-day Royal pass valid for 16-18 July does not grant access to the 14 July concert; a separate ticket is required. [Moustique](https://www.moustique.be/culture/2026/11/09/mariah-carey-aux-francofolies-de-spa-2027-un-concert-exclusif-en-belgique-le-14-juillet-IT6D3FSATBFLFLPJUFZGUB6VNM/) reports that Royal tickets will start from 89 euros in the first sales wave, with VIP packages from 199 euros.

## What's Next

The booking positions Francofolies de Spa as a growing draw on the European summer circuit and signals Belgium's appetite for major international headliners. For the festival, the challenge will be balancing its francophone identity with the broader ambitions its director now champions. For fans, the message is simple: with a single Benelux-and-France date and a separate ticket, demand is likely to be fierce when sales open.
