# Person Shot in Humbeek, Suspect Arrested After Manhunt

A person was shot and wounded in the leg near a CoMarkt supermarket in Humbeek, part of the municipality of Grimbergen in Flemish Brabant, on the morning of 9 November 2026. The perpetrator initially fled the scene but was arrested a few hours later in Kapelle-op-den-Bos, police and prosecutors confirmed.

## What Happened

According to [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/11/09/humbeek-grimbergen-schietpartij-comarkt/), the shooting occurred in the morning near the CoMarkt store on Kerkstraat. The incident was confirmed by Margaux Vander Oost, spokesperson for the Halle-Vilvoorde prosecutor's office.

Initial information suggested that four shots were fired. One person was struck in the leg and taken to hospital. Police closed off Kerkstraat at the scene of the shooting.

## Manhunt Ends in Arrest

The perpetrator fled, but their vehicle was quickly located in Kapelle-op-den-Bos. Multiple police teams and a police helicopter were deployed in the search. A few hours later, around midday, the suspect was apprehended near the border with Willebroek.

"They were able to detain him near the border with Willebroek," said Mathias Diricx (CD&V), the mayor of Kapelle-op-den-Bos, confirming the arrest.

## Context

Humbeek is a village within the municipality of Grimbergen, just north of Brussels in the province of Flemish Brabant. CoMarkt is a supermarket chain belonging to the Colruyt Group, and its Humbeek branch is located on Kerkstraat. The investigation is being handled by the Halle-Vilvoorde prosecutor's office.

## Analysis and Open Questions

Several key questions remain unanswered. Authorities have not disclosed the motive for the shooting, and it is not yet known whether the incident was targeted or whether the victim and suspect knew each other. The identities of both the victim and the suspect have not been released.

The number of shots fired — reported as four — is based on initial information and has not been independently confirmed by authorities. The victim's condition has not been made public.

## What's Next

The suspect is now in custody, and investigators are expected to work to establish a motive and a clear account of the events. Further details may emerge as the prosecutor's office continues its inquiry. Local outlets are likely to publish follow-ups as the investigation develops.
