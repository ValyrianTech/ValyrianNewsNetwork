# China Tops Shooting Worlds With 13 Golds, 7 Olympic Quotas

China closed the 2026 ISSF World Shooting Championships atop the medal table with 13 gold, 6 silver and 8 bronze medals and, crucially, secured seven Olympic quotas for the Los Angeles 2028 Games, [Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/sports/20261114/b040a10f768b4c1ea3cdc168d7b10a23/c.html) reported on Saturday. In the 12 individual Olympic events, the Chinese team claimed 6 gold, 5 silver and 5 bronze — its best-ever performance in Olympic disciplines at a World Championships.

The championships in Doha, Qatar, concluded on November 13 and served as the first quota event of the LA 2028 cycle, offering 36 Olympic quotas across 12 individual disciplines. Italy finished a distant second on the medal table with 3 gold and 1 bronze.

## A Sweep of the Air Events

China's quota haul was anchored by near-total dominance in the air rifle and air pistol events, securing all four individual quotas on offer in those disciplines: men's and women's 10m air rifle and men's and women's 10m air pistol. The team added quotas in women's 25m sport pistol, women's skeet and men's trap.

The standout was Sheng Lihao (盛李豪), who won the men's 10m air rifle individual title and, per [Guancha](https://www.guancha.cn/TiYu/2026_11_04_906984.shtml), completed a career "super grand slam" — victories at the Olympics, World Championships, World Cup Finals, Asian Games, Asian Championships and national events in the same discipline, the first man to do so in a rifle event this century. The 21-year-old also teamed with Wang Zifei (王子菲) to win the 10m air rifle mixed team gold, with Zhang Changhong and Du Yuchen taking silver for a Chinese one-two finish, [Cover News](https://www.thecover.cn/news/GETxLks58oCH90qSdq8Jkw==) reported — a result that gave Sheng a third straight World Championship title in the mixed team event. Hu Kai (胡凯) added gold in the men's 10m air pistol, and Chinese shooters swept the top three places in women's skeet on November 6.

## Where China Left Quotas on the Table

Despite the commanding medal haul, the quota system caps any single nation at a theoretical maximum of 12 at these championships. The top three finishers in each of the 12 individual Olympic finals earn a quota, but each country may claim only one per event, and each athlete can win only one quota for their delegation over the entire qualification period.

That math — and three notable gaps — explains why China finished with seven rather than a larger number. The team conceded that it performed below par in the men's and women's 50m rifle three positions and the men's 25m rapid fire pistol, failing to reach the finals in any of them.

## Analysis: A Strong Start

China's Doha showing is a strong early foothold in the LA 2028 cycle, built on almost complete control of the air events and a deep enough roster to fill a Chinese one-two in the mixed air rifle team event. The seven quotas are just over half the theoretical maximum of 12 available here, and the three rifle-and-rapid-fire gaps mark the clearest areas to address at later qualifying events.

The field in Doha was far from soft. In the women's 10m air rifle, China's Han Jiayu took silver and Wang Zifei bronze, with the gold going to South Korea's Ban Hyo-jin — the reigning Paris Olympic champion in the event — who won the final with 253.1 points, according to Guancha. Chinese shooters still secured one Olympic quota in the discipline.

## What's Next

The seven quotas are provisional, pending formal allocation by China's national Olympic committee and the ISSF, and the athletes who will fill them will be finalized later in the cycle. The immediate priority is clear: rebuilding form in the three rifle and rapid-fire pistol events where no quota was earned, which will be on offer at later qualifying events. For a team that has now posted its best-ever Olympic-event result at a World Championships, the gap between dominance and depth is the story to watch.
