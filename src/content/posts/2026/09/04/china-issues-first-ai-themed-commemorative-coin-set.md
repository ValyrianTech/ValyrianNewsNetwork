# China Issues First AI-Themed Commemorative Coin Set

The People's Bank of China (PBOC) issued a set of "Sci-Tech Future (Artificial Intelligence)" commemorative gold and silver coins on September 4, marking the country's first legal tender coin series dedicated to artificial intelligence. The three-coin set—comprising one gold coin and two silver coins—celebrates China's scientific and technological ambitions in AI development, according to [Xinhua News](https://www.news.cn/photo/20260904/25d9f790a2b143e0aec595f6fe5cb010/c.html).

The centerpiece of the collection is a 5-gram square gold proof coin measuring 18mm by 18mm with a face value of 80 yuan and 99.9% purity. With a maximum mintage of just 10,000 pieces, it represents a significant milestone: the first square-shaped small-weight gold coin ever issued in China's precious metal commemorative coin program. The coin's reverse design features a chip at its center, surrounded by brain-shaped circuit textures and the binary digits 0 and 1—a visual representation of the brain-inspired AI concept.

## A Set Designed for the AI Era

The two accompanying 15-gram circular silver proof coins each carry a face value of 5 yuan, measure 33mm in diameter, and boast 99.9% purity, with a maximum mintage of 20,000 pieces per design. One silver coin depicts a human profile combined with circuit textures and a spiral dot matrix, symbolizing human-machine interaction. The other features a key surrounded by AI field element symbols, representing AI unlocking the door to future technology.

All three coins bear the National Emblem of the People's Republic of China on the obverse, along with the country name and year. The reverse of each coin is inscribed with "Sci-Tech Future - Artificial Intelligence" and its face value.

The coins were minted by Shanghai Mint Co., Ltd. and Shenzhen Guobao Mint Co., Ltd., with China Gold Coin Group Co., Ltd. serving as the general distributor, as detailed in the [PBOC's official announcement](https://m.xa.bendibao.com/live/153438.shtm).

## First-of-Their-Kind Designations

The issuance carries particular significance for China's numismatic program. As [MyDrivers](https://news.mydrivers.com/1/1147/1147130.htm) noted, this is the first AI-themed legal tender gold and silver commemorative coin set in China, and square gold coins remain exceptionally rare in the country's precious metal coin history. The design choice directly incorporates brain-inspired AI concepts into the coin's visual language, with the 5g gold coin featuring an intricate combination of chip imagery, neural circuit patterns, and binary code.

The "Sci-Tech Future" series represents a new thematic direction for the PBOC's commemorative coin program, reflecting China's positioning of AI development as a national strategic priority. The coins were part of the central bank's 2026 precious metal coin issuance plan, which includes 10 projects and 53 varieties—one of the most extensive lineups in recent years, according to [Changsha Evening News](https://hbj.icswb.com/h/168/20251208/957733.html).

## Collector Interest and Market Considerations

The release comes at a time of heightened interest in China's commemorative coin market. The coin collecting community has responded enthusiastically to the dual "firsts" represented by this set—the first square small-weight gold coin specification and the first AI-themed precious metal coin series. As [SMZDM](https://post.smzdm.com/p/axkl8drw/) observed, while no one can guarantee whether these firsts will generate sustained premiums, the initial wave of thematic coins has historically been when market narratives are strongest.

The 5g gold coin's limited mintage of 10,000 pieces adds to its appeal among collectors. However, market analysts caution that recent new commemorative coin issues have experienced mixed secondary market performance, with some trading below their issue prices. The volatile international gold market—which has swung between approximately $4,400 and $4,700 per ounce in less than a month—adds further uncertainty to the coin's material value proposition.

## Purchase and Distribution

Collectors can register for the coins through the China Gold Coin website (www.chngc.net/qd) or the "China Gold Coin" WeChat public account. Lottery registration through the Gold Coin Cloud Mall platform closes on September 7 at noon. As with all commemorative coins, the face value does not reflect the actual market price, which is influenced by gold and silver material costs, supply-demand dynamics, and collector sentiment.

## Looking Ahead

The issuance of this AI-themed coin set signals how China is weaving technological narratives into its cultural and monetary heritage. As the country continues to position artificial intelligence as a cornerstone of its economic future, the "Sci-Tech Future" series may well become a recurring theme in China's commemorative coin program. Whether these coins appreciate in value remains to be seen, but their historical significance as China's first AI-themed legal tender coins is already secured.
