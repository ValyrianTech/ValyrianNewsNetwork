# China Unveils First AI-Themed Commemorative Coin Set

The People's Bank of China (PBOC) officially issued a set of gold and silver commemorative coins themed around artificial intelligence on September 4, marking the first time the central bank has dedicated a legal tender coin series to AI and technological innovation. The three-coin set, titled "Science Innovation Future (AI)," comprises one gold coin and two silver coins, all of which are legal tender of the People's Republic of China, according to [Xinhua News](https://www.news.cn/photo/20260904/25d9f790a2b143e0aec595f6fe5cb010/c.html).

The release comes amid a period of intense global focus on AI development and underscores China's ambitions to position itself at the forefront of artificial intelligence research and commercialization.

## The Coin Set

The centerpiece of the collection is a 5-gram square gold coin with a face value of 80 yuan and a maximum mintage of just 10,000 pieces. Its reverse design features a computer chip, brain-shaped circuit textures, and binary digits 0 and 1, directly incorporating the concept of brain-inspired AI onto the coin's surface. Square-shaped gold coins are notably rare in China's precious metal commemorative coin program, adding to the piece's collectible appeal.

The set also includes two 15-gram circular silver coins, each with a face value of 5 yuan and a maximum mintage of 20,000 pieces. One silver coin depicts a human side-profile portrait combined with circuit textures and spiral dot matrix patterns, symbolizing human-machine interaction. The other features a key surrounded by AI-related element symbols, representing the unlocking of future technology through artificial intelligence. All three coins are proof-quality with 99.9% purity and feature the National Emblem of the People's Republic of China on their obverse sides.

As [MyDrivers](https://news.mydrivers.com/1/1147/1147130.htm) reported, this is the first set of AI-themed legal tender gold and silver commemorative coins in China. The coins were minted by Shanghai Mint Co., Ltd. and Shenzhen Guobao Mint Co., Ltd., with China Gold Coin Group Co., Ltd. serving as the exclusive distributor.

## Part of a Broader Program

The AI-themed coin set is part of the PBOC's 2026 precious metal commemorative coin issuance plan, which includes 10 projects and 53 varieties, one of the largest lineups in recent years, as detailed in the [full PBOC announcement](https://finance.sina.com.cn/money/bank/bank_yhfg/2026-08-28/doc-inipvvzv4636119.shtml). Other projects in the program include the 2026 Chinese Dragon silver coin, Wuyishan National Park coins, and the 2027 Panda series.

The announcement was initially published on August 22, with the coins officially released to the public on September 4. The [Economic Daily](http://www.ce.cn/xwzx/gnsz/gdxw/202609/t20260901_3185938.shtml) noted that sales channels are available through the China Gold Coin website and its official WeChat public account.

## Regulatory Context

The coin issuance coincides with new RMB trading regulations that took effect on September 1, 2026. The PBOC and State Administration for Market Regulation jointly issued Announcement No. 19 of 2026, which establishes clearer rules for the buying and selling of RMB currency. Notably, the new regulations explicitly permit the trading of precious metal commemorative coins while prohibiting the trading of circulating RMB before public exchange, as reported by [Tencent News](https://view.inews.qq.com/a/20260901A0BXKL00). This provides a clearer legal framework for legitimate coin collecting and trading.

## Market Outlook

The limited mintage of the gold coin—just 10,000 pieces—has already attracted attention from the collector community. However, analysts caution that recent commemorative coin releases have shown mixed performance in the secondary market, with some new issues falling below their initial offering prices. The actual market price of these coins will differ significantly from their nominal face values due to the intrinsic value of the precious metals and supply-demand dynamics.

Industry observers advise collectors to approach the market rationally rather than engaging in speculative buying. The 2026 commemorative coin program was announced in December 2025, with the [Changsha Evening News](https://hbj.icswb.com/h/168/20251208/957733.html) noting that the inclusion of the AI-themed project represents a new direction for China's commemorative coin program, which traditionally celebrates cultural heritage, historical events, and natural landmarks.

## Significance

The issuance of AI-themed legal tender coins reflects a broader national strategy to celebrate and promote China's technological ambitions. By incorporating visual elements associated with artificial intelligence—chips, neural networks, and binary code—the coin set serves as both a collectible artifact and a cultural statement about China's commitment to becoming a global leader in AI innovation.

As the world's central banks and governments grapple with the implications of artificial intelligence, China's decision to immortalize the technology in precious metal is a distinctive move that bridges numismatic tradition with cutting-edge innovation. The coins now enter circulation as both legal tender and symbolic markers of a nation positioning itself at the intersection of technology and culture.

For collectors and observers alike, the set offers a tangible piece of a transformative moment in technological history—one that China has chosen to commemorate in gold and silver.
