# 5.1 Magnitude Earthquake Hits Qinghai's Haixi Prefecture

A 5.1 magnitude earthquake struck the Haixi Prefecture direct-administered area in Qinghai Province, northwestern China, in the early hours of Friday, according to the [China Earthquake Networks Center (CENC)](https://english.news.cn/20260904/23d7c44cd4bf40e5ae9b22386a8372d5/c.html). The quake, centered at 37.84 degrees north latitude and 95.59 degrees east longitude, occurred at a focal depth of 10 kilometers. No casualties have been reported as of the latest information available.

## Immediate Response

The China Earthquake Administration (CEA) activated a Level IV emergency response immediately after the quake, coordinating with the China Earthquake Networks Center and the Qinghai Provincial Earthquake Agency to manage the situation, as [reported](https://news.yunnan.cn/system/2026/09/04/034143795.shtml). The Qinghai Provincial Earthquake Agency has dispatched a field work team to the affected area to assist local government response efforts.

The epicenter was located approximately 20 kilometers from the Dachaidan Administrative Committee, 165 kilometers from Delingha City, and 170 kilometers from Golmud City, according to [Xinhua News](https://www.news.cn/local/20260904/a3e1af0b95864de0b5b7e49bbf76650a/c.html).

## Water Infrastructure Assessment

The Ministry of Water Resources has activated its earthquake disaster relief dispatch command mechanism, conducting urgent assessments of water conservancy projects and rural water supply systems in the affected region, [CCTV News reported](https://news.cctv.com/2026/09/04/ARTI0hZbegpIhMWDKmfLsnsv260904.shtml). The ministry convened a special consultation meeting to analyze the earthquake's impact on water infrastructure and to assess rainfall and water conditions, with light rain forecast in the affected area over the next three days.

## Local Impact and Resident Accounts

Residents in the Dachaidan area reported very strong shaking, with many fleeing buildings in the early morning hours. According to [local accounts](https://www.ntdtv.com/gb/2026/09/03/a104129921.html), one resident described the experience: "I had just gone to sleep at 1 AM, got up to use the bathroom, and as soon as I stood up the whole room was shaking... scared, I threw on clothes and ran down to the hotel lobby, which was already packed with people." The tremor was also felt in Xi'an, Shaanxi Province, and Lanzhou, Gansu Province, hundreds of kilometers from the epicenter.

## Part of an Ongoing Seismic Sequence

Friday's earthquake is the latest in a series of seismic events that have rattled the Haixi region since June 2026. The sequence began with a 6.3 magnitude earthquake on June 16 that caused one death and eight injuries, followed by multiple significant aftershocks including a 5.2 magnitude event on June 23, which was officially classified as an aftershock of the June 16 main shock, according to [Jiemian News](https://m.jiemian.com/article/14634721.html).

Seismic activity has continued through the summer, with a 5.6 magnitude earthquake striking the same area on August 19, prompting a Level IV response from the CEA and causing temporary power outages in some residential areas, as [reported](https://www.zaobao.com.sg/news/china/story20260819-9539964). A 4.8 magnitude earthquake followed on August 20 with no reported casualties or property damage, according to [Jiemian News](https://m.jiemian.com/article/14957606.html).

The region sits at the northeastern edge of the Tibetan Plateau, at the boundary where the Qilian and Qaidam blocks converge in a compressional thrust zone. The June 16 earthquake was identified as a thrust-type rupture on the Zongwulongshan Fault, approximately 3 kilometers from the epicenter, with experts from the CENC noting that aftershock activity would likely continue for an extended period, as [analyzed](https://www.jinantimes.com.cn/news-216-5273456.html).

## Assessment and Outlook

Despite the strong shaking and the ongoing nature of the seismic sequence, no casualties or significant damage have been reported from Friday's earthquake. This is consistent with recent events in the region, where the sparsely populated area and ongoing seismic safety assessments since the June 16 earthquake have helped mitigate impacts.

Authorities remain vigilant, particularly regarding water infrastructure integrity given the forecast of light rain in the coming days. The Ministry of Water Resources is closely monitoring water conservancy projects and rural water supply systems, while local authorities continue damage assessments across the affected area.

As the seismic sequence continues, experts anticipate further significant aftershocks may occur. The CEA and provincial agencies maintain enhanced monitoring and are prepared to respond to additional events as the situation evolves.
