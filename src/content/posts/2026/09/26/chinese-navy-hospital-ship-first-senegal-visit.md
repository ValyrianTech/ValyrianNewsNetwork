# Chinese Navy Hospital Ship Makes First Senegal Visit

The Chinese People's Liberation Army Navy hospital ship Auspicious Ark arrived in Dakar, Senegal, on Friday, September 25, beginning a goodwill visit and providing medical services—the first time a Chinese naval hospital ship has visited the West African nation. According to [Xinhua](https://imgs.news.cn/english/20260926/ccc5bf8f19b3484da3b28f556c81b014/c.html), the vessel docked at the Port of Dakar at around 11:00 a.m. local time.

![Chinese Navy hospital ship Ji Xiang Fang Zhou (Auspicious Ark) arriving at Dakar, Senegal](https://lesoleil.sn/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/navire-Chine.jpg)

The hospital ship is carrying out Mission Harmony-2026, the 12th iteration of China's overseas humanitarian medical service program that began in 2010. Senegalese military and government officials, representatives of the Chinese Embassy, local residents, and members of the Chinese community welcomed the vessel at the port before boarding for tours and talks with mission personnel.

## A First for Sino-Senegalese Military Cooperation

The visit marks a significant milestone in the longstanding military-to-military cooperative relationship between the two countries. Senegal has maintained diplomatic relations with the People's Republic of China since 1971 and is one of relatively few West African states never to have switched recognition to Taiwan.

Senegal's state newspaper [Le Soleil](https://lesoleil.sn/actualites/societe-fait-divers/marine-chinoise-le-navire-hopital-ji-xiang-fang-zhou-propose-des-soins-medicaux-gratuits-a-dakar/) reported that the ship will remain at the autonomous port of Dakar from September 24 to October 1, offering free medical consultations and treatment. The initiative is jointly organized by Senegal's Directorate of the Armed Forces Health Service and the Ministry of Health and Public Hygiene.

The Chinese vessel is a 10,000-ton-class ocean-going hospital ship—the third independently designed and built by China. It houses 18 clinical departments, five auxiliary diagnostic departments, and eight standard operating rooms, capable of performing more than 60 types of examinations, treatments, and surgeries spanning general surgery, orthopedics, obstetrics and gynecology, and ophthalmology.

## Mission Harmony-2026: The Latest Leg

According to the Xinhua departure report, the Auspicious Ark [left](https://www.news.cn/politics/20260722/c78ffadc84814299ba38e74cfed5a6cc/c.html) a naval port in Qingdao, Shandong Province, on July 22 for a mission planned to last more than 200 days, visiting Djibouti and other countries across Africa and South Asia. This is the ship's first-ever overseas mission, making it a test of China's newest naval medical-diplomacy platform.

Per Xinhua, the mission aims to enhance China's traditional friendship and practical cooperation with the countries concerned, provide more public safety goods to the international community, and serve the building of a "maritime community with a shared future."

Senegal is the second stop of Mission Harmony-2026. The ship previously called at Nouakchott, Mauritania, arriving around September 15 for a seven-day goodwill visit. During that stop, the ship's medical team performed an emergency C-section, delivering its first "auspicious baby," a 3.4-kilogram boy. Both mother and baby were reported safe. "Crossing mountains and seas to welcome a new life—this is a truly meaningful day!" lead surgeon Xu Xiaozhe said, as quoted by [China Daily](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202609/21/WS6ab11b77e4b06d4aa055f52e.html).

## Careful Handling on the Ground

Senegalese reporting emphasized that free treatment would be available only to pre-selected patients, not the general public. Le Soleil noted that the Directorate of the Armed Forces Health Service and the Ministry of Health and Public Hygiene specified the services "will not be accessible on the basis of free public registration" and will benefit "exclusively patients who have been previously identified and selected by their respective services."

This operational detail reflects differing emphases: China frames the mission as providing medical services to local people, while Senegal channels delivery through its own health and military authorities. Medical consultations run daily from September 25 through September 29, with only morning consultations on the final day, September 30.

During the visit, the ship will also send medical training personnel for hospital exchanges, organize a deck reception, visit a local orphanage, and hold a friendly basketball match with Senegalese military personnel.

## What to Watch

The deployment of a purpose-built hospital ship on its maiden overseas voyage signals China's continued investment in medical diplomacy as a tool of soft power across Africa and South Asia. Senegal, under President Bassirou Diomaye Faye, has pursued a more sovereign, pan-African foreign policy while maintaining ties with multiple partners—making the successful execution of this visit a point of interest for both capitals.

The ship is scheduled to depart Senegal on October 1. Its onward itinerary beyond the confirmed stops in Mauritania and Senegal has not been fully enumerated in available sources.
