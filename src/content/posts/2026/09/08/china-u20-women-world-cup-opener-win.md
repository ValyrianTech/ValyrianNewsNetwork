# China U20 Women Dominate World Cup Opener 5-0

China's U20 women's football team opened their FIFA U20 Women's World Cup campaign with a commanding 5-0 victory over New Caledonia on September 7 at Arena Sosnowiec in Poland, with Huang Jiaxin scoring a remarkable 12-minute hat-trick to announce China's return to the world stage after an eight-year absence.

The result, reported by [Xinhua News](https://www.news.cn/sports/20260908/c9bb7feffa444ca7b51eb649a3c7e3e3/c.html), marks China's first appearance at the tournament since the 2018 edition in France and puts the team top of Group F with three points after the opening round of matches.

## A Tale of Two Halves

The match unfolded in starkly contrasting halves. China dominated possession and territory throughout the opening 45 minutes, registering 20 shots, yet only three found the target as New Caledonia goalkeeper Elizabeth Aben produced an inspired performance to keep the score level at halftime.

According to [FIFA.com](https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/womens/u20womensworldcup/poland-2026/articles/matchday-three-roundup-reports-highlights), the breakthrough came just 23 seconds into the second half when Xiao Yafei converted a dangerous cross from close range. The floodgates then opened in spectacular fashion.

## Huang Jiaxin's Historic Hat-Trick

Huang Jiaxin, a natural center-back who was pushed forward to striker by head coach Wang Jun at halftime, delivered a devastating scoring display. She intercepted a defensive back-pass error to score in the 71st minute, added a second from close range in the 80th, and completed her hat-trick with a clinical volley just three minutes later.

"I am very excited to help the team win the match," Huang said after being named Visa Player of the Match, as quoted by FIFA.com. "It is an honour to be top of the goalscoring standings and I am very happy to be the first player to score a hat-trick in this competition."

The 20-year-old from Shanghai, who is the sister of Tianjin Jinmen Tiger national team player Huang Jiahui, was making her mark on the global stage in emphatic fashion, as noted by [Sohu Sports](https://www.sohu.com/a/1073024865_121119509).

## Substitute Sparks the Rout

Half-time substitute Chen Ruilin proved the catalyst for China's second-half explosion, providing assists for four of the team's five goals. Zhou Xinyi also found the net in the 73rd minute after cutting in from the left flank.

"I am very happy and honoured to have four assists after I entered the field as they helped our team win the match," Chen said, according to FIFA.com. "We will 100 per cent fight for each game in the group stage."

The final statistics were emphatic: China recorded 83 percent possession, 36 shots (13 on target) against New Caledonia's four (none on target), and 16 corners to zero.

## Historic Debut for New Caledonia

For New Caledonia, this was a historic first-ever appearance at a FIFA U-20 Women's World Cup. Despite the heavy defeat, their goalkeeper and coach expressed pride in the team's resilient first-half display.

"This is a first in the history of women's football in New Caledonia," Aben said, as quoted by FIFA.com. "It allows us to gain experience, but also to find out what the highest level is all about. I come away from this match without regrets, but proud of the whole team and the staff."

## Preparation and Context

China's return to the U20 World Cup follows a qualification secured through reaching the semi-finals of the U20 Women's Asian Cup in April. Head coach Wang Jun, who took over the team in early July, oversaw a 46-day intensive training camp across Kunming, Dalian, and Frankfurt, Germany, before the squad was trimmed to its final 21 players, as detailed by [Beijing Youth Daily via Qianlong](https://sports.qianlong.com/2026/0826/8717614.shtml).

The team's preparation included a focus on attacking football, with Wang emphasizing the importance of teamwork and resilience against world-class opposition.

## What's Next

China now faces a significantly tougher test against Nigeria on September 10 (local time), with Spain awaiting in the final group match on September 13. Spain, the 2022 tournament winners, opened their campaign with a 2-0 victory over Nigeria in the other Group F match.

China's emphatic goal difference from this opener provides valuable breathing room in a group where the top two teams and four best third-placed sides advance to the Round of 16. As [163.com's Five Star Sports](https://www.163.com/dy/article/L68VA0U90530S17O.html) noted, the five-goal margin creates a significant buffer for the tougher matches ahead.

The result also reflects broader momentum in Chinese women's football, with the senior national team having qualified for the 2027 Brazil Women's World Cup and the U17 team securing its own World Cup berth. This young squad's World Cup journey represents another step in that upward trajectory.
