# Clouseau to Be First Belgian Band at King Baudouin Stadium

Belgian pop band Clouseau will become the first Belgian band or artist ever to headline the King Baudouin Stadium in Brussels, the band announced on 23 September 2026. The concert is set for Wednesday, 4 August 2027 — a booking that pushes a domestic act into a venue previously reserved for international superstars.

Brothers Koen and Kris Wauters revealed the news on the Radio 2 programme *Goeiemorgen morgen*, as [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/09/21/clouseau-staat-volgend-jaar-als-eerste-belgische-band-ooit-in-ko/) reported. "Nog 315 dagen en wij gaan in het Koning Boudewijnstadion spelen. Een absolute hoogdag," Koen said in Dutch. "Als je denkt aan Koning Boudewijnstadion en muziek, dan denk je aan wereldsterren. Het is wel gaaf dat wij dit als Vlaamse gasten kunnen proberen."

## A venue built for world stars

The King Baudouin Stadium, on the Heizel plateau in Brussels, opened in 1930 as the Jubelstadion and took its current name in 1995. With an official capacity of [50,093 seated](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koning_Boudewijnstadion), it is the largest stadium in Belgium. Its concert history reads almost exclusively as an international roll call: Céline Dion, Johnny Hallyday, Bruce Springsteen, U2, Madonna, Beyoncé, Coldplay, Metallica, Rammstein, The Rolling Stones, Ed Sheeran and The Weeknd have all played there, with BTS and Bad Bunny scheduled for 2026.

Despite that pedigree, no Belgian band had ever headlined the stadium — until now. "Telkens als ik naar concerten in het Koning Boudewijnstadion ging, dacht ik, 'hoe gaaf moet het zijn om hier te staan, zoveel mensen die allemaal tegelijk meezingen, zo indrukwekkend'," Kris Wauters said.

The announcement also confirmed that the concert coincides with Clouseau's 40 years on stage. The band debuted in 1987 at Marktrock in Leuven, and according to the [AFAS Dome](https://www.afas-dome.be/nieuws/clouseau-viert-40-jaar-carriere-met-meer-dan-200000-fans-in-het-sportpaleis), their very first performance took place exactly 40 years before their 2024 anniversary shows — at CC Sint-Genesius-Rode in 1984. Across their career the Wauters brothers have released 27 albums, including two in English, and built a catalogue of Dutch-language standards such as 'Daar gaat ze', 'Anne', 'Nobelprijs', 'Vonken en vuur', 'Passie' and 'Domino'.

## From the Sportpaleis to centre stage

The stadium booking follows an unprecedented run at the Sportpaleis in Antwerp, now the AFAS Dome. No other artist has played that venue more often than Clouseau — 137 times per VRT NWS, while the venue itself recorded a 125th show in December 2024 and 124 performances between 2000 and 2017 for nearly two million visitors.

Koen Wauters was careful to frame the stadium as a different order of magnitude. "Daar was een traditie ontstaan, mensen gingen daar vaker naartoe. We zijn daar niet begonnen met ineens 10 keer," he said. Kris added: "Je komt binnen in een leeg Sportpaleis en je denkt, 'dat is groot'. Maar als je dan binnenkomt in een leeg stadion, wij vielen achterover."

The emotional weight of the moment was plain. "Nog altijd, elke keer als we het uitspreken, kijken we naar elkaar en zeggen we: 'Dit is totaal ongelofelijk'," Kris said. "We zitten ergens in de spreidstand tussen geweldige euforie en een beetje in ons broek doen." Koen described seeing AI-generated crowd animations of the stadium: "Met AI hadden ze daar dan volk in gezet. En dan denk je 'holy shit'. Ik weet niet hoe ik dat ga beleven, hoe ik dat ga vatten op het moment zelf."

## Tickets, staging and access

The concert will use a 360-degree, centre-stage configuration, which Kris Wauters said will allow room for just under 60,000 spectators — above the venue's standard capacity. Tickets go on sale on Wednesday, 30 September 2026 at 10:00 via Clouseau.be, a detail confirmed in an [official band statement reported by P-Magazine](https://p-magazine.com/nl/articles/clouseau-pakt-uit-met-enorm-grote-primeur).

Accessibility has been a talking point. "Waar je ook woont in Vlaanderen, binnen de 15 kilometer van je woonplaats zal je op een bus kunnen stappen die je naar het stadion brengt, en na afloop ook weer naar huis," Kris said.

The announcement was teased in the preceding days by a viral campaign in which prominent Flemish personalities posted cryptic videos bearing the message "Ik ga" ('I'm going'). The band's official statement captured the mood: "Wij gaan in het KONING BOUDEWIJNSTADION spelen op 4 augustus 2027!!! Wij zijn gewoon de eerste Belgische artiest ooit die dit mag doen… Kippenvel!"

## What to watch

With the concert still more than 300 days away, the production and setlist remain under development. The event is a significant moment for Belgian popular music, placing a Dutch-language act on a stage that has hosted only global names. Whether the centre-stage layout, bus network and ticket pricing can translate the Sportpaleis formula to nearly 60,000 people will be the key test — and, as Kris Wauters put it, something that still needs time to sink in.

According to [Podiuminfo](https://www.podiuminfo.nl/news/59366/Clouseau-treedt-als-eerste-Belgische-artiest-ooit-op-in-het-Koning-Boudewijnstadion/), Koen Wauters said he wanted to do "iets geks" before his 60th birthday: "wel, dit valt daar zeker onder!"
