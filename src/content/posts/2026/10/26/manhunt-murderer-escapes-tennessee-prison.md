# Manhunt Underway After Murderer Escapes Tennessee Prison

A man serving a life sentence for murder escaped from a Tennessee prison by breaching a perimeter fence, prompting a coordinated manhunt across a rural stretch of the state near the Mississippi River, authorities said.

Jacob Allen Morley, 28, was discovered unaccounted for around 11:30 p.m. Sunday at the Northwest Correctional Complex in Tiptonville, according to the [Tennessee Department of Correction](https://www.tn.gov/correction/news/2026/10/26/tdoc-seeking-information-locating-escaped-inmate-jacob-allen-morley.html). Security footage later confirmed that Morley had slipped through the facility's perimeter fence and fled while still wearing his prison uniform. Officials have not said how he got through the fence.

TDOC has added Morley to the Tennessee Bureau of Investigation's Most Wanted list and is offering a reward of up to $2,500 for information leading to his arrest. Officials warn that he should be considered dangerous and that members of the public should not approach him, instead calling 911 or the TBI tip line at 1-800-TBI-FIND.

![Booking photo of Jacob Morley released by the Tennessee Department of Correction](https://www.wjhl.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/98/2026/10/JMorley-FaceSheet.jpg)

## The Original Crime

Morley was sentenced to life in prison for the 2022 killing of his co-worker Cody Birchfield, [the Associated Press reported](https://apnews.com/article/tennessee-man-escaped-prison-f59cae91a8075f5a464c416b4a8aca34). Birchfield was reported missing in February 2022, and his body was discovered about a month later, buried in a box beneath Morley's Carter County home in East Tennessee.

When deputies arrived at the home in 2022, Morley led police on a vehicle chase that ended in North Carolina. He was convicted of first-degree murder at trial on February 3, 2025, and sentenced that June in Carter County Criminal Court.

According to [WJHL](https://www.wjhl.com/news/crime/morley-receives-life-in-prison-after-being-found-guilty-of-missing-mans-murder/), the life term carries a chance of parole, and Morley also received consecutive sentences for aggravated burglary, evading arrest and vandalism, alongside concurrent terms for abuse of a corpse and tampering with evidence. The AP noted the additional convictions for aggravated burglary, tampering with evidence, vandalism and evading arrest.

## The Search and the Facility

The Northwest Correctional Complex sits in an isolated, rural area of flat land near a bend in the Mississippi River, roughly 90 miles (145 kilometers) north of Memphis and close to the Missouri border. It can house 1,776 men and also holds minors convicted as adults.

TDOC said that it, in coordination with law enforcement partners, had searched and cleared buildings and structures in the surrounding area, and that the Tennessee Highway Patrol had deployed aviation assets and state troopers to assist. Residents were asked to review home security and doorbell cameras, as well as trail and hunting cameras, for possible sightings.

Morley is described as 5-foot-11 (1.8 meters) and about 240 pounds (109 kilograms), with brown hair and green eyes.

## Scrutiny of a Troubled Prison

The escape has drawn fresh attention to the Northwest Correctional Complex, which has a documented history of violence. As the AP reported, a grand jury in July returned [dozens of indictments](https://apnews.com/article/tennessee-prison-murders-assaults-0cdbe4e7141299b5ab86c7d83cb023c9) tied to five inmate murders and assaults against staff and other inmates.

A [2023 audit](https://apnews.com/general-news-9aa80ef4dd7aa6a3a7e71c2d7dd2733b) of Tennessee prisons also found that the department was struggling to fill open positions and had a 61% vacancy rate for correctional officers in that fiscal year — a staffing shortfall that raises questions about how a life-sentenced inmate could breach a perimeter fence without immediate detection.

The breach was only discovered during an emergency count at the 11:30 p.m. check, leaving open how long Morley may have been out of custody and whether he had assistance or a planned destination. Officials have not addressed whether anyone helped him.

## Broader Trend

The escape comes more than a year after a [breakout at a New Orleans jail](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2025/jun/28/new-orleans-jailbreak-fugitive-captured) in June 2025, in which 10 detainees fled through a hole behind a toilet — all of whom were later recaptured. That episode and the Tennessee escape have renewed scrutiny of security and staffing at U.S. correctional facilities.

## What's Next

The manhunt remained ongoing Monday evening with no capture reported. As the search continues, attention is likely to turn to how Morley breached the fence, why he was not detected sooner, and whether staffing shortages at the facility played a role — questions that could echo well beyond Tiptonville.

Anyone with information on Morley's whereabouts should call 911 or the TBI at 1-800-TBI-FIND (1-800-824-3463).
