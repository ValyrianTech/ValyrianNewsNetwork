# Wang Zhijun Appointed Party Secretary of Hunan

China's central leadership has appointed Wang Zhijun as the new Party Secretary of Hunan Province, one of the most senior positions in a central Chinese province, according to [Xinhua News](https://www.news.cn/politics/20261024/9424d9c4d539439f9b5dd9e6f6ea5eb4/c.html). The announcement, made on 24 October 2026, simultaneously removed Shen Xiaoming, who had held the post since March 2023 and who stepped down "due to age reasons."

The decision was announced at a provincial leading-cadres meeting in Hunan by Hao Junhui, Deputy Head of the CPC Central Organization Department. It forms part of a broader, coordinated reshuffle of Hunan's top leadership that also reshaped the provincial government and deputy party secretary roles.

## A Coordinated Leadership Package

The change was not limited to the top post. Xinhua reported that the Central Committee approved that Mao Weiming — Hunan's governor since January 2021 — no longer serves as Hunan Deputy Party Secretary, Standing Committee member, or Committee member, while remaining governor of the provincial government. Separately, the Hunan Provincial Party Committee decided that Xie Weijiang becomes Party Secretary of the Hunan Provincial Government (省政府党组书记), the standard precursor to becoming provincial governor, according to a [Jiefang Daily report carried by Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-10-25/doc-iniwkwff3501659.shtml).

Hao framed the adjustment in standard official terms, saying it was made by the Central Committee "proceeding from the overall situation, based on work needs and the actual state of the Hunan provincial leadership team, after comprehensive consideration and careful study."

## Who Is Wang Zhijun?

Wang Zhijun, male and of Han ethnicity, was born in September 1965 and is a native of Hohhot in Inner Mongolia. He holds postgraduate qualifications and a Master's degree in Economics, and is a member of the 20th CPC Central Committee.

His career spans central economic policy, industrial administration, and regional party work. He served as Director-General of the fifth and first bureaus of the Central Financial and Economic Leading Group Office, before becoming Vice Minister of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology in January 2019. He later served as Deputy Party Secretary of Heilongjiang Province, and from June 2023 as Deputy Secretary-General of the State Council, handling the daily work of the State Council General Office at ministerial rank, as well as Deputy Party Secretary of its leading Party members' group.

His move from a senior central government administrative post to a provincial Party Secretary role is a familiar pattern in Chinese elite politics: experienced central officials being dispatched to lead major provinces. His combination of economic-policy, industrial, and regional party experience positions him for a prominent provincial leadership role.

## Shen Xiaoming's Departure

Shen Xiaoming, born in May 1963, was formerly Governor and then Party Secretary of Hainan Province before becoming Hunan Party Secretary in March 2023. He is also a member of the 20th CPC Central Committee. Xinhua reported that he stepped down from his Hunan posts "due to age reasons," the only rationale stated in accessible official reporting.

Xie Weijiang, the new Provincial Government Party Secretary, is notably younger — born in January 1973 and a native of Fugou in Henan. An engineer by training with a Master of Engineering degree, he previously served as Vice General Manager and Standing Committee member of Harbin Electric Corporation, Vice Governor of Hunan, and Secretary-General of the Hunan Provincial Party Committee. From December 2023 he was a Standing Committee member of the Hunan Provincial Party Committee and Party Secretary of Yueyang City, and from July 2025 Hunan Deputy Party Secretary while remaining Yueyang Party Secretary.

## Broader Reshuffle Cycle

The Hunan changes came amid a wider wave of provincial-level leadership rotations in 2026. In late September, leadership changes were announced across six provinces and regions — Tibet, Gansu, Qinghai, Anhui, Henan, and Fujian — in a pattern consistent with periodic, centrally coordinated personnel adjustments. Hunan's own top team saw multiple simultaneous changes, affecting the Party Secretary, the Deputy Party Secretary, and the Provincial Government Party Secretary.

## What to Watch

The elevation of Xie Weijiang — born in 1973 — to Provincial Government Party Secretary alongside Shen's age-based departure points to a generational transition within Hunan's leadership, with Mao Weiming remaining governor but yielding his deputy party secretary role. Observers will watch whether Xie formally assumes the governorship, as the Provincial Government Party Secretary title typically precedes, and how Wang Zhijun applies his economic and industrial policy background to Hunan, a province of major economic and agricultural significance anchored by the capital Changsha. The appointments are also a signal of how the Central Committee is positioning personnel ahead of the next phase of its leadership cycle.
