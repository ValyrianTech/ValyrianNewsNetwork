# Belgian Princess Elisabeth Turns 25, Palace Shares Portrait

Crown Princess Elisabeth of Belgium, first in line to the throne and the future first female monarch of the country, celebrates her 25th birthday on Sunday. To mark the occasion, the royal palace released a new black-and-white portrait of the heir to the throne, photographed by Bas Bogaerts, according to [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/10/25/kroonprinses-elisabeth-viert-25e-verjaardag/).

Born on 25 October 2001, Elisabeth is the eldest daughter of King Filip and Queen Mathilde and has held the title Duchess of Brabant since 21 July 2013, when her father acceded to the throne. The palace gave no details of how the birthday would be celebrated, keeping the occasion essentially private.

## A Deliberately Sober Portrait

The new portrait, described by [RTL Info](https://www.rtl.be/actu/belgique/famille-royale/la-princesse-elisabeth-fete-ses-25-ans-le-palais-royal-diffuse-un-portrait-en/2026-10-25/article/804461) as "sober" and conveying a certain maturity, shows the princess with a slight smile. The choice of a black-and-white image is consistent with how the palace has carefully managed her public image over the years.

Monarchy specialist Thomas de Bergeyck told RTL Info that the birthday "will remain essentially private." He said the official photos resemble "the way the palace has always managed Elisabeth: she is shown little, but when she is shown, everything is perfectly prepared."

## A Year of Transition

The milestone comes at a pivotal moment for the crown princess. In May 2026 she completed a master's degree in Public Policy at the Harvard Kennedy School in Boston, one of the world's most prestigious universities, following an earlier bachelor's degree in History and Politics at Lincoln College, Oxford. She also attended school in Wales.

Speaking to VRT NWS on the day of her graduation, Elisabeth signaled that she intends to take her time before assuming a full-time royal role. "My student days were very intense, I threw myself into them completely," she said. "I will now try to take some distance and lay a solid foundation for what lies ahead. Nothing rushed, everything in its time."

She added that she would like "a year or a little longer" to reflect on her next steps. In the meantime, RTL Info reports, experts expect her to return to the public scene around 2028, after a sabbatical year that Belgian media have linked to plans for an Atlantic sailing trip, a report the palace declined to comment on.

## Preparing for the Throne

As crown princess, Elisabeth is expected in the future to lead Belgian trade missions, a role currently carried out by Queen Mathilde, and to receive her own household and a state dotation when she takes up her active role.

Commentators have framed her education as deliberate preparation for the responsibilities of head of state. Journalist Amélie Schildt, author of a book on Elisabeth, described her to RTL Info as "a young woman who is very fulfilled, very balanced," adding that her studies at Harvard "transformed her. She owns her choices and her communication."

## Public Appetite for Change

Interest in the princess's coming role appears strong. According to RTL's "Grand Baromètre" survey, nearly one in two Belgians believes she should succeed her father within the next five years.

For now, the palace is keeping the focus on the quiet symbolism of the moment: an heir who has reached full adulthood, formally educated and publicly poised, but deliberately unhurried in stepping into the spotlight.

## What's Next

The birthday portrait maintains a near-annual tradition of releasing an official image on Elisabeth's birthday, documented in 2021, 2023, 2024 and 2025. What remains open is the timing of her formal return to public life. With her studies complete and a gap year underway, observers will be watching for the first signs of the trade missions, official engagements and institutional arrangements that will define her transition from heir to working royal.
