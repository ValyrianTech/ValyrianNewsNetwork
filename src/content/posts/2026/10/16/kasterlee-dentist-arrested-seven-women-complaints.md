# Kasterlee Dentist Arrested After 7 Women Report Touching

A 46-year-old dentist from Kasterlee, in Belgium's Antwerp province, has been arrested on suspicion of sexually transgressive behavior toward female patients. Seven women have filed complaints of touching that had no medical necessity, according to the Antwerp prosecutor's office, which confirmed the case first [reported by VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/10/16/tandarts-kasterlee-opgepakt-seksueel-grensoverschrijdend-gedrag/).

Police carried out searches on 15 October at both the dentist's practice and his private residence, and arrested him during those operations. He was interrogated and brought before an investigating judge, who ordered his release under strict conditions — including a professional ban that prevents him from practicing dentistry.

## How the Investigation Unfolded

The case began last summer, when a 71-year-old woman filed a complaint with the Politiezone Regio Turnhout. She alleged that the dentist fondled her groin during a treatment, VRT NWS reported.

In early September, a second report followed via the Politiezone Geel-Laakdal-Meerhout from a 28-year-old woman who also described transgressive behavior. Later that month, two more women, aged 32 and 33, came forward. This week, a 57-year-old woman went to police with a similar complaint.

Authorities also reopened an older dossier from several years ago, in which two women had reported alleged sexual offenses. That investigation had previously yielded insufficient evidence for prosecution. With the new complaints, the file was reopened and folded into the current judicial investigation — bringing the total to seven potential victims.

"It appears the dentist crossed boundaries during dental treatments and touched women in places that are not part of a treatment, thus without any medical necessity," said Kristof Aerts, spokesperson for the Antwerp prosecutor's office.

## A Specific Charge, Not Rape

The facts have been qualified as "aantasting van de seksuele integriteit door een persoon met een gezags- of vertrouwensrelatie" — a violation of sexual integrity by a person in a position of authority or trust. The prosecutor's office explicitly stated that the case is not classified as rape.

"We collected those complaints and requested an investigating judge," Aerts said. On the release conditions, he added: "The man was interrogated and brought before the investigating judge. He decided to release the dentist, but under strict conditions. One of those conditions is a professional ban."

The judicial investigation is ongoing, and authorities are examining whether additional victims exist. "Anyone who recognizes themselves in the described facts or had a similar experience during a treatment with the dentist concerned can report to the police," Aerts said. "Every report can be important for the investigation. Victims can count on support and guidance."

## A Profession Under Scrutiny

The case lands amid a cluster of high-profile allegations against dentists in Flanders. In September, a 72-year-old dentist from Wilrijk went on trial accused of assaulting and raping female patients over many years, with prosecutors demanding a five-year sentence. By then, [73 victims had been identified](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/09/16/proces-tandarts-wilrijk-keelfetisjisme/) in that case, which dated back to a first complaint in 1995.

The Wilrijk case centered on so-called "throat disinfections" — a medical procedure that does not exist — which prosecutors described as a pretext for abuse. The charges included possession of images of child sexual abuse found on the defendant's computer.

Vincent Koningsveld, chairman of the Verbond van Vlaamse Tandartsen (Flemish Dentists' Association), said the pattern of negative coverage damages trust in the profession. "First and foremost it is of course very bad for the victims. But let it be clear: these are exceptions. It is a shame that trust in the care provider is damaged again," he said.

"Such news also reflects on colleagues who mean well and act correctly. We are a care profession that many people are already anxious about or don't like going to. When stories like this are added, it doesn't help the image at all."

Experts note that [unusual sexual preferences](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/09/18/hoe-onstaat-een-fetisj/) are not inherently harmful. Sexologist Rik Van Lunsen told VRT that such preferences typically become problematic only when a person can no longer feel arousal otherwise, or when they cross the boundaries of others.

## How to Report

Authorities have urged anyone with relevant information to come forward. Reports can be made through the website of the Politiezone Regio Turnhout, through the prosecutor's office website, or by email at opsporingen@politieregioturnhout.be.

## What to Watch For

The dentist has not been publicly named, and the allegations against him have not been tested in court; he is presumed innocent unless proven guilty. As the investigation continues, the key questions are whether more complainants emerge and how the reopened older dossier factors into the eventual case.

The outcome will also be watched against the backdrop of the Wilrijk trial, which is due to deliver its verdict on 27 October — a ruling that could further shape how institutions and the public address misconduct allegations in the dental profession.
