# Massive Attack to headline Rock Werchter after 24 years

British trip-hop pioneers Massive Attack will headline the opening night of Rock Werchter in 2027, returning to the Belgian festival for the first time in nearly a quarter of a century, [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/10/28/massive-attack-staat-voor-het-eerst-in-24-jaar-weer-op-rock-werc/) reported on Wednesday. The band will top the bill on Thursday 1 July 2027, sharing the day with American hardcore outfit Turnstile.

Their last appearance on the Werchter meadow dates back to 27 June 2003. General ticket sales for the festival open on Friday 30 October at 10:00.

## Context

Formed in Bristol in 1988 by Robert Del Naja (3D), Grant Marshall (Daddy G), Adrian Thaws (Tricky) and Andrew Vowles (Mushroom), Massive Attack are widely credited as the archetypal trip-hop act and one of the most influential British groups of the past four decades. Only Del Naja and Marshall remain active members today.

The group's sound — slow beats, dark atmospheres, soulful vocals and electronic layers — produced landmark albums including *Blue Lines*, *Mezzanine* and *Heligoland*, and enduring songs such as "Unfinished Sympathy" and "Teardrop." Rock Werchter is one of Belgium's largest and most prestigious festivals, held annually at Festivalpark in Werchter since 1976 and organised by Live Nation Festivals NV.

## Key developments

In its announcement, the festival said Massive Attack remains as relevant as ever. "Massive Attack is still of today, their music remains defining. Hip-hop, soul, reggae, dub, post-punk and electronica go into the mix with social engagement and activism. A Massive Attack concert is an experience," the [festival](https://www.rockwerchter.be/nl/nieuws/massive-attack-maakt-zijn-return-op-rock-werchter) said.

The festival also cited a recent review from *De Morgen* describing a concert as "musically sublime, visually merciless." The return caps a busy autumn of lineup announcements for the 2027 edition — Muse, Angèle, Sam Fender and Turnstile have all been confirmed in recent weeks, with Tame Impala previously announced as the festival's first headliner.

The booking also signals something of a comeback for the band on Belgian soil. Massive Attack had been scheduled to play Gent Jazz in July 2025 but [cancelled](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2025/07/09/gent-jazz-massive-attack-soulwax/) when a band member fell ill, with Soulwax stepping in as a replacement. The group returned to European stages in the summer of 2026 with a selective run dubbed "xLive2026," including a rare hometown show in Bristol — their first European solo excursion in roughly seven years.

## Analysis

The announcement is a significant draw for Rock Werchter, whose staggered reveal strategy — one major name at a time through October — is designed to sustain media attention ahead of ticket sales. A band with a 24-year absence and a devoted following provides exactly the kind of headline moment festivals compete for.

Massive Attack's return also brings a distinctly political dimension. The band has been outspokenly engaged on climate and war, and in September 2025 [removed their catalogue](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2025/09/19/massive-attack-haalt-muziek-van-spotify/) from Spotify in protest at CEO Daniel Ek's investment in a military AI company, becoming the first major major-label act to do so. The festival describes that activism as part of the band's identity rather than a footnote.

## What's next

Rock Werchter 2027 runs from Thursday 1 July to Sunday 4 July at Festivalpark, with an opening party in The Hive campsite the evening before. More names are expected to be announced in the coming months, and fans will be watching how the opening night shapes up around its newly confirmed headliner. For Belgian audiences, the question now is less about whether Massive Attack still matters than how a 2027 crowd will receive a band whose long absence has only grown its legend.
