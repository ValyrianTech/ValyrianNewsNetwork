# Fire at Antwerp's Botanic Sanctuary Evacuates 100 Guests

Around 100 guests were evacuated from Antwerp's five-star Botanic Sanctuary hotel early on Wednesday morning after a fire broke out in the wellness area, with none reported injured. Among those led to safety was Russian chess grandmaster and former world champion Garry Kasparov, in the city for a simultaneous exhibition at the KMSKA museum.

According to [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/10/21/honderd-hotelgasten-geevacueerd-in-luxehotel-botanic-sanctuary-i/), the Antwerp fire brigade received the first alert at 4:24 am, reporting a blaze in the swimming pool section of the hotel on Leopoldstraat in central Antwerp.

## Guests Sheltered Across the Street

“There was a fire at the swimming pool section of the hotel,” said Marie De Clercq of the Antwerp fire service. “The hotel guests were received in the cafeteria of the ZAS Elisabeth hospital across the street.”

The fire was brought under control within about half an hour but burned “briefly yet very intensely,” De Clercq said. Footage showed around a dozen cracked windows and considerable damage inside, with the sauna blackened. Emergency crews carried out some demolition work because hotspots had developed in the insulation. All guests were allowed back to their rooms shortly before 6:00 am after measurements confirmed no smoke or carbon monoxide was present.

## ‘An Adventure,’ Says Kasparov

The evacuation unfolded calmly. A Dutch family from Nijmegen, woken by the alarm around 4:30 am, initially mistook it for a false alarm. “But when we came outside, we saw the flames shooting out of the spa,” they told VRT. “It was a shock, especially when we had to go back to the rooms, but everyone stayed calm.”

A Spanish guest described a well-organised response. “There was no panic at all. Everyone reacted calmly and it was all very well organised.” Kasparov, speaking to Karrewiet, summed up the night with characteristic understatement: “Het was een avontuur” — it was an adventure. He was still scheduled to face 30 opponents at the KMSKA that evening.

## Spa Out of Service

The Botanic Health Spa — a [1,000-square-metre facility across three floors](https://www.botanicantwerp.be/botanic-health-spa-overview/facilities/) featuring an 18-metre panoramic pool, saunas, steam rooms and ten treatment rooms — will remain closed while damage is assessed. The hotel's sales and marketing manager, Daisy Tucci, said the rest of the property was operating relatively normally. “Guests can simply go for breakfast in the breakfast room; the lobby and the other restaurants are also open as usual,” she said, adding that the exact damage in the spa section still needs to be measured.

## Cause Still Unknown

The cause of the fire has not yet been established. Regular visitors Florence Sophie and a friend surveyed the aftermath on Wednesday morning. “The sauna is blackened and all the windows are cracked. It is sad to see such a beautiful complex like this,” she said — though she noted the timing had limited the danger. “Fortunately no one was working out when it happened.”

The Botanic Sanctuary, housed in a former medieval hospital complex beside the Antwerp Botanical Garden, is a member of The Leading Hotels of the World and one of the city's most prestigious venues. The fire is a significant setback for its wellness offering, even as the broader hotel operation continues.

## What to Watch

Investigators will now determine the cause and full extent of the damage, with the pool and spa area facing an extended closure. For the roughly 100 guests whose night ended in a hospital cafeteria, the disruption appears to have been brief — a testament to an evacuation that officials and eyewitnesses alike described as swift and orderly.
