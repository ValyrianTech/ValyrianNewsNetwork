# Sombr to Headline Main Stage at Rock Werchter 2027

American singer-songwriter Sombr will perform on the Main Stage of Rock Werchter 2027, organizers announced on Tuesday, adding major international star power to Belgium's flagship summer festival. The 21-year-old New Yorker will take the festival's biggest stage on Sunday 4 July 2027, the closing day of the event's 52nd edition, where Australian act Tame Impala is already confirmed to close the night.

The announcement, made by the festival on [6 October 2026](https://www.rockwerchter.be/en/news/sombr-is-coming-to-rock-werchter-2027), positions Sombr alongside one of the most anticipated headliners of the European festival calendar and underscores his rapid ascent from a bedroom-recording project to a global pop phenomenon.

## A Rising Star Returns to Belgium

Sombr — the stage name of Shane Michael Boose — has built a particularly strong following in Belgium over the past year. According to [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/10/06/sombr-op-rock-werchter/), the young singer sold out the Ancienne Belgique in Brussels earlier this year and performed on the Main Stage at Pukkelpop 2026, one of the country's most important festivals.

His Belgian momentum extends into 2027: Sombr is scheduled to play the AFAS Dome in Antwerp on 16 May 2027 as part of his *You Are The Reason Tour* across Europe, with general ticket sales for that show having opened on 4 September, according to [Podiuminfo](https://www.podiuminfo.nl/news/59256/sombr-in-mei-2027-met-You-Are-The-Reason-Tour-naar-Amsterdam-en-Antwerpen/). Belgian music outlet [Dansende Beren](https://www.dansendeberen.be/2026/10/06/nieuw-op-rock-werchter-2027-sombr/) reported that the Antwerp date sold out "within seconds" — a sign of the demand the American now commands in the country.

"With '12 to 12', 'my body isn't ready', 'back to friends' and 'undressed', SOMBR has rapidly made a name for himself," Rock Werchter said in its official announcement. "The young New Yorker is rightly one of the most talked-about names in pop right now. He also makes a huge impression live. SOMBR will perform on our Main Stage on Sunday 4 July. Don't miss it!"

## From Bedroom to World Stage

Sombr's rise has been remarkably swift. Born in New York in 2005, he trained in classical voice at the city's LaGuardia High School while recording indie-rock songs in his bedroom during the COVID-19 pandemic, according to [Festivalinfo](https://www.festivalinfo.nl/news/59446/Rock-Werchter-2027-strikt-ook-SOMBR-voor-Main-Stage-op-zondag/).

His global breakthrough came in early 2025 with the single "back to friends," which earned him an MTV Video Music Award for Best Alternative and became the sixth most-streamed song of 2025 worldwide. His debut album, *I Barely Know Her* (2025), produced by Tony Berg, featured hits including "12 to 12" and "undressed." He followed up in 2026 with singles such as "Homewrecker" and "My Body Isn't Ready," and he was nominated for a Grammy for Best New Artist, Podiuminfo reported.

As [RTBF Actus](https://www.rtbf.be/article/sombr-rejoint-les-tetes-d-affiche-de-rock-werchter-2027-11795376) noted, Sombr is "one of the most vibrant artists of his young generation," equally at home in the raw power of a large rock stage and the quiet emotion of an intimate acoustic session. At Coachella in April 2026, he famously brought Billy Idol on stage during his set.

## A Sunday Slot Alongside Tame Impala

Sombr joins an already star-studded Sunday lineup. Tame Impala — the Australian project of Kevin Parker — was announced as the festival's first headliner on 24 August 2026 and will close Rock Werchter 2027 on the same day. According to Dansende Beren, Sombr will perform just before Parker takes the stage — a neat echo of the fact that the American also appeared on the Pukkelpop Main Stage on the day the festival first teased Tame Impala for Werchter.

Rock Werchter 2027 runs from Thursday 1 to Sunday 4 July at the Festivalpark in Werchter — the festival's 52nd edition. Organizers began rolling out names unusually early this year, a shift from the festival's traditional rhythm that signals heightened ambition for the lineup.

## Tickets and What's Next

General ticket sales for Rock Werchter 2027 open on Friday 30 October at 10:00, with an exclusive presale a day earlier on Thursday 29 October. Exact ticket prices had not been released as of the announcement.

More acts are expected to be revealed in the coming months. Organizers said additional names would follow, and the festival's wishlist-driven lineup rollout — signaled by early teasers during Pukkelpop — suggests further high-profile bookings are in store.

For Sombr, the Werchter booking caps a breakout year and reinforces his growing European footprint. Having conquered the AB, Pukkelpop and now the main stage of Belgium's premier festival, he arrives at Werchter with a rare combination of critical acclaim and mainstream streaming success. For Belgian music fans, the message from organizers is simple: with Tame Impala closing and Sombr before him, the final night of Rock Werchter 2027 is shaping up to be one of the summer's must-see events.
