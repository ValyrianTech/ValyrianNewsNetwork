# China's 5th Plenum Adopts Landmark Party-Building Decision

The 20th Central Committee of the Communist Party of China (CPC) held its Fifth Plenum in Beijing from October 26 to 29, 2026, adopting a sweeping decision on Party governance and setting the date for the next national congress, according to the [official communique](https://www.news.cn/politics/leaders/20261029/3dc769be3bff40aeafcbf957eb4e47bb/c.html) released by Xinhua. The session, presided over by the Politburo and addressed by General Secretary Xi Jinping, was devoted to the theme of persistently advancing full and rigorous Party governance — a signature of Xi-era internal discipline.

The plenum reviewed and adopted the *Decision of the CPC Central Committee on Several Major Issues Concerning Persistently Advancing Full and Rigorous Party Governance*, which state media described as a "programmatic document" for advancing the great project of Party building in the new era. Xi delivered a work report on behalf of the Politburo and an explanation of the decision draft.

## A Session Focused on Internal Discipline

The choice to dedicate a Fifth Plenum to Party building rather than a five-year economic plan is itself notable. The 19th Central Committee's Fifth Plenum in 2020 adopted proposals for the 14th Five-Year Plan; this session turned inward to governance and discipline.

According to the communique, 162 full members and 117 alternate members attended — roughly 279 of those elected at the 20th Party Congress in 2022. In a widely circulated side-account of the proceedings, Xinhua framed the meeting's purpose around a single declaration: "The key to running China's affairs well lies in the Party, and in full and rigorous Party governance."

The decision lays out seven guiding principles, including serving the Party's mission, taking political building as the lead, maintaining comprehensive strictness, adhering to a problem-oriented approach, upholding integrity and innovation, leading by example, and governing the Party by regulations. It also sets out seven strategic tasks: strengthening Party spirit, implementing democratic centralism, building a high-quality cadre contingent, building a rigorous organizational system, correcting conduct and tightening discipline, advancing the integrated anti-corruption approach, and rule-based Party governance.

## Xi Frames the Case for Persistence

In the side-account dispatch published by Xinhua, Xi is quoted explaining why the campaign against corruption and indiscipline must continue without pause. He addressed a persistent question directly: "Has full and rigorous Party governance hindered economic and social development, or has it safeguarded and promoted it?"

According to the [Xinhua side-notes dispatch](https://www.news.cn/20261031/47b03ffc1ed24f6c8eed01d93e258783/c.html), Xi answered that any effect on the economy has been "positive rather than negative," arguing that discipline "supports and purges obstacles, removes blockages, and corrects deviations" while protecting fair market competition.

He also offered a candid diagnosis of cadres' failures: "When cadres go wrong today, it is mainly a problem of virtue — of weak Party spirit." The decision devotes its first strategic task to strengthening Party spirit, describing it as "the foundation for Party members and cadres to establish themselves, build their careers, and cultivate their character."

The dispatch invoked the historical "two answers" to escaping the cycle of dynastic rise and fall — "letting the people supervise the government" and "self-revolution" — casting them as the CPC's distinctive contribution to the history of Marxist party building.

## Record Expulsions Confirmed

Beneath the consensus framing, the communique confirmed a striking wave of disciplinary action. The plenum ratified the expulsion from the Party of 33 senior figures for serious disciplinary and legal violations, including former senior officials and multiple People's Liberation Army generals.

Those expelled include high-profile figures such as Ma Xingrui, Zhang Youxia, Liu Zhenli, Lan Tianli, Liu Jianchao, Wang Lixia, Jin Zhuanglong, Lei Fanpei, Yi Huiman, Wang Xiangxi, and Sun Shaocheng. Li Yunze received the lesser sanction of removal from Party posts.

The scale of the expulsions — the largest confirmed at a single plenum since reform and opening up in 1978, according to analyses — underscores the continuing intensity of the anti-corruption drive and the scale of elite turnover at the highest levels. The inclusion of military figures signals that the campaign reaches deep into the armed forces.

## Leadership Adjustments and the Road to 2027

The plenum co-opted Zhang Shengmin as a member of the Politburo and promoted ten alternate members to full Central Committee membership: Lu Dongliang, Bao Gang, Xing Shanping, Qu Yingpu, Lü Jun, Zhu Tianshu, Zhu Guoxian, Liu Jun, Liu Jie, and Liu Qiang.

Crucially, the plenum decided that the 21st CPC National Congress will be held in the second half of 2027 in Beijing. The communique described the congress as "a major event in the political life of the Party and the state," coming at a key moment in the drive to basically achieve socialist modernization. Setting the political calendar positions the leadership cycle for the years ahead.

## Analysis: A Dual Message

The plenum projected a dual message. Externally, the official narrative emphasized unity, resolve, and achievement — the atmosphere-building genre of the side-notes dispatch is designed to convey consensus rather than hard news. Internally, the record expulsions and the lowest plenum attendance rate since 1978, noted in external analyses, suggest that the meeting's political significance runs deeper than the celebratory framing conveys.

The formalization of "Xi Jinping Thought on Party Building" as an ideological framework, described in the communique as an important component of Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, elevates the governance agenda to foundational doctrine. Xi's emphasis on "problem orientation" and his admission that cadres fail on "virtue" reflect an internal acknowledgment of persistent governance challenges.

## What's Next

The decision's implementation will be a major political task for the Party in the period ahead, with the communique calling deep study of its spirit "a major political task for the whole Party." With the 21st Party Congress set for late 2027, the coming year will be shaped by how thoroughly the governance agenda is institutionalized — and by whether the anti-corruption drive continues at its current intensity. For observers, the key indicators will be further elite turnover, the consolidation of the new ideological framework, and the political preparations for the 2027 congress.
