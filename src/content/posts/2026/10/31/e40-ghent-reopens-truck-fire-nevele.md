# E40 to Ghent Reopens After Truck Fire Near Nevele

The E40 motorway from the Belgian coast toward Ghent fully reopened at approximately 11:20 AM on Saturday, 31 October, after a burning truck blocked the route near Nevele for several hours, according to [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/10/31/e40-in-nevele-richting-gent-versperd-door-brandende-vrachtwagen/). Throughout the morning, all traffic heading toward Ghent was forced off the motorway and diverted along a detour to the Drongen on-ramp.

## What Happened

The incident began overnight at around 3:15 AM, just past Nevele. The truck driver lost control of the vehicle, entered the central median, and the truck caught fire. The driver was injured but his condition was not life-threatening; he was taken to hospital, VRT NWS reported.

The truck burned out completely. Firefighting operations were completed by Saturday morning, after which recovery crews had to tow both the tractor unit and the trailer and deploy a sweeper machine to clean the road surface before reopening.

## Disruption and Detours

The stretch of the E40 between Drongen and Nevele is a heavily used commuter corridor into Ghent, and Saturday's closure caused significant disruption. VRT NWS noted that some motorists stuck in the morning traffic resorted to driving the wrong way in order to reach the Nevele exits.

With the motorway fully reopened since 11:20 AM, residual delays were expected but were predicted to clear quickly.

## Context

Truck fires periodically cause major disruption on Belgian motorways, because burning cargo and the intense heat needed to extinguish it can damage the road surface, requiring cleaning and sometimes resurfacing before traffic can safely return. A separate truck fire occurred on the same E40 stretch between Drongen and Nevele in March 2021, highlighting that this is a recurring type of incident on the corridor. Nevele, a municipality in East Flanders, was merged into the city of Deinze in 2019.

## What to Watch

Authorities have not indicated any structural traffic measures for the corridor following Saturday's fire. Motorists travelling toward Ghent from the coast should remain attentive to any residual delays during the morning, which emergency services expect to dissipate as the day progresses.
