# Swiss President Parmelin Arrives in Beijing on Working Visit

Swiss Federal President Guy Parmelin arrived in Beijing on Friday, opening a three-day working visit to China, according to [Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/world/20261030/c6c6d35dee6146d2a62023cd1d120d4c/c.html). The visit runs through November 1.

The trip coincides with the 10th anniversary of the China-Switzerland Innovative Strategic Partnership, the Chinese state news agency reported. Parmelin, who is also head of Switzerland's Federal Department of Economic Affairs, Education and Research, is expected to meet Chinese leaders during his stay.

## A Relationship Framed Around Cooperation

In its coverage of the visit, Xinhua noted that China's Foreign Ministry has said Beijing is willing to work with Switzerland to use "a steady China-Switzerland relationship" to create more well-being for both peoples, provide favorable conditions for enterprises on both sides, and inject "more certainty" into world economic development.

That language places the visit within Beijing's broader emphasis on predictable, rules-based engagement with European partners at a time of global economic uncertainty. For Switzerland, the trip is a continuation of a relationship long centered on trade and market access.

## What to Watch

The immediate focus is Parmelin's scheduled meetings with Chinese government leaders over the coming days, and what concrete agreements, if any, emerge from them. With the visit framed by both sides around the anniversary of the strategic partnership, the tone of the engagements will offer a signal of how the relationship is being positioned for the years ahead.

For now, both capitals are presenting the visit as evidence that steady cooperation between Europe and China remains a shared priority.
