# China U17 Women Beat France on Penalties, Reach Quarterfinal

China PR reached the quarterfinals of the FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup Morocco 2026 for the first time in its history on Thursday, defeating France 4-3 on penalties after a 0-0 draw in the Round of 16 in Rabat. Goalkeeper Niu Ziqi was the hero, saving two spot-kicks and earning Player of the Match honours as the Asian side advanced to a last-eight meeting with Brazil.

The match, played at the Mohammed VI Football Complex on 29 October, was the first goalless draw of the tournament — a tense, attritional contest decided only by the composure of two young goalkeepers and the nerve of China's penalty takers.

## A Historic Step Forward

The victory marks a clear advance for China's youth programme. According to [Xinhua News](https://www.news.cn/sports/20261030/7a170b9d5fea4ef78f718e59f974e912/c.html), the team's previous best at this tournament was a Round of 16 exit in 2025, when it lost 0-3 to Brazil. By eliminating France, China has now pushed its all-time best finish to the quarterfinals.

The result did not come easily. France dominated the first half and created the better chances, with their clearest opening arriving in the ninth minute when Maelys Ane forced a brilliant save from Niu. China weathered the pressure and reached the interval level.

At the start of the second half, head coach Ma Xiaoxu introduced Yang Lan and Yang Yuhan to spark the attack. China's forward play improved, with Cai Xinlin, Liu Yuke and Yang Yuhan all testing the French defence, but neither side could break the deadlock and the tie went straight to penalties.

## The Shootout

China took the first kick and never trailed in the shootout. Liu Yuxi converted the opener, and Niu immediately saved France's first attempt. Liu Yuke scored China's second, but Liu Yuchen missed the third, allowing France to level at 2-2. Yang Lan and Cheng Wandi then converted in succession, before Niu saved France's fifth kick — taken by Lisa Mabilon — to seal the win.

The [FIFA](https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/womens/u17womensworldcup/morocco-2026/articles/rabat-matchday-11-round-up) match report confirmed that Niu's saves came from Lea Motyka and Mabilon, and that the goalkeeper was named Player of the Match for a performance her coach described as the product of steady improvement.

> "Today the game was tough and we were positive, but we worked hard and we won the game. I think our goalkeeper has been in her best condition during the past few games. And she has been improving and becoming more and more confident. And so, everyone from our team, we are so united and we give our appreciation to her."
>
> — Ma Xiaoxu, China PR head coach

France coach Franck Plenecassagne was gracious in defeat, saying his side had no regrets and that France deserved to lead at half-time before fading physically in the second period.

> "It's a tough outcome, but I'd say there are no regrets since we gave it everything we had. We definitely deserved to go into half-time with the lead, and then we played a second half, let's call it a courageous one, because we faded physically, as usual."
>
> — Franck Plenecassagne, France head coach

## Built on a Strong Group Stage

China arrived at the knockout stage as Group E winners with a perfect record. The team beat Kenya 3-0, Samoa 7-0 and the United States 3-2, scoring 13 goals and conceding just two across three matches. In the head-of-group decider against the USA, Liu Yuxi scored twice and Zhao Jiayi added a third to secure top spot.

That attacking fluency was largely absent against a disciplined France side, but the defensive resilience China showed — and the calm of its goalkeeper — proved decisive when the match went to spot-kicks.

The coaching appointment of Ma Xiaoxu, a former star of the Chinese women's national team, has been framed as part of a broader push to rejuvenate the youth setup. The Chinese Football Association named her to lead the U17 side in January 2026, with the World Cup and the AFC U17 Women's Asian Cup in mind. The quarterfinal berth is an early vindication of that investment.

## What's Next

China faces Brazil in the quarterfinals on 2 November (Beijing time), a rematch of the 2025 Round of 16 defeat that ended its previous campaign. Brazil advanced by beating Poland 8-7 on penalties after a 3-3 draw, and will present a formidable attacking challenge.

For a young squad still writing its history, the pressure is now different. Having already surpassed every previous Chinese U17 side, Ma's players enter the Brazil match with both momentum and a score to settle — and with a goalkeeper who has shown she can deliver when everything is on the line.
