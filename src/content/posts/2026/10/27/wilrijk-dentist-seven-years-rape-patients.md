# Wilrijk dentist gets 7 years for raping patients

A 72-year-old former dentist from Wilrijk, in the Antwerp district, has been sentenced to an effective seven-year prison term for the rape and sexual assault of his female patients over a period of roughly 25 years. The Antwerp court delivered its verdict on Tuesday morning, [VRT NWS reported](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/10/27/vonnis-tandarts-wilrijk/), convicting the man of rape, assault of sexual integrity, forgery, fraud, the illegal practice of medicine and unlawfully presenting himself as a doctor.

The sentence exceeded what prosecutors had requested, a notable decision that the court justified on the grounds of the abuse's duration, the number of victims and its impact on both the individuals and society.

## The fabricated procedure at the heart of the case

According to the court, the dentist invented a non-existent medical treatment, which he called "keelontsmettingen" (throat cleanings or throat disinfections), to penetrate patients' throats with his fingers. He became aroused during these procedures, masturbated and filmed the treatments, positioning himself behind some patients.

"He used his position for 25 years to indulge his sexual desires," the judge said. "He invented medical procedures and scheduled appointments at moments when no other people were present. Statements show that his victims often feel humiliated and abused. At the same time, there was a lack of empathy and remorse during the investigation."

The court stressed that rape encompasses any form of sexual penetration, regardless of its nature or the instrument used, when the victim has not consented. "Also a penetration of the throat without any dental purpose and with sexual intent falls under that description," the judge said.

The man was also ordered to pay compensation to 27 civil parties and to RIZIV, Belgium's national health insurance institute, which joined the case as a civil party to recover amounts wrongly billed for the fictitious treatments. He was acquitted of three counts of rape and of possession of images of sexual abuse of minors.

## Twenty-five years of abuse that went unnoticed

The case began to unravel at the end of 2024, when a patient complained that she had felt something strange after a treatment. Testimonies then poured in, and an investigation led to his arrest in April 2025, as [VRT NWS detailed](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2025/04/03/tandarts-71-uit-wilrijk-gearresteerd-voor-zedenfeiten-sprake/) at the time. Eventually 73 victims came forward. The earliest complaint dates back to 1995.

Police seized extensive video material from his dental practice and his home, showing sexually tinged acts during dental treatments. Images of child sexual abuse were also found on his computer.

During the trial in September, several victims spoke about the abuse, describing the throat cleanings as torture. Some testified that the dentist masturbated while his fingers were in their throats, and some patients were treated under full anaesthesia, leaving them uncertain about what happened while they were unconscious. "My body was used without my consent," one victim said. "My peace and my trust in doctors is gone."

The defendant admitted the facts during the trial and is following intensive therapy. The Antwerp lawyers Walter Damen and Cato Van Hoof represented roughly 20 victims and civil parties in the case.

## A sentence above the prosecution's request

The public prosecutor had asked for five years' imprisonment, half of it suspended, effectively about two and a half years to serve. The court deemed that disproportionate.

"That does not stand in proportion to the long duration, the large number of victims and the traumatic consequences for the victims and for society," the judge said.

The verdict raises the question of whether the defence will appeal, given that the sentence exceeds what the prosecution sought. As of Tuesday afternoon, no appeal had been reported.

## How a fetish can turn into abuse

The case has also prompted public discussion about how unusual sexual preferences develop. In the wake of the trial, sexologist Rik Van Lunsen explained on Radio 1, in a piece published by [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/09/18/hoe-onstaat-een-fetisj/), that a fetish can arise when sexual arousal in childhood becomes linked to a particular experience or emotion, and that this is not always rooted in trauma.

"Most people have unusual sexual preferences, but most of those people manage to embed them in a normal life without ever crossing the boundaries of others," Van Lunsen said. "That is clearly what this gentleman did."

## What comes next

The verdict closes one of Belgium's most prominent cases of sexual abuse by a trusted professional, but the questions it leaves open may prove more enduring. Victims have asked how the abuse could continue for so long and how the dentist remained in practice for years despite the sheer number of people who came after them.

Those questions now point to the systems meant to protect patients: professional oversight, health regulators and the reporting channels open to people who suspect they have been mistreated in a medical setting. For the 73 people who came forward, the sentence is a measure of accountability. Whether it is matched by reform is a separate reckoning still to come.
