# Drunk driver kills German woman (69) on E34 in Kallo

A 69-year-old German woman was killed in a high-speed collision on the E34 motorway at Kallo early on Saturday morning after her car was struck from behind by a driver who had tested positive for alcohol, according to [VRT NWS](https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2026/10/24/dodelijk-ongeval-e34-kallo-onder-invloed/). The crash occurred around 02:00 in the direction of Antwerp, in the East Flanders municipality of Beveren-Kruibeke-Zwijndrecht, and closed the motorway for several hours.

## What happened

The victim was travelling alone in a BMW when she changed lanes to make room for a truck joining the E34 from the R2 ring road. According to the first findings reported by [HLN](https://www.hln.be/beveren-kruibeke-zwijndrecht/duitse-vrouw-69-sterft-bij-crash-op-e34-in-kallo-bestuurder-29-van-porsche-wordt-voorgeleid-bij-onderzoeksrechter~af46a3e4/), the driver of an approaching Porsche was travelling at a far too high speed and could no longer brake in time, hitting the BMW with considerable force. Emergency services were called at around 02:20.

The German woman died at the scene. The Porsche driver, a 29-year-old man reportedly from the Beveren-Kruibeke-Zwijndrecht area, was unhurt.

## Suspect arrested

The Porsche driver provided a positive breath test and was arrested. VRT NWS reports that he is to appear before an investigating judge within 48 hours, who will decide on his pre-trial detention. A traffic accident expert has been appointed to establish the precise circumstances of the collision.

Under Belgian law, driving with a blood alcohol content above 0.5 g/L is an offence, and a positive breath test can trigger arrest and referral to an investigating judge. Serious cases can lead to prosecution for involuntary homicide with aggravating circumstances for intoxication.

## Road closed for hours

The E34 towards Antwerp was closed for several hours because of the crash and only reopened to traffic at around 06:30.

VRT NWS issued a correction on Saturday afternoon, noting that an earlier version of its report had stated the victim was 50 years old. The woman who died was 69, the broadcaster confirmed.

## What to watch

The investigating judge's decision on whether to hold the suspect in pre-trial detention is expected within two days of his arrest. The traffic expert's reconstruction of the crash is also likely to shape any eventual prosecution, as authorities work to establish the exact speed and sequence of events on one of the region's busiest motorway corridors.
