# China Unveils Top-Level Plan for New Productive Forces

China's Central Committee of the Communist Party and the State Council have jointly issued a sweeping top-level policy framework for developing "new quality productive forces," laying out a roadmap across five innovation areas and 19 key measures intended to steer economic development through the 15th Five-Year Plan period from 2026 to 2030. The document, titled the "Opinions on Developing New Quality Productive Forces," was issued on 9 October and analyzed in state media on 12 October.

The full text, published by the [State Council](https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202610/content_7082819.htm), organizes its measures around technological innovation, industrial innovation, development-model innovation, institutional reform, and talent work-mechanism innovation. It frames "new quality productive forces" as the internal requirement and key focus of high-quality development, characterized by high technology, high efficiency, and high quality.

## Context

The concept of "new quality productive forces" has evolved rapidly from theoretical articulation under Xi Jinping's leadership into formal national policy. It describes productivity driven by technological revolution, innovative allocation of production factors, and deep industrial transformation.

The concept was emphasized in 2024, written into government work reports, and embedded in the reform agenda of the Third Plenum of the 20th CPC Central Committee. The October 2026 Opinions mark its codification into a comprehensive national roadmap. According to the [Economic Information Daily](https://www.news.cn/fortune/20261012/1652b68b312c48279c8b3d0f48359037/c.html), the policy's industrial space and policy dividends are expected to unfold throughout the 15th Five-Year Plan.

## Key Developments

### Four guiding principles

The document establishes four guiding principles: innovation-led development, reform as the key, adaptation to local conditions, and "establishing the new before abolishing the old." A senior official of the National Development and Reform Commission (NDRC) said these principles are a corrective for problems seen in practice, including blind investment detached from reality, rushing into emerging industries, neglecting traditional-sector upgrades, and prioritizing development over reform.

### Science and technology first

"Advancing scientific and technological innovation" heads the list of five tasks, with five measures attached. These include strengthening original and disruptive innovation, breaking through key core technologies, coordinating national strategic science and technology forces, reinforcing enterprises' role as innovation actors, and accelerating the conversion of scientific results into real productive forces.

The Opinions propose an enterprise R&D reserve fund system and a higher additional tax deduction ratio for R&D expenses. They allow qualifying enterprise R&D projects aligned with national strategic needs to be elevated to national projects, and they support capable private enterprises in leading major national technological breakthroughs.

China's enterprise R&D spending has stayed above 75% of total society-wide R&D expenditure for consecutive years. Yan Yang, vice president of the Beijing IoT Intelligent Technology Application Association, said the real test of the policy lies in corporate performance rather than official announcements: "The testing ground for new quality productive forces is not in the spotlight of a press conference, but in real corporate financial reports."

### A graded industrial layout

The Opinions arrange industrial policy in a deliberate gradient: upgrading traditional industries, cultivating emerging industries, and planning future industries.

Notably, "upgrading traditional industries" is placed first. The document names mining, metallurgy, chemicals, light industry, textiles, machinery, shipbuilding, and construction as targets for upgrading with digital, intelligent, and green technologies. This ordering sends a clear signal that developing new quality productive forces does not mean focusing only on new sectors.

Wang Shengxiao, a researcher at the Party School of the CPC Central Committee, said new quality productive forces are a "qualitative state" rather than a "business format." "Every industry and field can develop new quality productive forces," he said. "Traditional industries, empowered by technology and upgraded, can also incubate new industries and form new quality productive forces."

For emerging industries, the document targets next-generation information technology, new energy, new materials, intelligent connected new energy vehicles, robotics, biomedicine, high-end equipment, and aerospace. For future industries, it names quantum technology, biomanufacturing, hydrogen and nuclear fusion energy, brain-computer interfaces, embodied intelligence, and 6G.

### The "AI+" action

The Opinions dedicate a standalone measure to fully implementing the "AI+" action, treating artificial intelligence as a cross-cutting enabler. It calls for advancing AI's transformation of traditional industries, accelerating applications for intelligent connected vehicles, AI phones and computers, and humanoid robots, and strengthening the supply of computing power, algorithms, and data, while ensuring AI remains "safe, reliable, and controllable."

An NDRC official said the emphasis aims to promote deep integration of AI with the real economy and to apply intelligent innovation results to specific industries and industrial chains. A [Xinhua](https://www.news.cn/tech/20261010/c2d4afed526c4188ab07c4f0cbd7e045/c.html) analysis described the framework as the arrival of top-level design for developing new quality productive forces.

### Finance, talent, and red lines

On financing, the document encourages banks to develop loan products suited to new quality productive forces, supports long-term capital that "invests early, small, long-term, in hard tech," and calls for strengthening "patient capital."

On talent, it calls for decoupling researcher assessment and incentives from project reporting, paper publishing, and awards. It also promotes a "new eight-grade worker" vocational skill-grade system and smoother upward development channels for industrial workers.

Crucially, the framework sets explicit red lines: no decoupling from the real economy, no seeking grandeur, no bubbles, no industrial hollowing-out or industrial-chain breaks, and no blind pursuit of novelty. It warns that misuse of policy under the guise of developing new quality productive forces, and blind investment causing major losses, will be seriously pursued.

## Analysis

The Opinions represent the formalization of a concept that has moved quickly from political vocabulary to operational policy. The deliberate placement of traditional-industry upgrading first, combined with explicit warnings against bubbles, hollowing-out, and "involution-style" competition, signals official concern about past over-enthusiasm in emerging sectors.

The document's architecture also reveals a broader strategy: rather than treating AI, green transition, and industrial upgrading as separate tracks, it positions them as an integrated system, with AI as a horizontal enabler across traditional, emerging, and future industries. The central and local dissemination of the full text across government websites suggests a coordinated push to align implementation nationwide.

## What's Next

The NDRC and the Central Financial and Economic Affairs Office are tasked with coordinating theoretical research, strategic planning, cross-regional coordination, and monitoring and evaluation. The policy's success will hinge on how local governments translate the national framework into action without falling into the very pitfalls the document warns against.

Observers will watch whether the promised financing mechanisms, R&D incentives, and talent reforms materialize during the 15th Five-Year Plan period, and whether the emphasis on local adaptation prevents the kind of uniform, bubble-prone investment the framework explicitly seeks to avoid.
